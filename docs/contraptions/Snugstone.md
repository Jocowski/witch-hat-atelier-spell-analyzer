# Snugstone

The Snugstone is one of the creations of Olruggio.

The snugstone is made from some kind of mineral, cut into flat slices, with a glyph drawn on it's surface. This glyph allows it to radiate a faint, pleasant warmth, without the need of an open flame.

The snugstone glyph contains flipped column keystones, which are likely the reason why it radiates heat outwards. Interestingly, a dispersion keystone would probably elicit the same effect, but they are not used.

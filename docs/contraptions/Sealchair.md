# Sealchair

Sealchairs are a type of contraption and vehicle that works like a wheelchair but with legs.

Sealchairs are wooden chairs that possess four carved legs, each one ending in a shape that resembles a single toed hoof, like that of a horse, with the sealchair glyph drawn on the underside. It is functionally identical to a wheelchair, allowing someone who is unable to walk to move of their own accord.

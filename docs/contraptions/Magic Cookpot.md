# Magic Cookpot

Magic Cookpots are a type of contraption used in cooking, utilizing repitition seals to keep the food contained within as fresh as the day it was made.

A magic cookpot is a pot with repetition seal is drawn on the inside of the lid. This magic acts on anything placed inside the pot (or anything one might point the lid towards), effectively rewinding the state of its target. Food will not spoil so long as the seal is maintained.

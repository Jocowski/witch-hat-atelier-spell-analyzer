# Raincleaver

The Raincleaver is a magical sword used by Qifrey during the events of the River Rescue Arc.

It has the power to cut any liquid that the blade comes into contact with. The blade has many tiny, interconnected glyphs (possibly rainflinger glyphs) carved into it to enchant the item.

When many small glyphs are connected together, their strength increases, to the point that many interconnected small glyphs are stronger than a single large glyph, even though they take up the same amount of space. That is why the raincleaver is so strong.

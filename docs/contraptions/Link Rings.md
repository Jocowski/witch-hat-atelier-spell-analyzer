# Link Rings

Link Rings are a type of contraption used by Olruggio.

They are fashioned as a pair of two rings. Each ring is adorned with half of a glyph. In the case of the ones Olruggio gave to Agott, they were rainflinger glyphs. The glyph activates when both rings are brought together. The small size of the rings limits their power and range.

The link rings’ rainflinger glyph is of the drying variant of the spell, which is both aesthetically and functionally similar, but not identical to, a normal, water repelling dispel water water glyph, with an air sigil instead of water, and two surrounding fire sigil variants instead of normal fire sigils.

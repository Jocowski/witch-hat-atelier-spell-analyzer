# Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade

The mirror cloak of Borrowshade is a cloak that uses glyphs too creates an illusion that "mirrors" the appearance of the nearby fauna, disguising the wearer.

The mirror mantle uses mirror and gathering shadow spells to create an illusion that allows the wearer of the cloak to disguise themselves as any nearby animal life.

One of the cloaks was later modified by Euini, who removed the mirror glyph, allowing him to blend into the shadows.

# Vapor Bubble

The Vapor Bubble is a type of magical contraption that collects moisture from the air in order to be used by witches.

It's a simple item that consists of two trays with a spell written into the lower tray. When the two trays are separated from each other, the moisture in the air is gathered into a large bubble of water. By tipping the lower tray, water can be poured from the vapor bubble.

It is normally used to wash things or to drink in case of a need.

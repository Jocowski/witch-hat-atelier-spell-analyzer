# Glowstone Path

The Glowstone Path is a magic contraption that will glow when pressed on, normally by walking on it.

It consists of 2 pieces of stone, with a floatglow lamp (known as dancing lights in the scan) glyph drawn on it. The top stone is levitated by float keystones, and when it is stepped on, the weight of the person stepping on the tile forces the 2 halves of the incomplete glyphs together, completing the glyph and causing the stone to glow. The color that the stone glows can be changed to anything, and they can even be made to glow only when a specific person steps on them.

# Spike

Spike is a spell of unknown type.

## Description
Spike generates several large spikes directly from the glyph that are flung out at lethal speeds, and is a spell used for offensive purposes. The construction of the spell is entirely unknown.

## Usage
The spell was seen used by Sasaran during his fight with Qifrey within the Serpentback Cave. When Sasaran fired the spell at Qifrey, Qifrey managed to block it with a wall of earth.

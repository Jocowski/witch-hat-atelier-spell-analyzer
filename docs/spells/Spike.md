# Spike

> A spell of unknown composition that generates several large spikes directly from the glyph and flings them outward at lethal speed — a combat offensive spell used by Sasaran.

## Overview
Spike is an offensive combat spell used by Sasaran during his confrontation with Qifrey inside Serpentback Cave (ch. 27). The spell generates "several large spikes directly from the glyph that are flung out at lethal speeds." When Sasaran fired it at Qifrey, Qifrey blocked it with a wall of earth. The wiki classifies it as "Unknown type" and states "the construction of the spell is entirely unknown." No signs, sigils, or composition details are available from any source.

## In plain terms (sum up)
A combat spell that shoots large spikes from the seal at lethal speed. Think of it as the magic system's equivalent of a ballistic projectile attack. No information is available about how it is drawn. Qifrey had to generate a full earth wall to block it, suggesting the spikes carry significant physical force and material substance.

## Deduced effect
The spell produces multiple large solid spikes from the glyph's location and launches them at high velocity outward. Based on the effect (solid spikes with physical impact sufficient to require an earth wall as a shield), the following **ranked hypotheses** are offered:

**Hypothesis 1 (most likely): Earth sigil + Bolt + Column.**
The `earth` sigil (manipulates stone/soil/wood) paired with **Bolt** (which "makes fragments" and shoots them outward) would produce fast-flying earth/stone fragments. Column would shape them into elongated spikes rather than scattered pebbles. This is consistent with: (a) Qifrey blocking with earth (same material — a material familiarity effect), (b) the "lethal speed" suggesting a bolt-like fragmentation, and (c) spike/column = elongated projectile.
*What would confirm it:* a clear view of the glyph showing an earth sigil and Bolt signs.

**Hypothesis 2 (possible): Crystal sigil + Column + Bolt.**
Crystal (creates hard crystalline objects) would produce rigid, pointed spikes naturally — crystal shards are inherently spike-like, and the Crystal Shard spell already demonstrates this pairing. The Spike spell may be a more offensive, multi-spike variant.
*What would confirm it:* a crystal-colored or crystalline appearance of the produced spikes in the art.

**Hypothesis 3 (possible): Earth + Dispersion + Column.**
Dispersion pours magic outward in all directions; combined with earth and a Column shaping it into spikes, this could produce a radial volley of earth spikes. Less directionally controlled than Bolt but potentially more area-covering.
*What would confirm it:* spikes appearing to spray in multiple directions from a central point.

**Hypothesis 4 (long-shot): A Brimmed-Cap-exclusive or forbidden mechanism.**
Sasaran is a Brimmed Cap witch who has shown willingness to use extreme or unusual magic. The spell's entirely-unknown composition could involve signs outside the documented set, or a specialized material (wood, bone, crystal) chosen for lethality. The fact that the wiki explicitly flags it as "entirely unknown" even by Witch Hat Atelier standards suggests it may involve unusual components.
*What would confirm it:* the spell appearing in other Brimmed Cap combat contexts, or any panel showing the glyph detail.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Based on the described effect, the substance must be something that can form into **solid, rigid spikes** — pointing to earth (stone), crystal, or possibly metal (if a yet-unknown sigil exists). The "lethal speed" phrasing parallels the Bolt sign's documented behavior ("fast fragments") and the directional force of Column.

The most consistent cross-reference is the **Crystal Shard spell**, which uses Crystal + Column + Bolt to produce fast-flying crystal shards. If Spike is structurally similar but using earth or a different material, it would be a parallel design. Crystal Shard is explicitly classified as a crystal spell; if Spike uses earth, the result would be heavier stone spikes rather than crystal shards — more blunt, less sharp, but potentially larger.

Qifrey blocking Spike with an **earth wall** is suggestive: earth is the most natural counter to flying stone/earth projectiles (same-material barrier), and it required a full defensive spell rather than a dodge. This suggests the spikes carry significant mass and force, consistent with stone rather than the lighter/sharper crystal.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Crystal Shard** — most structurally similar: produces flying sharp projectiles using Crystal + Column + Bolt.
- **Water Bolt** — water + Bolt = fast water fragments; same Bolt mechanic applied to water.

**Bottom line.** A combat spell of entirely unknown composition. The described effect (large solid spikes at lethal velocity) best matches an earth or crystal sigil paired with Bolt and/or Column. This is a **DOCS-ONLY entry** — no catalog recipe is warranted without a confirmed composition.

# Watershot Seal

Watershot seal is a water-type spell that creates a stream of water.

## Description
Watershot seal is a spell that consists of a central water sigil surrounded by column keystones. Assuming all the keystones are of equivalent length, it will shoot water straight up from the glyph.

## Usage
Watershot seal was the first spell which Coco learned while at Qifrey's Atelier. When Coco completed the glyph, it misfired and hit Agott in the face, soaking her. To Coco's surprise, Agott wasn't mad, and she walked towards Coco and explained that the reason the spell shot of to the side rather than straight up was because Coco had drawn one of the column keystones longer than the others, causing the spell to be off balance and shoot in the direction of that longer keystone. Coco later used this same concept during the Consent of the Crown to make the Skysoaring Seal, which she used to pass the test.

# Light Beam

> Projects a steady beam of light straight up from the seal.

## Overview
Light Beam is a light-type spell: a **Light** sigil ringed by **Column** keystones. It generates a beam of light pointing up out of the glyph. The more precisely the glyph is drawn, the **steadier** the beam — it is one of the spells used as drawing practice (Agott).

## Validity
**Valid** (engine-confirmed). Light core + inward Column ring. Engine: **stable, radial symmetry**; aim "above the seal."

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** Light — produces light.
- **Form (signs):** Column ×4 (cardinals; the wiki shows four), inward, ringed. Column projects the substance as a tight beam/column.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain.

## Deduced effect
Engine readout: *"The light is projected as a tight column or beam, above the seal."* A clean vertical shaft of light — the light analogue of the Watershot Seal (water + Column ring). Balanced columns keep it vertical; an unbalanced/larger column would lean the beam toward it.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Light sigil | substance | The light being projected. |
| Column ×8 (inward) | form | Collimates the light into a tight upward beam. |
| Drawing precision | quality | Cleaner glyph → steadier, straighter beam (canon). |
| Column count (4 vs more) | quantity | Denser ring + matcher count only; **same** effect & power (engine power = average sign *size*, not count). |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Column "makes the magic manifest as a column/beam above the glyph… if signs are unbalanced, it manifests toward the side with more/larger signs" ([docs/signs.md](../signs.md)). With Light as the substance this is the textbook beam: the same construction as Watershot (water) and the column-driven projections, just emitting light. The "steadier when drawn precisely" note matches the general rule that neater seals are more stable.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Light sigil** at the center.
2. Ring it with **Column** signs, pointed **inward**.
3. Keep the ring even and clean for a steady, vertical beam.
4. Enlarge one column (or the whole seal) to bias/strengthen the beam.

## Usage ideas
- **Signal / searchlight** — a vertical pillar of light visible from afar.
- **Illumination** — light a shaft, well, or dark room from a drawn surface.
- **Practice glyph** — its steadiness is a direct readout of drawing accuracy.

## Similar spells
- **Watershot Seal** — identical Column-ring construction on water (a jet instead of a beam).
- **Floatglow Lamp Seal** — light + Float/Column for a *floating* lamp rather than a fixed beam.
- **Bird of Light Beacon** — light shaped into a moving form rather than a straight beam.

# Sand Bridge

The sand bridge is an earth-type spell used to construct a bridge out of sand.

## Description
The spell is composed of several unrecognizable keystones, the only one known being column. The spell functions by creating a bridge out of the earth, and is used to make temporary bridges until an actual bridge can be constructed.

Coco looking at the constructed sand bridge
Coco looking at the constructed sand bridge

## Usage
The spell was used by Olruggio after the Stairway River disaster had subsided. As the original bridge had collapsed, crossing the river became impossible. To solve this, he created a sand bridge as a temporary fix. Since he was drawing the glyphs on rocks, a normal wand would have been unusable, so he utilized searneedle wand to etch the glyph into the stone.

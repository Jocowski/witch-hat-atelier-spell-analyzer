# Anti Scalewolf Curse

Anti Scalewolf Curse is a forbidden spells that nullifies the mental effects of the Scalewolf Curse spell

## Description
The only thing known of this spell is that, contrary to the common belief that reverting the sigils from a spell reverts the effect, the Anti Scalewolf Curse is a completly diferent spell from it's counterpart. When used on someone affected by the scalewolf curse, the afflicted individual will revert to a human completely.

## Usage
At first, the girls tried to a reversed drawing of the Scalewolf Curse to cure Euini from the spell, but this didn't work. Iguin explained that most forbidden spells can only be nullified by drawing a completly different spell. To prove his point, Iguin makes a collar with this spell and demonstrates it's ability to cure Euini, only to remove it, causing Eunie to revert back to a scaled wolf and tries coercing Coco into joinng the brimhats, telling her in order to get the spell from him and save Eunie, she has to join him. However, the girls steal the collar with a Grasping Wind spell, thwarting Iguin's plan.

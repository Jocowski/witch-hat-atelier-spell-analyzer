# Vapor Bubble Spell

> A two-circle dew-still that pulls moisture from the surrounding air, chills it past
> its dew point, and collects the condensate into a single globule of fresh water.

## Overview
Inscribed on the inner surfaces of two shallow saucers, the Vapor Bubble Spell is a
hybrid **air + water** contraption. The outer circle circulates humid air (Wind), draws
vapor in (Gather), channels it (Column), and chills it (Cool); the nested inner circle
holds a Water core that collects the resulting droplets into one shimmering bubble of
drinkable water. It is canon — the volume 12 bonus introduces both the **Cool** and
**Gather** signs specifically for this spell.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine: two nested circles, both well-formed).
- Outer circle: `info` — stable (bilateral symmetry); aim "above the seal" from the
  directional signs.
- Inner circle: `warning` — "no signs around the core (raw, undirected discharge)." This
  is a **false alarm for this design**: the Water core is meant only to *pool* the
  condensate, not to be shaped into a tool or weapon; all the forming work (gather, chill,
  channel) is done by the outer ring.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):**
  - **Water** (inner core) — manipulate, **collect**, and create water; long-duration
    water spells *collect* because creating is costlier (sigils.md:41). This spell runs
    in the cheap collect mode, harvesting existing atmospheric moisture.
  - **Wind ×2** (outer, flanking left/right) — movement and manipulation, **but not the
    creation**, of air (sigils.md:65). Circulates humid air through the device.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Gather ×2** (directional) — actively draws material in to feed the spell; "may be
    less passive than collection" (signs.md:197). Pulls vapor toward the water core.
  - **Column ×4** (directional) — balanced ring channels/confines the airflow rather than
    firing a beam (signs.md:34).
  - **Cool ×4** (non-directional) — chills the gathered air below dew point; the docs name
    this as the condensation mechanism, "to help it condense from the air" (signs.md:147).
- **Ring:** outer open / prepared; inner closed.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
Engine — outer: *"the air is projected as a tight column or beam, above the seal; it
actively draws in surrounding material to feed the spell; it is chilled and cooled."*
Inner: *"the water pours and flows outward."* Combined: the air-handling ring with the
water-collection basin nested inside it.

In plain terms: **a dew-still.** The outer circle circulates humid air, pulls vapor in,
channels it through a cooling zone where it condenses, and the inner Water core gathers
the droplets into a single globule of fresh water.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Water (core) | water sigil / substance | the collection basin; without it the dew never pools into a coherent bubble |
| Wind ×2 | air sigil / substance | move humid air through the seal; without them only stale adjacent air is processed |
| Gather ×2 | directional / support-intake | actively draws vapor inward to the core; without them condensation forms but doesn't migrate in |
| Column ×4 | directional / form | channel and confine the airflow; without them the current disperses past the cooling zone |
| Cool ×4 | non-directional / special | **the condensation step** — chills humid air below dew point; without them no liquid forms at all |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The spell is a physically literal **atmospheric-water generator**, and every piece maps
onto a real condensation chain:

1. **Wind moves humid air** into the seal. Wind cannot *create* air (sigils.md:65), but
   circulating existing humid air is squarely within its remit — and that's all this needs.
2. **Cool drops that air below its dew point**, precipitating vapor into liquid droplets.
   The docs name this as Cool's purpose in exactly this spell (signs.md:147).
3. **Gather actively pulls** the condensing moisture inward (signs.md:197) — note Gather is
   more aggressive than Collection, which suits drawing in diffuse vapor rather than
   scooping standing water.
4. **The Water core collects** it into one globule, running in water's **cheap collect
   mode** rather than the costly create mode (sigils.md:41). That is why a saucer-mounted
   still can run continuously without exhausting the caster: it isn't conjuring water from
   nothing, it's harvesting what's already in the air — exactly the long-duration use the
   docs describe.

The **two-circle architecture is the elegant part**: the outer ring is the air-conditioning
plant (intake → channel → chill) and the nested inner ring is the catch basin. The nest
relation means the water only forms once the outer ring is sealed and the air is being
conditioned — you cool and gather *before* you collect. That is a genuine division of
labour, not decoration.

Two engine flags are artifacts of this design, not defects:
- *"Air projected as a tight column/beam above the seal"* — the engine routes balanced
  Columns through its standard "aim up" math, but balanced Columns have no net direction
  (signs.md:34); on a saucer they **confine the airflow**, they don't fire a jet upward.
- *"Inner core: raw undirected discharge"* — the warning fires because the Water core has
  no signs of its own, but here that is **correct and intended**: the water just pools; the
  forming work lives in the outer ring.

**Closest canon analogues** (reading from the docs, not an engine catalog match):
- **Purify** — also a standing, passive water-processing device that uses an intake sign
  (Collection). Vapor Bubble is its **air-sourced cousin**: where Purify cleans standing
  water, this harvests water *from vapor* with Gather + Cool. Both run in water's collect
  mode.
- **Rainbringer** — also condenses atmospheric water, but releases it as *rainfall*. Vapor
  Bubble is like a miniaturized, contained Rainbringer that **keeps** the water.
- **Water Pen** — same "pool, don't shoot" philosophy as the inner core here.

**Bottom line:** a fully feasible, canon dew-still. Where most water spells either *create*
water or *collect* it from a liquid source, the Vapor Bubble is the only one that
**harvests it from atmospheric humidity** — a true air/water hybrid. Cool is the innovation
that makes condensation possible; Wind + Gather supply and pull the air; the nested Water
core simply pools the harvest into a drinkable globule.

## How to draw it
1. Draw two concentric circles (a small inner ring inside a larger outer ring).
2. **Inner ring (closed):** Water sigil at the centre. Optionally flank it above and below
   with the two **Gather** signs right at the inner ring's edge (so they read as part of
   the ring and pull vapor toward the core).
3. **Outer ring:** a **Wind** sigil on the left and another on the right.
4. Beside each Wind sigil place a **Column** sign (four total), balanced, to channel the
   airflow — keep them even so the air is confined, not aimed.
5. In the four diagonal positions place the four **Cool** signs.
6. Maintain bilateral symmetry for stability; keep the seal neat for a long-running device.
   A larger seal yields more throughput.

## Usage ideas
- **Survival water source** in a desert or at sea — fresh, drinkable water from thin air,
  no reservoir needed.
- **A pair of saucers** set out overnight that you find a bubble of water in by morning.
- **Dehumidifier** for a damp cellar, archive, or atelier to protect parchment and pigments.
- Mix **Azuremoon Flower** dye to extend duration for an all-day still; **Roaming Scallop
  Shells** to keep the seal waterproof against its own condensate.

## Similar spells
Purify (air-sourced cousin), Rainbringer (releases water as rain instead of keeping it),
Water Pen (pools water into a coherent body rather than a jet). The Vapor Bubble is the
distinctive one for sourcing its water from atmospheric humidity via the Cool + Gather pair.

## Notes & limitations
- The Cool sign's meaning is canon-uncertain — "to either make the water cooler to drink or
  to help it condense" (signs.md:147); the Gather + Wind context makes condensation the
  load-bearing reading here.
- The "no signs around the inner core" warning is intended (the core is a basin).
- Multi-circle spells currently skip the engine's catalog matcher, so the analyzer reports
  no "similar" match for this spell even though it is now catalogued — see the planned
  multi-circle matching fix.
- Not forbidden: pure elemental utility, nothing body- or reality-affecting.

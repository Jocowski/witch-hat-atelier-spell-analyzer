# Rising Wave

Rising wave is a water-type spell that creates a surge of water.

## Description
Rising wave has a central water sigil surrounded by a ring of column keystones. On half of the glyph, directional keystones are located between the column keystones. This spell creates a surge of water that travels up diagonally, allowing the caster to propel themselves up into the air. The glyph was used by Qifrey in his battle with Sasaran.

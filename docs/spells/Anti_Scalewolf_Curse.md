# Anti Scalewolf Curse

> A forbidden counter-spell that completely reverses the Scalewolf Curse, restoring a transformed person to fully human form — something a simple inversion of the Scalewolf Curse cannot accomplish.

## Overview
The Anti Scalewolf Curse is a forbidden spell designed specifically to undo the Scalewolf Curse. Unlike the Inverted Scalewolf Curse (which only restores the mind), the Anti Scalewolf Curse reverses both the mental and physical transformation, returning the affected individual to a completely human form. It is classified forbidden because it directly affects the human body. Iguin demonstrated it by placing it on a collar around Euini's neck; when removed, Euini reverted to scalewolf form, confirming the spell requires ongoing contact or repeated application. No sigils or signs are known.

## In plain terms (sum up)
The Anti Scalewolf Curse is the only complete cure for the Scalewolf Curse. You cannot cure it by simply inverting the original spell — the curse binds too deeply for that. Instead, this is an entirely different spell with its own composition, one powerful enough to undo the fused creature-identity and restore a human body. Iguin inscribed it on a collar; as long as Euini wore the collar, he appeared human. When he removed it, he transformed back — which suggests the spell may need to remain active (or be permanently drawn on the body) to hold.

## Deduced effect
The confirmed canon effect is full reversal of the Scalewolf Curse — physical and mental restoration to human form. The spell must interface with the same body-transformation that the Scalewolf Curse imposed, which means it likely operates at the level of substance-identity imposed by the scalewolf sigil.

Because the composition is entirely unknown, only ranked hypotheses are possible:

1. **Most likely — a dedicated body-restoration or purification pathway:** The spell may use a `purify`-class operator to strip the creature-identity from the body, or a specialized form of the repetition/revert mechanic to reset the body's substance-state. Unlike simple inversion (which tries to run the original operators backwards), a "purification" approach would act on the result, not the mechanism, explaining why it works when inversion fails.

2. **Possible — scalewolf sigil with opposing operators:** The spell may contain the same scalewolf sigil as the Scalewolf Curse, but paired with operators that cause the creature-identity substance to be *dispelled outward* from the body rather than fused in. Region signs pointing outward, or inverted pull signs (i.e. push), could expel the creature substance.

3. **Possible — envelop / bind with counter-identity:** The spell could envelope the body in a binding that suppresses the creature-transformation, rather than reversing it on the cellular level. This would explain the collar mechanic — the binding is held in place by the physical object and released when the object is removed. The cure would be conditional, not permanent, unless the spell is drawn directly on the body (which is precisely what makes it forbidden).

4. **Long-shot — contraption with repetition sub-seal:** A nested repetition mechanism could continuously reset the body to its pre-curse state. This would require an ongoing energy input and would explain why the collar stops working when removed.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Anti Scalewolf Curse must undo something that simple inversion cannot. Inversion reverses the direction of invertible operators — it runs the mechanism backwards. For the Inverted Scalewolf Curse, this restores the mind (which is a reversible mental state) but cannot reverse the physical fusion (which is locked in). The Anti Scalewolf Curse therefore operates on a different axis — it either removes the creature-substance entirely, suppresses its expression, or triggers a different biological reset.

This mirrors the system principle from CORE.md: operators have preconditions and failure modes. Inversion fails here because the precondition for "reversing the transformation" is not met by simply flipping the original operators — the substance (fused creature material) is still present in the body. The Anti Scalewolf Curse must address the substance, not just the operator direction.

**Closest canon analogue:** The Repetition Seal rewinds objects to a prior state. If the Anti Scalewolf Curse works on a similar principle applied to a living body, it would be the most direct analogy — but Repetition is documented as non-body-targeting. Purify strips contaminants from a substance; an analogous "purify body of creature-substance" would be the conceptual twin. Both comparisons highlight why this spell is forbidden: it applies substance-manipulation directly to a human body.

**Bottom line:** The Anti Scalewolf Curse is a complete and standalone forbidden spell, not an inversion. Its exact composition is unknown. The collar-delivery mechanism confirms it can be inscribed on objects rather than directly on skin, but it must remain in contact (or be permanently applied) to sustain the effect.

# Snugstone Spell

The snugstone spell (unofficial name) is a fire-type spell invented by Olruggio which radiates warmth.

## Description
The spell consists of a fire sigil, backwards column keystones, and a radial keystone. It radiates warmth outward without the need for a flame and is used on the snugstone, an item used to keep you warm while you sleep.

## Usage
This spell was first seen drawn on the snugstones that Olruggio gave to Qifrey and his aprentices so they could sleep better.

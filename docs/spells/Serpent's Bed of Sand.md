# Serpent's Bed of Sand

The serpent's bed of sand is an earth spell created by the four apprentices of Qifrey's Atelier that creates a cloud made of sand.

## Description
The serpent's bed of sand is an amalgamation of several spells. At it's center is a billowing collection spell, which contains a central billowing keystone surrounded by collection keystones. Arranged around that are several wall breaker seals, the seals containing an earth sigil paired with crush and column signs, all wrapped up in a repetition seal which contains repetition sigils, convergence signs, and column signs. It creates a large, fluffy cloud out of sand, and is strong enough to hold up a dragon.

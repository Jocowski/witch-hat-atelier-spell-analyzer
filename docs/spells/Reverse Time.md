# Reverse Time

Reverse Time is a family of niche-type spells. Distinct from repetition spells, as they do not use the repetition sigil.

## Description
Reverse Time spells have no identifiable sigil, but seem to be similar to the repitition spells, as there appear to be a number of intermediary spells between the two, such as the ones on Ininia's gloves. They are made of straight lines which form many geometric shapes in a six-sided star shape. Like the name implies, they are used to reverse time on what they are used on, mostly to undo wear and other types of damage.

These spells have effect up to a maximum time of a day and are not permanent. Once the time frame expires, the glyph vanishes by itself and time takes effect on the target once more, putting it back in the state it was without the glyph. The glyph can however be reapplied again to repeat the effect. According to Qifrey, they are rather difficult spells to learn and, despite the law, their nature leads every witch to think about using them on a human body.

## Usage
Qifrey demonstrates the effects of time reversal on an ink pot Coco accidentally breaks. This not only puts the pot back together, but the spilled ink goes back in it, allowing the pot to be emptied into another one to save the ink before the glyph expires.

Ininia uses a large time reversal glyph to prevent Dagda from dying from the wounds dealt to him, successfully rewinding him back to before he was wounded. This not only removes his wounds but resets his memories of the time affected, leaving him in a state of confusion. The glyph expires several times and has to be redrawn quickly after it vanishes so he does not die.

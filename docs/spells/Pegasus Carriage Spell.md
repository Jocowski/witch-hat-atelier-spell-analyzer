# Pegasus Carriage Spell

The Pegasus Carriage Spell is a wind spell that allows the Pegasus Carriage to fly.

## Description
The spell contains several sigils similar to the wind sigil, but not identical. The spell is the main component of the Pegasus Carriage, which uses the spell to keep it aloft while it is being pulled.

It is composed of two sigils of Whorling Wind, as well as two sigils of Wind Underfoot. Surrounding the sigils of Whorling Wind are three signs of columns. Surrounding the sigils of Wind Underfoot are four signs of levitation and four signs of aeriforms defined. Around the internal ring are various signs of columns and around the external ring are signs of wind. At the center of the seal are signs of Stability and Level Planes.

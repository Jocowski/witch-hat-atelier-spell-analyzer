# Flowers of Light

> A light sigil shaped by the Flower decorative sigil and surrounded by five inward-facing
> Levitation signs — produces numerous floating blossoms of light.

## Overview
Flowers of Light is a light-type spell confirmed in the docs/sigils.md spell lists (both the
Flower sigil and the Light sigil list it). It has no dedicated wiki page, but a clean seal
image exists in `assets/images/spells/Flowers_of_Light.jpg`. Reading that image directly:
the center carries a **Flower sigil** (the five-petalled pentagon) with a **Light sigil**
inscribed inside it, and five **Levitation signs** are placed evenly around the ring (each
with the arrowhead pointing inward toward the core). The recipe is therefore: light (substance)
+ flower (decorative shape) as the core pair, with ×5 inward levitation signs. The spell
produces flowers made of light that float free of gravity — blossoms of luminous material
drifting in the air.

## In plain terms (sum up)
This spell makes glowing flowers appear and float in the air. The light sigil creates the
luminous material; the flower sigil wraps it in a blossom shape; and the ring of levitation
signs lifts the flowers free of gravity so they drift and float rather than falling. The result
is a display of softly glowing flower-shapes drifting through the air — a light-and-decorative
spell that is as much art as utility. Unlike the simple Light Beam or Bird of Light Beacon,
this is a many-blooms display: multiple flower shapes all floating at once.

## Validity
**Valid.** The Light sigil at the center is the substance core; the Flower sigil is a valid
secondary sigil (decorative, element `decorative`). Five Levitation signs are inward-facing
in a radially symmetric ring. All preconditions met: light is self-sufficient (create mode);
levitation on a light/fire substance lifts straight up (no aim needed, no element source
needed). The five-fold symmetry is stable (radial). No blocking issues.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):**
  - **Light** (`light`, fire family) — the primary sigil; creates luminous material.
    Capability **create** (self-sufficient; sigils.md:29). State **luminous**.
  - **Flower** (`flower`, decorative) — secondary sigil inscribed around the light sigil at the
    center; shapes the light "to manifest in the shape of numerous flowers" (sigils.md:141).
    Decorative sigils are "mostly aesthetic" (sigils.md:113) — Flower imposes a blossom-shape on
    the luminous output without adding a new substance. State **immaterial** (a shape constraint).
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Levitation ×5** (`levitation`, directional, motion) — five signs evenly distributed around
    the ring at 72° intervals; each with the arrowhead **pointing inward** (toward the core),
    read from the seal image. On a **light** substance, levitation lifts the output straight up
    without steering — "for water, fire, and light spells, levitation causes the spell's effect,
    as well as objects held overtop the seal, to levitate in the air" (signs.md:55;
    signs-directional.md, Levitation). The sign's size sets the weight of objects lifted aloft.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink (no dyes visible in the seal image).

## Deduced effect
**Numerous flowers of light, floating free of gravity above the seal.** The Light sigil
generates luminous material; the Flower sigil shapes it into blossom forms (multiple flowers —
"numerous," per sigils.md:141); and the five inward Levitation signs lift all the flowers free
of gravity so they drift upward and float in the air. The result is an ascending cloud of
glowing flower-shapes — a luminous floral display.

The deduction is confirmed by the seal image and both sigil entries; confidence is **high** for
the recipe and effect description.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Light sigil | substance | Generates the luminous material the flowers are made of. Self-sufficient; no source needed. Without it there is no light to shape. |
| Flower sigil | decorative (shape) | Imposes the blossom form on the light. Without it the light would manifest as raw glow or whatever the Levitation forms allow — not flower-shaped. |
| Levitation ×5 (inward) | motion | Lifts the flower-shapes free of gravity so they float. On a light substance: straight upward lift, no steering. Without it the flowers would sit motionless at the seal surface rather than drifting up. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Light is a fire-family create substance.** It "functions to produce magic that manifests in
the form of light" (sigils.md:29) and generates its own luminous material with no external
source. The spell is therefore entirely self-sufficient: it works in any lighting condition,
with no need for ambient fire or existing light to manipulate.

**Flower as a decorative sigil:** "Flower causes magic to manifest in the shape of numerous
flowers" (sigils.md:141). As with all decorative sigils, the Flower does not supply a new
substance — it shapes the existing one (light here) into the creature/object form it depicts.
It is placed as a secondary sigil at the center alongside Light. Canon says decorative sigils
are "mostly aesthetic" (sigils.md:113), and the Flower is no exception: it is purely a
shape-giver. The practical effect is that instead of light-columns or light-beams, the
substance emerges as flower-shapes.

**Levitation on light:** per the canon rule documented in signs-directional.md and signs.md:55,
levitation on fire/light/water lifts **vertically without steering** — size of the levitation
sign sets the weight that can be held aloft, but the direction is always upward. The five inward
levitation signs lift all the blossom-shapes straight up, allowing them to drift freely above
the seal. The engine may report a spurious lateral aim or spin reading from the levitation
geometry — override this; the lift is purely vertical for a light substance.

**Five-fold symmetry:** unlike most spells that use 4-fold (cardinal) or 8-fold symmetry, Flowers
of Light uses five levitation signs in a pentagonal arrangement. This five-fold symmetry matches
the Flower sigil's natural shape (five petals, pentagonal center) — a neat design choice where
the sign count echoes the sigil's form. The arrangement is stable (radially symmetric).

**Closest canon analogue — Pyreball Seal:** the Pyreball (fire + levitation ring) also uses a
levitation ring on a luminous/energetic substance to hold an elevated output above the seal.
The difference: Pyreball is a single levitating fireball (no shape); Flowers of Light is a
field of levitating flower-shapes (decorative sigil adds the form). Both are "levitating
light/fire source" spells; Flowers of Light adds the Flower sigil for an aesthetic multi-bloom
result rather than a single ball.

**Comparison to Bird of Light Beacon:** the Bird of Light Beacon (light + bird_a decorative +
dispersion) is the other canon decorative-sigil + light spell. Both use a decorative sigil
to shape light into a living-thing form. The difference: Bird of Light Beacon uses the bird
sigil + Dispersion (spreading, flying motion); Flowers of Light uses the Flower sigil +
Levitation (floating upward). One makes a flying light-bird; the other makes floating light-blossoms.

**Bottom line:** Flowers of Light is one of the simplest-to-reason spells in the light family.
Light (create, self-sufficient) shaped by Flower (blossom form) and lifted by Levitation (five
evenly-spaced inward signs). The recipe reads cleanly from the seal image; the physics are
standard; the only unusual point is the five-fold (pentagonal) symmetry echoing the Flower
sigil's five-petal form.

## How to draw it
1. **Light sigil** at the center.
2. **Flower sigil** drawn as a larger ring around the light sigil, both at the core position —
   this is the two-sigil center: light inside, flower around it. (The seal image shows the flower
   pentagon surrounding the light square-diamond.)
3. **Five Levitation signs** evenly spaced around the outer ring at 72° intervals (0°, 72°, 144°,
   216°, 288°), each with the **arrowhead pointing inward** toward the center.
4. Keep the spacing even for stable five-fold symmetry; uneven spacing tilts the float.
5. Close the ring. The flowers appear and drift upward; larger seal = more flowers and more lift.

## Usage ideas
- **Decorative display / celebration** — a cloud of glowing flower-blossoms for festivals, Silver
  Eve, or ceremonial occasions.
- **Mood lighting** — soft floating light in flower shapes; more pleasant than a column beam.
- **Marking a location** — the floating blossom cloud is visible at range.
- **Welcoming / farewell display** — a classic magical "flowers floating upward" farewell or
  celebration image.
- **Combination with dyes** — color dyes would tint the flowers, making a multi-colored blossom
  display.

## Similar spells
- **Bird of Light Beacon** — light + bird decorative sigil + dispersion; a flying light-bird
  rather than floating blossoms.
- **Pyreball Seal** — fire + levitation ring; same "levitation holds luminous material aloft"
  structure, single fireball instead of many blossoms.
- **Floatglow Lamp Seal** — light + float/column; a floating lamp (practical lighting), not
  decorative flowers.

## Notes & limitations
- **No dedicated wiki page:** recipe confirmed only from sigils.md lists + the seal image.
  If a full wiki page is published, check for additional signs not visible in this image.
- **Engine levitation artifact:** four (or five) balanced levitation signs on a light substance
  may trigger a spurious lateral aim/spin reading in the engine. Override it; per canon the lift
  is straight up (signs-directional.md, Levitation findings).
- **Single-sign "asymmetric" artifact:** a five-fold ring with five identical signs should be
  stable; if the engine flags instability, it is an artifact of the odd count (not 4- or 8-fold).
- The Flower sigil is decorative and adds **no practical utility** beyond the shape — the spell
  does not do anything flowers can do (grow, pollinate, etc.); it only makes light look like flowers.
- Not forbidden: ordinary light + decorative sigil, no body magic.

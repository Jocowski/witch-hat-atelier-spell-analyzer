# Flame Burst Spell

> A fire-type spell using Column and Pull signs — likely draws in ambient flame and projects it
> outward as a focused burst. **No dedicated wiki page exists**; this entry reasons from the
> sign documentation.

## Overview
Flame Burst Spell is a fire-type spell listed in the docs/spells.md spell index and visible in
`assets/images/spells/Flame_Burst_Spell.png` (a manga panel). The wiki's spell list page links
to it but has no dedicated page (red-link as of the batch date). The spell's composition is
confirmed by cross-referencing two sign pages: **docs/signs.md** lists it under both **Column**
("Spells Using Column: Flame Burst Spell …") and **Pull** ("Spells Using Pull: Flame Burst
Spell …"). Therefore: fire sigil + column + pull. The exact sign counts and geometry are
unknown — the manga panel shows a partial seal in active use, not a clean construction diagram.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Pull draws in nearby fire (or any fire-element matter) toward the seal; Column channels it out
as a focused beam. Together on a fire substance they produce a "burst": ambient flame (or the
seal's own created fire) is gathered inward and then projected outward in a tight column. Think
of it as a fire-pump — sucking fuel in on one side, firing it out the other as a pressurized
burst of flame. The name "burst" suggests a short, powerful discharge rather than a sustained
jet.

## Validity
**Valid** for the identified components. A fire sigil satisfies the core requirement. Column is a
form operator (projects the fire as a beam); Pull is a direction operator (draws same-element
matter inward). The pairing is mechanically sound: Pull gathers fire inward while Column
channels it outward — the two directional forces resolve into a concentrated discharge.

The exact sign counts and placement are unknown; symmetry cannot be confirmed. If the pull and
column signs are balanced, the spell fires upward (or in the balanced direction); if unbalanced,
it leans toward the dominant signs.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** **Fire** (`fire`) — creates and manipulates flame and heat. Capability
  **create and manipulate** (sigils.md:13). Self-sufficient; no external fire source required,
  though Pull can also draw in real ambient fire for additional power.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Column** (`column`, directional, form) — projects the fire as a tight beam/column.
    "Causes a spell to manifest in a column or beam above the seal" (signs.md:34). Inward-facing
    columns channel fire into a directed jet. On fire: a lance of flame.
  - **Pull** (`pull`, directional, direction) — "causes anything matching its seal's sigil to
    be pulled towards the seal when the arrow portion is pointing inwards" (signs.md:65). On a
    fire substance: draws ambient flame/heat inward. This gathering-then-firing action is the
    "burst" mechanic: Pull compresses fire toward the seal, then Column forces it out the front.
- **Ring:** closed (canon active; seen in use in the manga panel).
- **Ink / dyes:** unknown.

## Deduced effect
The deduction is **tentative** (no wiki recipe confirmation, seal geometry unknown):

**Most likely reading:** Pull draws ambient fire (and/or the self-created fire substance) toward
the seal; Column then forces that concentrated fire out as a directed column of flame. The effect
is a high-pressure fire burst — like igniting a jet, not a sustained flame. "Burst" implies short
duration and high intensity.

**Ranked hypotheses for sign arrangement:**

1. **Most likely — Column outward, Pull inward:** Column signs facing outward (or balanced inward
   to fire above the seal) with Pull signs pointing inward, drawing fire in from the environment
   and simultaneously projected by Column. The Pull + Column pairing on fire creates a
   compression-then-projection cycle: pull fire in → column fires it out → burst.
   *What confirms it:* a clean manga image of the seal showing pull arrows inward + column facing
   outward/upward.

2. **Possible — Column as beam, Pull as range amplifier:** Pull draws fire from a distance into
   the beam's path, making the Column-projected flame reach farther and hit harder. Pull here
   amplifies the column's effective range by supplying extra fire to the jet mid-flight.
   *What confirms it:* if the seal shows pull signs aimed laterally (toward the column's path)
   rather than inward to the core.

3. **Long-shot — inverted Pull as propellant:** inverted Pull **pushes** fire away (signs.md:65).
   If some Pull signs are inverted, they push fire outward while Column aims it, creating a
   two-stage fire projector (push + shape). Canon does not show inverted Pull in any confirmed
   spell; mark this speculative.
   *What confirms it:* inverted Pull flags visible in a clean seal scan.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Fire sigil | substance | Creates/provides the fire; self-sufficient. Without it there is no flame substance. |
| Pull | direction | Draws existing fire inward (or pushes it outward if inverted) — the gathering / "charging" phase of the burst. |
| Column | form | Channels the concentrated fire outward as a tight beam — the "firing" phase of the burst. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Fire is a create + manipulate substance** (sigils.md:13). It generates its own flame without a
source — so the spell is self-sufficient even if no ambient fire is present. But Pull also draws
*existing* fire inward: in a room with flames, the spell pulls that real fire toward it and
amplifies the output. This dual-mode (self-created + gathered) makes the Flame Burst Spell more
powerful in fire-rich environments.

**Pull on fire:** Pull is a direction operator that "draws matter of the same element inward"
(signs.md:65; signs-directional.md, Pull). On fire, it draws ambient flame/heat toward the seal.
The canon precondition is "it moves *existing* same-element matter" — but fire can also be
self-created here, so the seal never fails for want of a source.

**Column on fire:** Column fires a "lance of flame" (CORE.md §1, example). It is the fire
analogue of the Watershot jet — water→jet, fire→lance. The combination Pull + Column on fire
reads naturally as a "suck-in and blast-out" mechanism: Pull compresses the ambient fire toward
the core, Column fires the compressed bundle outward as a forceful burst.

**Comparison to Flame Shot Seal:** The existing Flame Shot Seal (fire + Column + Region) projects
a sustained directional flame. The Flame Burst Spell (fire + Column + Pull) swaps Region for
Pull — Region *confines* the flame forward (acts as a barrel); Pull *draws* fire inward first.
The difference: Flame Shot = a directed jet from the seal's own fire; Flame Burst = a charged
compression-release. The word "burst" reinforces the idea of a brief, high-intensity discharge
rather than a sustained beam.

**Bottom line:** The sign documentary confirms Column + Pull on fire. The most likely reading is
that Pull gathers fire inward and Column fires it out — a fire burst. The exact geometry (sign
counts, facing angles) and whether Pull is inward or angled (vortex mode) remain unknown without
a clean seal image. The spell is buildable and mechanically coherent; confidence in the
interpretation is medium.

## How to draw it
(Reconstruction guidance — not a confirmed recipe.)

1. **Fire sigil** at the center.
2. **Column signs** around the ring, pointing inward (toward core) — these aim the flame
   upward/outward as a projected lance.
3. **Pull signs** pointing inward — these draw fire toward the seal, charging the burst.
4. Signs likely alternate (pull-column-pull-column) or are arranged in complementary groups
   for balance.
5. For a "burst" rather than a sustained jet, fewer signs (3–4 of each) may be used; a dense
   ring would sustain the flame longer.

## Usage ideas
- **Combat fire burst** — project a brief, high-intensity column of fire at a target.
- **Fire in fire-rich environments** — near existing fires, Pull amplifies the output by drawing
  ambient fuel.
- **Area denial** — project the burst upward to ignite overhead material.
- **Pyrotechnics / signal flare** — a dramatic vertical fire burst as a signal.

## Similar spells
- **Flame Shot Seal** — fire + Column + Region; a directional sustained flame. Swap Region for
  Pull → Flame Burst.
- **Pyreball Seal** — fire + Levitation; a floating ball of flame rather than a projected burst.
- **Spiraling Flame** — fire + Column + Sign of Wind; a spinning flame column. The sign-of-wind
  is the twist keystone; here Pull is the gather keystone.
- **Grasping Wind** — wind + Pull (angled for vortex); the wind-element Pull-based spell that
  confirms Pull's vortex capability.

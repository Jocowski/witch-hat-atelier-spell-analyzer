# Cloak Spell

> A complex compound seal written on a cloak that lets the wearer control it remotely, hide inside its shadow, and pass objects through it between distant locations.

## Overview
The Cloak Spell is a fusion of three sub-spells merged into a single contraption seal, composed by the Brimmed Cap witch Sasaran. It combines the **Gathering Shadows** effect (light absorption / concealment), the **Flying Puppet of Diversion** motion (remote erratic flight), and the **Windowway Spell** spatial link (passage between locations). Written on the inside of Sasaran's cloak, it allows him to cast spells *through* the cloak at a remote location while keeping his actual body hidden elsewhere.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Draw this on a cloak and you get three things at once: the cloak drapes itself in shadow so it and anything beneath it are invisible, it can fly around and dart erratically on its own (controlled remotely by the wearer's mind), and it can act as a portal so something can pass through it from one location to another. Sasaran used this to fight Qifrey while standing far away, making his cloak the decoy, the spell-caster's hand, and the doorway all at once.

## Validity
**Valid — contraption (compound multi-function seal).** The seal is described as having a central body with a Vision sigil at core, supplemented by Eye signs on 4 of the 8 Vision points and Wind sigils on the remaining 4 points; Bend and dancing_puppet signs ring the outer body; a Windowway-type ring loops around the entire main body. Each sub-spell is valid on its own (Gathering Shadows, Flying Puppet of Diversion, Windowway are all documented canon spells). The fusion seal is radially structured. The complexity is high — this is advanced witchcraft, not a beginner composition.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):**
  - **Vision** (`vision_sigil`) — occupies the center; the light-manipulation core that anchors the concealment and sight component. A sign-as-sigil (canBeCenter).
  - **Wind** (sigils, ×4 placed on the 4 remaining Vision points) — provides the substance for the aerial motion component (the Puppet's flight). Wind is **manipulate** mode: it moves air but does not create it ([sigils.md](../sigils.md)), so the cloak flies by acting on ambient air.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Eye ×4** (non-directional, special) — placed on 4 of the 8 Vision-asterisk points. With Vision and Bend they form the concealment trio: absorbs light into shadow, making the host invisible.
  - **Bend ×4** (non-directional, special) — placed off the other 4 Vision points. Bends light and shadow around the object, completing the concealment trio.
  - **dancing_puppet ×4** (`dancing_puppet`, non-directional/asymmetric, motion) — around the ring of the body. Causes nearby objects to dart erratically into the sky; supplies the remote-flight component of the cloak.
- **Windowway ring** — a separate enclosing ring of bolt/direction signs (see Windowway Spell) that loops around the entire main body, adding the spatial-link / passage component.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
The Vision + Eye + Bend trio absorbs all light hitting the cloak, draping it (and what it covers) in shadow — the concealment component. The Wind sigils + dancing_puppet signs animate the cloak to fly remotely through air, darting and moving independently while Sasaran controls it from a distance. The outer Windowway ring creates a spatial passage, allowing objects (and spells) to travel through the cloak between locations. Combined: **a remote-controlled shadow-cloaked flying portal**.

> **Note on partial identification:** The wiki describes the Windowway ring and body clearly, but the exact keystones of the Windowway sub-component are not fully resolved from the source text alone (see Windowway Spell doc). The overall fusion behavior is canon-confirmed from Sasaran's use.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Vision sigil | core / substance | Anchors the light-manipulation; without it the concealment trio has no substance |
| Wind sigil ×4 | secondary substance | Provides the aerial element for Puppet's flight; without it the cloak cannot move through air |
| Eye ×4 | special — light absorption | Makes the host object absorb light and disappear into shadow |
| Bend ×4 | special — light bending | Curves light and shadow around the object, sealing the concealment effect |
| dancing_puppet ×4 | motion — remote flight | Makes the cloak dart and fly through the air; enables remote control |
| Windowway ring | spatial link | Opens a passage through the cloak between distant locations |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Vision** is a sign-as-sigil: it has no elemental substance of its own but is `canBeCenter`, supplying the light-manipulation domain. With **Eye** and **Bend** it forms the canon concealment trio ([signs.md: non-directional section](../signs.md)) — the same core found in Gathering Shadows. The trio makes the host object absorb all incoming light, causing it to vanish into shadow when used as a cover.

**Wind** is a manipulate-only element — it moves existing air rather than creating it ([sigils.md](../sigils.md), air family). The **dancing_puppet** sign causes objects near the seal to dart through the sky ([signs.md](../signs.md)), using the wind sigils as the elemental material those darting motions act on. Wind + Puppet is exactly the grammar of **Flying Puppet of Diversion** (canon: wind sigil + dancing_puppet), which Tetia used in ch. 6 to make a cloak decoy fly. The Cloak Spell embeds this directly into the same seal.

The **Windowway ring** is a wrapped-around spatial spell (see Windowway Spell). It turns the cloak into a two-location gateway. The wiki explicitly states this is a fusion of the three sub-spells; the outer ring "loops around the spell" as described by Euini in ch. 21.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Gathering Shadows** — the exact same vision+eye+bend composition used here for concealment; the Cloak Spell embeds it.
- **Flying Puppet of Diversion** — wind + dancing_puppet; the same wind-flight mechanism used here.
- **Windowway Spell** — the spatial-link ring component.

**Bottom line:** The Cloak Spell is an advanced compound contraption that merges three independently valid canon spells into one integrated seal on a garment. Each sub-spell is documented and works by its own grammar. The challenge is the fusion architecture: it requires the three effects to operate simultaneously on the same object (the cloak) without interference — a feat that marks Sasaran as a highly skilled witch.

## How to draw it
This is a contraption-class seal. In broad strokes:
1. Draw the **Vision sigil** at the center.
2. Place **Eye signs on 4 of the 8 Vision asterisk-points** (alternating), and **Wind sigils on the other 4 points**.
3. Surround the body with **Bend signs ×4** at 90° intervals and **dancing_puppet signs ×4** alternating with the bends around the outer ring.
4. Draw a complete **Windowway-ring** (bolt + region-type signs in paired opposing rings) enclosing the entire main body.
5. Maintain radial symmetry throughout for stability.
Note: "each windowway is unique" (wiki) — the spatial ring must be unique per pair to avoid crossing links.

## Usage ideas
- **Remote combat disguise:** send the animated shadow-cloak as a decoy while the true caster operates safely from cover.
- **Surveillance / remote spellcasting:** channel spells *through* the cloak's Windowway at a target location without being physically present.
- **Concealed delivery:** pass objects through the cloak from one place to another without anyone seeing the transfer.
- **Escape tool:** step through the cloak portal while the Puppet animation keeps the caster's apparent position elsewhere.

## Similar spells
- **Gathering Shadows** — the concealment sub-spell at the core.
- **Flying Puppet of Diversion** — the wind-flight sub-spell.
- **Windowway Spell** — the spatial-link sub-spell.
- **Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade** — another multi-function garment spell combining Gathering Shadows + Mirror Spell.

## Notes & limitations
- This is a **contraption** (multi-ring, multi-spell), significantly more complex than a single-ring seal. The wiki says it was "used by Sasaran" — advanced witch construction.
- The Windowway component requires a **paired second Windowway** at the destination; the spell cannot open a one-ended portal.
- Wind is manipulate-only — in still air or an enclosed room the flight motion depends on ambient air being present.
- The Vision + Eye + Bend concealment requires the host object to physically cover the caster; the spell doesn't project an illusion, it absorbs light hitting the cloak.
- The Puppet motion is described as "erratic" — the caster can control it remotely, but the movement style is not precisely aimed.
- Not forbidden: concealment and remote manipulation are permitted magic; no body effects.

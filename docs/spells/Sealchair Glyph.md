# Sealchair Glyph

The sealchair is a niche-type spell that allows the sealchair to move.

## Description
The spell is incredibly complex, and the signs it is composed of are too small to make out. It seems to be made of a main spell with 12 satellite spells surrounding it, four of them being large and the other eight being small. Out of the keystones that can be made out, one looks similar to the dancing puppet keystone seen in the flying puppet of diversion spell, though whether or not this is coincidence is not clear. Not much is known about this spell, but its function seems to be controlling the movement of the sealchair, a legged contraption that functions like a wheelchair and is used by the disabled.

## Usage
The spell is present on the sealchairs used by Beldaruit and Coustas, who use them to assist in their mobility due to the two of them being unable to walk. Despite being an unknowing, Coustas was allowed to use a sealchair during his recovery after his legs were broken in an accident. The glyph itself was seen was when Coustas was attacked by three thieves, who knocked over his sealchair and accidentally broke it, which put the glyph in clear view, only for Tartah to arrive and scare them off, then fix the chair with a powerful glue.

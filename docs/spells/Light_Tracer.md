# Light Tracer

> Sends a ribbon of light from one fragment of a broken object to all other fragments
> made from the same original — a living thread connecting shared-origin pieces.

## Overview
Light Tracer is a light-type spell invented by Richeh for her Silver Eve contraption entry: the
**Crystal Fragment Bangles** (ch. 46). When drawn on one fragment of a broken crystal object, the
spell sends a string of light to every other fragment of that same original object. This makes
the bangles glow with a light-ribbon connecting all pieces worn by different people — a visual
thread of shared origin. The wiki describes it as "having the same basis as the Boulder Stretch
Rope, but with a light sigil replacing the earth one, and an extra keystone at the top with an
unknown effect (assumed to make the connection between the rings)." The confirmed signs are
**Weave** (transmutes the light into a ribbon) and **Link** (connects the ribbon to all same-
origin fragments); the extra keystone is unidentified.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Richeh's party trick: draw this spell on a piece of crystal, and a strand of light reaches out
to every other piece of the same original crystal — even if the other pieces are worn as bangles
on different people across the room. It works because light is the substance (created from
nothing, so it is self-sufficient), Weave turns it into a ribbon shape, and Link is the sign that
specifically says "connect this magic to every object sharing the same origin." The result is a
glowing string that bridges the fragments — visible proof that the pieces were once one.

## Validity
**Valid.** Light sigil at center; Weave wraps it to give the ribbon form; Link connects the ribbon
to same-origin objects. All preconditions met: light is self-sufficient (create mode); Weave needs
a substance to transmute (light is the substance here — it becomes the ribbon, unlike Boulder
Stretch Rope where Weave acts on an external boulder); Link needs partner objects made from the
same original (the other bangles). The extra unknown keystone makes the full deduction incomplete.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** **Light** (`light`, fire family) — produces light. Capability **create**
  (self-sufficient; no external source needed). State **luminous** (sigils.md:29;
  substances.md, Light: "functions to produce magic that manifests in the form of light").
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Weave ×1** (`weave`, semi-directional, transmute) — one large sign wrapped around the
    core (`surrounds`). Turns the light into "a long, flexible ribbon" (signs-semi-directional.md,
    Weave). On a create-substance (light), unlike earth's Weave (which needs a real boulder fed
    in), the light conjures its own material and Weave immediately transmutes it into the ribbon
    form. Self-supplying. Not inverted.
  - **Link ×1** (`link`, semi-directional, special) — "links its magic to every object sharing
    the same origin" (signs-semi-directional.md, Link). This is the core mechanic: the ribbon of
    light extends to every other fragment made from the same crystal. The wiki + the lexicon
    confirm: "Light sigil + Weave + Link → a ribbon of light spanning every ring made from that
    crystal" (signs-semi-directional.md, Link Findings).
  - **Unknown keystone ×1** — the extra sign "at the top" (wiki). Its effect is "assumed to make
    the connection between the rings" (the bangles' ring mechanism). Not identified in the sign id
    map; the deduction is incomplete without it. Best guess: it may be a `bind` or `entwine`
    sign that anchors the ribbon to the receiving fragments, but this is speculation.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink (Richeh's specialty: crystal, but the spell itself is light).

## Deduced effect
A **self-generating light-ribbon that connects same-origin fragments**. The light sigil creates
the luminous material; Weave transmutes it into a flexible ribbon; Link propagates the ribbon to
every other piece that was once part of the same original object. When one bangle activates, all
bangles made from the same crystal glow together, connected by visible threads of light.

The deduction is **incomplete** because the extra keystone (ring-connection function) is
unidentified. Possible roles:
1. **Most likely — a binding/anchoring sign** (`bind` or `entwine`): fixes the end of the light
   ribbon to the receiving fragment so it actually attaches rather than just reaching toward it.
   This would explain the "connection between the rings" description.
2. **Possible — a convergence** (`convergence`): focuses the ribbon's light to a point at each
   receiving fragment, making the contact visible and bright. Less likely given the wiki's
   "connection between rings" phrasing.
3. **Long-shot — a signal/vision sign**: triggers a visual acknowledgment when the ribbon makes
   contact. Pure speculation; no canon support for this reading.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Light sigil | substance | Creates the luminous ribbon material; self-sufficient (no crystal source needed). Without it there is nothing to weave. |
| Weave ×1 | transmute | Shapes the light into a long, flexible ribbon. Without it the light would manifest raw (a glow/beam, not a ribbon). |
| Link ×1 | special | Propagates the ribbon to every same-origin fragment. The key mechanic — without it, Light + Weave gives only a ribbon at the drawing site, not a reaching thread. |
| Unknown keystone | unknown (connection) | Assumed to handle the endpoint attachment to the other rings/fragments. Without it, the ribbon may not anchor at the far end. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The wiki description — "same basis as the Boulder Stretch Rope, but with a light sigil replacing
the earth one" — is the key statement. The **Boulder Stretch Rope** (earth + Weave) transmutes a
physical boulder into a ribbon; it runs in earth's **manipulate** mode and needs a real boulder
fed in ("a loom that needs raw stone" — signs-semi-directional.md, Weave Findings).

Light Tracer substitutes **light** for **earth** — and this changes the capability entirely:
- Earth: **manipulate only** — cannot create stone; needs real material. The rope has "physical
  weight" (the boulder must be present).
- Light: **create** — conjures its own light with no source. The ribbon is self-generating.

This means Light Tracer is more like **Crystal Ribbon** (crystal + Weave, also self-supplying)
than Boulder Stretch Rope in terms of capability. The light ribbon materializes from nothing;
there is no "loom that needs raw stone." The cost is in the magical ink and the complexity of
the Link sign.

**Link on light:** per the lexicon (signs-semi-directional.md, Link): "Light sigil + Weave + Link
→ a ribbon of light spanning every ring made from that crystal." The mechanism is that **Link**
keys on shared origin — pieces carved from one crystal are bound by their origin. The ribbon
travels between them through this origin-link, not through physical space in any ordinary sense.
Only objects sharing the **same original** are affected; new crystals not carved from that source
do not receive the ribbon.

**Physical state + Weave:** Weave "turns solid objects into long, flexible ribbons on contact"
(signs-semi-directional.md). On a **luminous** substance, the "solid object" interpretation is
unusual. The best reading: Weave here transmutes the magical output into a ribbon *form* — the
light does not solidify, but it is constrained into a one-dimensional ribbon shape rather than
dispersing as glow. This matches the visual in the bangles (a glowing thread/string, not a beam
or cloud).

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Boulder Stretch Rope** — same Weave mechanic; differs in substance (earth, manipulate, needs
  feedstock) vs. light (create, self-supplying).
- **Crystal Ribbon** — same light-variant pattern (create-mode substance + Weave = self-supplying
  ribbon); no Link, so it doesn't bridge fragments.

**Bottom line:** Light Tracer is a self-sufficient ribbon of light that links same-origin
fragments. The Weave + light substance combination is clean (create mode → no feedstock needed);
the Link sign supplies the fragment-to-fragment bridge. The recipe is otherwise confirmed; only
the extra keystone for the ring connection remains unidentified.

## How to draw it
1. **Light sigil** at the center.
2. **Weave** (the large wrapped loop) **surrounding the core** — draw it large enough to encircle
   the light sigil, with the two small tails at the bottom. This is one sign, not a ring of them.
3. **Link** sign nearby (traditionally at the ring, facing inward) — this is what broadcasts the
   ribbon to the other fragments.
4. The **unknown keystone** "at the top" — draw it at the top of the seal; exact form unknown.
   A bind sign or entwine sign would be the best approximation if the real mark is not available.
5. Draw on each fragment of the broken crystal object. Only one fragment needs the seal active
   for all connected fragments to glow (or draw on each for mutual activation — canon suggests
   drawing on one fragment suffices, given the "split-shard bangles" description).

## Usage ideas
- **Friendship / communication tokens** — break one crystal into pieces, draw Light Tracer on
  each; all pieces glow when one is activated, signaling the others.
- **Emergency signals** — a pre-broken crystal set: one piece kept as a trigger, others distributed;
  activating the trigger sends a visible light-thread to all holders.
- **Artistic installation** (Richeh's canonical use) — Silver Eve: a set of glowing bangles linked
  by light ribbons, all derived from one original crystal.
- **Tracking / locating** — if you can see the ribbon's direction, you can locate the other
  fragments.

## Similar spells
- **Boulder Stretch Rope** — the earth-substance ancestor: same Weave mechanic; manipulate mode
  (needs a boulder), no Link, no fragment-bridging.
- **Crystal Ribbon** — crystal + Weave; self-supplying ribbon but without Link (no fragment
  bridging).
- **Bird of Light Beacon** — light shaped by a decorative sigil (bird form) rather than Weave.

## Notes & limitations
- The **extra unknown keystone** (ring connection) makes the full recipe incomplete. It is assumed
  to anchor the ribbon endpoint to the receiving fragment; exact id unknown.
- The spell uses a **crystal** as the physical medium (the bangle is crystal), but the **light
  sigil** is the magical substance — the crystal is just the object the seal is drawn on.
- Until ch. 55 (per wiki), neither the spell nor the bangles were used by anyone, not even Richeh;
  the spell's behavior is canonical but witnessed in only one context (the design presentation).
- Engine note: a single Weave sign may trigger "asymmetric/unstable" warning — that is the known
  single-Weave artifact (signs-semi-directional.md, Weave Findings). Override it.

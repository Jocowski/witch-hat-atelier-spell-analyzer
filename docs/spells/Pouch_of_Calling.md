# Pouch of Calling

> A two-seal contraption drawn on a pouch that echoes back a recorded phrase and can move autonomously to deliver its message — an enchanted voice-messenger.

## Overview
The Pouch of Calling (also called an Echopouch) is a contraption invented by Agott Arklaum as her contribution to the Sincerity of the Shield make-up test (ch. 34). It consists of a small pouch or piece of cloth with two seals drawn on it: a **seal of pursuit** (using the Guidance sigil) on the outside, and a **seal of recorded sound** (using the Calling sigil) on the inside. Words or phrases are spoken into the fabric, and the pouch echoes them back. The seal of pursuit allows the pouch to move autonomously — hopping toward a recipient and then luring them elsewhere. Items (like a Snugstone or smoke) can be placed inside to add further effects.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Draw two seals on a cloth pouch. Speak a message into the fabric — the Calling seal records it and plays it back on demand. The Guidance seal on the outside makes the pouch hop and move toward whoever it is meant to reach, then draws them toward a desired location. The pouch can also carry small items inside (like a Snugstone for warmth, or smoke for a signal), letting it deliver both a message and a payload. It is essentially a magical voice-memo that also walks itself to the recipient.

## Validity
**Valid** (individual seals). Each of the two component seals uses a documented sigil. The contraption as a whole is a standard two-seal device drawn on a physical object (the pouch/cloth).

- **Seal of recorded sound (inner):** Calling sigil — "echoes back a recorded phrase" ([sigils.md:217](../sigils.md)). A simple, sign-minimal seal; possibly needs no signs beyond the Calling sigil itself, since the sigil defines the full behavior.
- **Seal of pursuit (outer):** Guidance sigil — "attracts objects to its location that match parameters set by the other signs and sigils within a spell" ([sigils.md:211](../sigils.md)). Here parameterized to attract specific recipients (or other Pouches of Calling in the Valance Leech defense use-case).

The individual signs within each seal are **not documented** by the wiki — only the sigil identities are confirmed.

## Deduced effect
When a phrase is spoken into the pouch, the Calling seal records it and can play it back. The Guidance seal provides autonomous movement ("hopping motion") toward the intended recipient, then lures them to a different location. The pouch can optionally carry a payload inside (Snugstone for warmth/lifelikeness in the Valance Leech defense; smoke as a navigation signal in Beldaruit's private-use version).

**Two known uses in canon:**
1. **Ch. 34 — Sincerity of the Shield test:** The pouch delivers a message and lures Beldaruit to the final surprise. Beldaruit later repurposed it to lead Coco to his quarters via the smoke he placed inside.
2. **Ch. 81 — Valance Leech defense at Silver Eve:** Numerous pouches loaded with Snugstones were distributed; the Snugstones made them feel "lifelike" enough to distract the Valance Leeches away from town — implying the Guidance seal was parameterized to *attract* the leeches.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The **Calling sigil** is documented as record-then-replay: "echoes back a recorded phrase" ([sigils.md:217](../sigils.md)). It is **immaterial** — it stores a sound pattern, not a physical substance. Its capability is **collect-then-replay**: capture input, return it on demand. No external source beyond the spoken phrase is needed.

The **Guidance sigil** is a **parameterized attraction force**: "attracts objects to its location that match parameters set by the other signs and sigils" ([sigils.md:211](../sigils.md)). In the Pouch of Calling, the parameters are set to identify and pursue the recipient. In the Valance Leech defense variant, the Guidance parameter was presumably tuned to match leech-attracting scent/warmth (provided by the Snugstone payload). This is the same sigil the lexicon identifies as attracting "all nearby Pouches of Calling" when used in a Guidance Orb ([lexicon/substances.md](../lexicon/substances.md)).

**Closest canon analogue — Guidance Orb.** Per the Sigils_Explained text, "a guidance based spell that contains a Pouch of Calling spell will attract all nearby Pouches of Calling." This is the inverse relationship: a Guidance Orb (unknown construction) uses Guidance to summon all nearby Pouches; the Pouch itself uses Guidance for its own pursuit.

**Calling is unique** — it has no documented precedent for use outside the Pouch of Calling, and its exact interaction with the other signs is not described. Treat its behavior as entirely defined by the sigil's documented record-replay function.

**Bottom line.** A two-sigil contraption where one sigil records/replays sound (Calling) and the other enables directed pursuit (Guidance). The individual signs within each seal are undocumented; the sigil identities are wiki-confirmed. The contraption is feasible and internally consistent — both sigils have documented capabilities that together produce the described behavior.

## How to draw it
1. Obtain a small pouch or piece of cloth that will accept conjuring ink.
2. On the **outside** of the pouch: draw the **Guidance seal** — Guidance sigil at center, with additional signs to parameterize the pursuit behavior (signs unknown; likely directional or target signs).
3. On the **inside** of the pouch: draw the **Calling seal** — Calling sigil at center, with any needed supporting signs (possibly sign-minimal or sign-free).
4. Speak the desired message into the fabric while the Calling seal is active.
5. Optionally place items inside the pouch to add effects (warmth, signaling smoke, etc.).

## Usage ideas
- **Voice messenger:** record a short message and send the pouch to a recipient; it hops to them and plays back the message.
- **Lure or decoy:** load with a scent or warmth (Snugstone) and set the Guidance parameter to draw creatures or targets toward a desired location.
- **Emergency signal delivery:** send the pouch with smoke inside; the recipient follows the smoke trail to find the sender.
- **Autonomous delivery service:** the pouch can navigate on its own, making it useful for deliveries to known recipients in familiar environments.

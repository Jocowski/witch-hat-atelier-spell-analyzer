# Twin Bottle's Spell

The twin bottle spell is a forbidden spell able to teleport fluids from one place to another.

## Description
The twin bottle spell is a forbidden spell with an unknown glyph construction due to the signs and sigils being too small to make out. The spell allows for the transfer liquids from one place to another, and according to Qifrey, was used by the healing witches of old to transport medicines over long distances, before their magic was decreed forbidden on the Day of the Pact.

## Usage
The spell was used by Iguin, who placed it on two tiny coins which were placed in ink bottles, one of which he possessed, and the other in the possession of Coco. Iguin used the spell to give Coco a forbidden, more powerful ink from a remote location without her knowledge, the ink implied to contain their own blood.

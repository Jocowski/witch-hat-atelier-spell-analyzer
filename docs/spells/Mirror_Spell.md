# Mirror Spell

> A reflection spell that causes any creature looking at the wearer to see the wearer borrow the appearance of that creature — magic that mirrors back another's form.

## Overview
The Mirror Spell is a niche spell that appears as a component of the **Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade**, described by Euini in ch. 21. It causes whatever animal or creature gazes upon the wearer to have its appearance "borrowed" and reflected back — effectively a shape-borrowing illusion triggered by the act of being looked at. On its own its exact effect is unknown (the wiki notes this explicitly); it is only defined in combination with Gathering Shadows, which adds the shadow-concealment component. The spell is also notable for its unusual architecture: a **main circle with 4 satellite circles** linked to it.

## In plain terms (sum up)
This is a "mirror" that doesn't show your face — it shows the face of whoever is looking at you. Written on a cloak, it lets you borrow the appearance of any animal that gazes upon you. The apprentices used it during a test in a cave to blend in among the creatures there. Euini scratched it out of his own cloak, leaving only the shadow-concealing part, which is what let him draw in secret. By itself, the Mirror Spell's full behavior is not confirmed by canon; what is known is that it handles the appearance-borrowing half of the borrowshade cloak.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The spell belongs to the **vision/illusion** family, catalogued as a "niche" spell and functioning in a cloak alongside Gathering Shadows — both are perception/light-manipulation effects. It does not create or move a physical substance; its domain is **appearance** (a perceived quality, like the `vision_sigil` / `eye` / `bend` concealment trio in Gathering Shadows). The appearance it borrows is an animal's form, which implies the spell reads and mirrors a visual input — a more complex operation than simple light absorption.

The **satellite architecture** is unusual and mirrors the structure of the Windowway Spell (which also uses an outer ring linked to the main body). In WHA magic, linked circles can distribute, amplify, or specialize an effect. Here, four satellites suggest the reflection needs to work from all directions (creatures may look from any angle), so each satellite is an angular anchor.

**Closest canon analogue:** Gathering Shadows — both are drawn on the same cloaks, both manipulate light/appearance, and both feature a Vision-type core. The difference is Gathering Shadows *absorbs* light (you disappear into shadow) while Mirror Spell *reflects/borrows* appearance (you look like whoever is looking at you). One is a nullifier; the other is an active mirroring system.

**Bottom line:** Mirror Spell is a perception/appearance spell whose exact mechanism cannot be deduced from available canon data — its constituent signs are currently unidentified. It is certain it handles the appearance-borrowing half of the Borrowshade Cloak and uses a multi-satellite contraption architecture; everything below the "what signs make that happen" level is hypothesis until the hourglass and bracket marks are catalogued.

## Usage ideas
- **Creature camouflage:** worn in a cave or forest, the cloak takes on the appearance of the local fauna, allowing the wearer to move among animals undetected.
- **Interrogation deterrent:** an observer who gazes at the wearer sees only themselves or their own species reflected back — deeply disorienting.
- **Combined with Gathering Shadows:** the full Borrowshade Cloak combines shadow-absorption with appearance-borrowing; together the wearer is both invisible in shadow and, when visible, appears to be the creature looking at them.

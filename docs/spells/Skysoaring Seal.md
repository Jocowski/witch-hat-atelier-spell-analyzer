# Skysoaring Seal

Skysoaring seal is a wind-type spell that can allow things to fly when drawn large enough.

## Description
The skysoaring seal contains a wind sigil located at the bottom of the glyph with a levitation sign spanning from just above the sigil to the opposite side of the glyph. The spell produces thrust in the direction of it's keystone, and when drawn on will something large such as a sail, can even allow that object to fly.

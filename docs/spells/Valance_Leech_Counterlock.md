# Valance Leech Counterlock

> A Counterclock spell with a Valance Leech decorative sigil added to its center, scoping the time-reversal specifically to valance leeches — resetting them to the physical state they were in 24 hours prior.

## Overview
The Valance Leech Counterlock is a custom variant of the Counterclock family, designed collaboratively by Agott, Lagrah, Beldaruit, and others (conceived in chapter 78, first cast in chapter 84). It is a standard Counterclock geometric seal with a **Valance Leech decorative sigil** (`valance_leech`) added at its center. This scoping addition causes the time-reversal to apply specifically to valance leeches, reverting them to their physical state 24 hours prior — the same window as a standard Counterclock. The spell was created to neutralize a catastrophic monstrous valance leech that had grown to near-city size by consuming magically-altered blood during the Silver Eve Arc. By tiling many instances of the spell across circular floatforms and enclosing the creature, the team was able to scale the effect large enough to fully revert the leech to its pre-monstrous state.

## In plain terms (sum up)
A Counterclock seal — which normally reverses damage and change on anything it is drawn on — with a Valance Leech sigil placed at the center to tell it to only affect valance leeches. When cast, it rolls the leech back to how it was 24 hours before the spell was drawn, then holds that reverted state for up to another 24 hours. After the spell expires, the leech resumes normal time again (not reverting further — just going forward from the reverted state). The crucial application: any blood the leech had absorbed is transferred back into the original bodies. The team used dozens of spell-bearing floatforms strung together to create a spell large enough to encompass a leech that had grown to nearly the size of the city of Ezrest. This is the only documented example of creating a massive-scale multi-surface spell via tiled floatforms.

## Validity
**Valid, with the same caveat as Counterclock.** The decorative sigil adds a scoping target to the Counterclock base. The Counterclock component has unidentified signs (see Counterclock doc); the Valance Leech sigil at center is a recognized id. The engine would identify the decorative sigil (`valance_leech`, element `decorative`) as the core, but the actual time-reversal substance comes from the Counterclock geometry surrounding it. This is a known gap in the engine model for this spell family.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):**
  - **Valance Leech** (`valance_leech`, element *decorative*) — at the center of the Counterclock pattern. Causes "magic to manifest in the shape of a valance leech" (sigils.md:187) in normal use; here it functions as the **target scoper**, telling the Counterclock effect to apply specifically to valance leeches rather than to anything touched.
  - **Counterclock geometric base** — the six-sided star geometry of straight lines that provides the time-reversal substance (see Counterclock doc). The Valance Leech sigil sits at the center of this pattern.
- **Form (signs):** Same unidentified sign set as Counterclock (straight-line geometric shapes forming a six-sided star). Not individually mapped.
- **Ring:** closed/active; the six-sided star geometry acts as the ring structure. The **Old Version** (pre-Volume 14) used the full six inner triangular sections; the **New Version** (Volume 14+) removed 3 of the 6 inner triangular sections (the same retcon as applied to all Counterclock spells).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
The spell reverses the physical state of targeted valance leeches back to 24 hours prior to when the spell was cast. This means:
- Any growth (mass, size) that occurred in the past 24 hours is undone.
- Any blood or material consumed is returned to its sources.
- The leech reverts to its pre-event physical configuration.
- The reversal holds for up to 24 hours, then the spell expires and normal time resumes from the reverted state.

In the Silver Eve Arc application: the leech had consumed blood from magically-altered humans, growing to near-city scale. The 24-hour reversal window was sufficient to predate the catastrophic growth event. After reversal, "all the blood it had consumed [was] transferred back into the bodies of those it had drank from" — the wiki notes this worked because from the spell's perspective, the liquid had merely moved from one container to another and hadn't technically *changed*, allowing it to remain in the reverted human bodies even after the spell wore off.

The scoping mechanic (how the decorative sigil narrows the Counterclock's target) parallels the documented principle for decorative sigils generally: they can "make a spell affect the living thing the sigil corresponds to" (sigils.md:113). Here, the Valance Leech sigil is doing exactly that — instead of creating a leech-shaped manifestation, it scopes the Counterclock effect to *act on* a valance leech.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Valance Leech sigil | target scoper (decorative) | Without it, the Counterclock would revert anything it touches; with it, only valance leeches are affected |
| Counterclock geometry | time-reversal substance | Provides the 24-hour-window reversal mechanism; without it there is no time effect |
| Radial six-sided star symmetry | stability | Same structural stability as all Counterclock seals |
| Tiled floatforms (Silver Eve application) | scale amplifier | Tiles many instances to enclose a huge target; the individual spell is normal-sized |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Valance Leech sigil is a **decorative sigil** — normally these "create magic in the shape of that living thing" (sigils.md:113). When placed at the center of a Counterclock, it instead performs the third documented use of decorative sigils: "make a spell affect the living thing the sigil corresponds to." This is the same scoping use seen in how the Horse sigil makes a water spell take the form of and pull loads *as* a horse. The Valance Leech sigil here narrows the Counterclock to be a *valance-leech-specific* time-reversal rather than a generic one.

This narrowing is important: an unscoped Counterclock drawn over a leech would also revert the humans nearby, the ground, the floatforms, and anything else within the seal's reach — potentially catastrophic and uncontrolled. The scoping sigil is what makes the spell surgically applicable.

The 24-hour reversal window is inherited from the base Counterclock. The wiki notes the two-version history: Old Version had the full six inner triangle sections and used the pre-Volume 14 Counterclock design; New Version uses the simplified (3-section-removed) Volume 14 design. Both function identically.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Counterclock** — the base spell; same reversal mechanism without the scoping sigil.
- **Water Horse** — another two-sigil composition where a decorative sigil (Horse) modifies the behavior of a primary substance sigil (Water); same pattern of "decorative sigil scopes/shapes the effect."
- **Repetition Seal** — sister time spell, continuous reset rather than temporary reversal.

**Bottom line:** A well-designed specialist variant of Counterclock that elegantly solves the scoping problem by adding one decorative sigil. The mechanism is clearly reasoned from documented principles. The only unresolved part is the Counterclock sign set itself (shared with all Counterclock spells). The Silver Eve Arc application demonstrates that this spell can be scaled arbitrarily by tiling — the individual seal is normal but the coverage is only limited by how many floatforms and hands you have.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Counterclock six-sided star** geometric pattern (straight lines forming geometric shapes in a six-pointed star).
2. Place the **Valance Leech sigil** at the center of the star instead of leaving the center empty (or in place of whatever center mark the standard Counterclock uses).
3. Use the **New Version** design (Volume 14+): 3 of the 6 inner triangular sections of the star are absent compared to the Old Version.
4. Draw directly on a surface that will contact or enclose the target leech (the Silver Eve Arc used circular floatforms).
5. **Tiling:** for a very large target, many sealed floatforms can be strung together to form one enormous effective seal — the individual seals must be close enough together that their combined field encloses the target.

## Usage ideas
- Emergency neutralization of magically-augmented valance leeches.
- Precautionary de-aging of any leech that has consumed too much recently.
- The tiling technique is generalizable: any Counterclock (or Repetition Seal) can in principle be tiled for scale, not just this variant.
- Research: because the blood was returned even after spell expiry, the wiki suggests the mechanism preserved the liquid's identity — this could have implications for studying magical transformation reversal.

## Similar spells
- **Counterclock** — base spell; this is a scoped variant.
- **Repetition Seal** — same family (time), continuous rather than temporary reversal.
- **Water Horse** — similar two-sigil structure (decorative sigil scopes a primary substance).

## Notes & limitations
- The Counterclock component shares all the limitations of Counterclock: 24-hour max window, temporary, requires redrawing to extend, and is forbidden when applied to human bodies (this variant targets leeches, not humans — so the human-body prohibition does not apply here).
- The article title on the wiki is "Valance Leech Counterlock" (not Counterclock); keeping the wiki spelling in the file name.
- The Old Version was **retconned** (magazine → Volume 14+); both versions function the same, but new drawings should use the simplified New Version.
- The wiki describes the leech's recombination with the human it consumed as possibly dangerous if physical barriers eventually fail — the spell does not permanently solve the separation, just resets the leech.

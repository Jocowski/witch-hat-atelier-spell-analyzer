# Rainbringer Seal

Rainbringer seal is a water-type spell which generates rain.

## Description
The rainbringer seal consists of a central water sigil with a rain sign surrounding it. When drawn, it creates a shower of rain.

## Usage
This spell was never actually seen being used by anyone. However, it is known that this spell is one that Qifrey's aprentices had commited to memory before the events that transpired within the Serpentback Cave. The rainbringer seal was seen in a mural of all the spells that the aprentices had in their arsenal when they were discussing a way to potentially turn Euini back into a human after he was afflicted with the scalewolf curse.

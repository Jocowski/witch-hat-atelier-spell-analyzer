# Cloak Spell

The cloak spell is a mixed spell that allows the user to preform magic remotley through the cloak, and control the cloak's movment from afar.

## Description
The cloak spell contains a central body and a windowway ring that loops around the spell. The spell's main body contains a central vision sigil with eye signs placed on 4 of the eight ends of the vision sigil, with wind sigils on the other 4. Along the ring of the glyph, 4 bend and 4 puppett keystones are placed.[1] This spell was used by Sasaran on his cloak, and it functioned to allow him to control the cloak remotley while preforming spells through it, hiding his main body elsewhere.[2] The cloak spell is a fusion of the gathering shadows spell, which drapes the object in shaodw, the flying puppet of diversion spell, which makes the object fly through the air, and the windowway spell, which allows objects to pass through it from one location to another.

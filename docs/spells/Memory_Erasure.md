# Memory Erasure

> A body-affecting niche spell sanctioned by the Day of the Pact that erases magical knowledge (and in its full form, all memories connected to magic) from the mind — the only kind of magic permitted to act directly on the human body.

## Overview
The Memory Erasure spell is uniquely exceptional in the WHA magic system: it is the **only spell permitted to act directly on the human body** under the Day of the Pact, because its use protects the secrecy of magic from non-witches and punishes those who break magical law ([forbidden-magic.md:7](../forbidden-magic.md)). It is wielded primarily by the Knights Moralis. The sigil at the center is described as **concentric circles with a dot in the middle**. Glaives protrude from the bottom of the seal. Dot-shaped signs ring the outer edge and top. Multiple variants exist with minor additions — for example, Qifrey's version (targeted to specific secrets) adds arrows along the side, removes the square-ring, and has a disconnected inner circle. The spells are drawn white on black rather than the usual dark-on-light, suggesting a different ink or surface type.

## In plain terms (sum up)
The Memory Erasure spell is essentially a memory wipe targeted at magic-related knowledge. In its full form, it strips everything connected to magic from a person's mind — which, for a lifelong witch, can erase almost their entire life including their own name. Modified versions can target a specific time period, or specific topics and people, leaving other memories intact. It's the one spell in the entire system that is officially allowed to work directly on the human body, because the Pact deemed protecting magic's secrecy worth this exception. The spell reaches out toward the target's face to take effect — shown visually as extending "arms" in the anime.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):** The central sigil — concentric circles with a dot in the middle — does not match any named sigil in the current catalog. It may be a unique niche sigil specific to memory/mind manipulation, or an `unknown_sigil` variant. The concentric circles visually suggest layers or depth (memories); the central dot suggests a focal point.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Glaives** — confirmed ("several glaives protrude from the bottom of the spell"). Glaives are documented as determining "how deeply magic will embed itself into an animal's flesh" ([signs_explained:266]); in the Memory Erasure, they control depth of magical penetration into the mind/body.
  - **Dot-shaped signs** — ring the outer edge and top; function unclear; likely orientation or targeting signs that define the outer scope of the erasure.
  - **Arrows (Qifrey's variant only)** — appear along the side; likely region or directional signs that narrow the targeting to specific memories rather than a blanket wipe.
  - Additional unknown signs account for the actual memory-access and erasure mechanics.
- **Ring:** Visible ring structure (the dot-signs are positioned "along the outer edge and top"); the concentric-circle sigil itself may provide the ring structure visually.
- **Ink / dyes:** White ink on a black background — the reverse of normal conjuring ink. This may represent a special Knights Moralis variant ink or a specialized quire medium.

## Deduced effect
Confirmed effect range (three documented variants):
1. **Full erasure:** All memories relating to magic are wiped, including ordinary life memories connected to magic (for witches, this means nearly everything — their own name, loved ones).
2. **Time-period targeting:** Erases memories from a specific period of time.
3. **Topic/person targeting:** Erases memories of specific subjects regardless of when they occurred.

The glaives are the key mechanistic element. Their documented purpose — controlling how deeply magic embeds into flesh — means they regulate the **depth of penetration** into the target's mind. More glaives or longer glaives would achieve deeper erasure; fewer or shorter glaives might produce the selective variants. This is consistent with Qifrey's modified version having different glaive-adjacent geometry.

Ranked hypotheses for the full mechanism:

1. **Most likely — glaives as depth-regulators + unknown sigil as mind/memory substance:** The unknown central sigil represents a non-elemental substance (mind/memory) that only this spell type works with. The glaives determine how deep the magic reaches into this substance (deep = full wipe; shallow = selective). The dot-signs define the target scope (all magic knowledge, or a narrower range). The white-on-black ink is the material difference — either a different conjuring ink formulated for mind-substance, or the black quire is the spell's "stage."

2. **Possible — vision-class sigil with memory-reach mechanic:** The concentric circles might be a variant of the `vision_sigil` (which relates to perception and illusion in the system). Paired with glaives and projection, it could "read" and then "erase" the mental record of magical knowledge, similar to how vision-class spells affect what is perceived or concealed.

3. **Possible — a niche sigil representing human-knowledge substrate:** The concentric-circle-dot design is unique and may represent an abstract substrate — the "accumulated layers of knowledge" (concentric circles = layers; dot = the self at the center). This would be a metaphorical sigil akin to the guidance/calling sigils, but for human consciousness rather than physical attraction or sound.

4. **Long-shot — linked pair spell with the spell-quire as the second half:** The white-on-black appearance and the "reaching toward the face" behavior suggests the spell actively seeks the target. A `link` component could connect the inscribed quire (the spell's source) to the target's body once it comes into proximity, with the glaives driving in once contact is made.

*The mind-substance and the erasure mechanism are not explained by the documented system components; this spell operates in territory the standard four elements do not cover.*

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Memory Erasure spell is exceptional by every system principle in CORE.md. It:

1. **Acts directly on the human body** — the one thing the Day of the Pact forbids, uniquely permitted for this spell ([forbidden-magic.md:7](../forbidden-magic.md)).
2. **Uses an unclassified substance** — mind and memory are not among the four elements, crystal, time, or any documented sigil substance. The central sigil must represent a unique niche substance for human consciousness.
3. **Uses glaives** — a sign type pre-dating the Pact, now rare, documented only in the Memory Erasure variants and the Slime Rendering Seal; glaives function specifically at the boundary between magic and living flesh.

The white-on-black presentation is the most visually distinctive property. In the standard system, conjuring ink is dark on light surfaces. The reversal could mean: a specially formulated Knight's ink, a particular quire (black quire as the "medium of authority"), or a sign-reversal that inverts all properties. Given the Knights Moralis' institutional role as the upholders of the Pact, their spell quires are likely standardized and regulated — the black format may be both a physical specification and a visual identifier that makes the spell unmistakably official.

Glaives date from before the Day of the Pact, which contextualizes the Memory Erasure as an **old magic technique** preserved specifically for this authorized use. The sign is described as possibly not even classified as a "sign" by some criteria (Engendale's comments per the wiki), suggesting it operates on a deeper or different level than the standard sign taxonomy.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Repetition Seal** — resets objects to a prior state; Memory Erasure resets the mind to a prior state (before knowing magic); the mechanism is conceptually parallel.
- **Vision-class spells** (Gathering Shadows, Makeover Mask) — affect perception and visual appearance; Memory Erasure affects the mental record rather than the sensory surface, but both operate on the "what is perceived/known" layer.

**Bottom line:** The Memory Erasure is a uniquely sanctioned body-affecting spell built around an undocumented mind-substance sigil and pre-Pact glaive signs. It is the system's clearest example of a spell that operates outside the standard four-element framework. Glaives are the key differentiator between deep (full wipe) and shallow (selective) erasure. The white-on-black ink marks it as institutionally special, not a normal conjuring-ink spell.

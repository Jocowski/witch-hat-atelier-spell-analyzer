# Capture Pennant Spell

> A two-ring contraption etched onto the Silver-White Capture Pennants of the Knights Moralis — it lets a knight mentally target someone, wraps the banner around them, and holds the banner in its original shape so it resists damage and bending.

## Overview
The Capture Pennant Spell is a multi-ring time-type contraption inscribed permanently on the Knights Moralis's cloth capture pennants. The inner ring centers on a **Repetition sigil** (acting as sigil, not sign) that continuously resets the pennant to its original form — preventing it from being bent, torn, or deformed. Straddling the inner and outer rings are **Sights Set** signs (mental targeting), **Entwine** signs (wrapping around the target), and **Strengthen** signs attached to some Entwine signs as modifiers that make the banner more durable. Together the spell makes a banner that a knight can aim with their mind, command to wrap around a person or object, and that will hold together under stress.

## In plain terms (sum up)
A knight carries a special pennant. When activated, the knight uses their mind to pick a target — the Sights Set signs handle that mental link. The banner then coils itself around whatever is targeted, thanks to the Entwine signs. Strengthen signs reinforced to some Entwine marks make the banner harder to break or shake off. Meanwhile the Repetition sigil at the heart of the seal continuously resets the banner to its original flat shape — so even when wrapping tightly or taking punishment, it bounces back instead of tearing or warping. The spell was first seen in use on Volume 9's cover; the complete design was documented in Volume 12. It is used by the Knights Moralis to restrain criminals, injured persons, and objects.

## Validity
**Valid.** The inner ring has a Repetition sigil as core (a valid `canBeCenter` sign-as-sigil), supplying the time substance. Signs straddle both rings. The Sights Set signs (directional-like, targeting) and Entwine signs (semi-directional, coiling/wrapping) form the action component; Strengthen signs on Entwine ends act as power modifiers. No blocking issues: Repetition at center covers the "needs a core" rule, and all signs have documented roles. The contraption structure (inner + outer ring) matches known multi-ring spells.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):** Repetition (`repetition_sigil`, element *time*) at center of the inner ring — "continuously resetting time for the objects it affects to the state they had when the spell first affected them … like a spring" (sigils.md:99). Here it resets the pennant fabric to its original state, making it elastic and damage-resistant rather than brittle.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Sights Set ×N** (directional-like / target) — "allows a knight to target a specific object or person for their capture pennants to wrap around" (signs.md:167); the mental-targeting interface of the spell.
  - **Entwine ×N** (semi-directional / form) — "make an object it is drawn on … wrap or 'entwine' itself around other objects" (signs.md:177); the coiling action.
  - **Strengthen ×N** (semi-directional / power) — attached to the ends of some Entwine signs; "makes objects stronger and more durable" (signs.md:157); reinforces the banner against being torn or pulled off.
- **Ring:** closed inner ring (active); outer ring frames the full contraption.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink (likely pre-inscribed with standard conjuring ink by a witch).

## Deduced effect
When the pennant's outer ring is activated, the full contraption runs: the knight selects a target via Sights Set (mental aim), Entwine causes the banner fabric to coil and wrap itself around that target, Strengthen-modified Entwine sections make the wrapped grip hard to break, and the central Repetition sigil continuously resets the pennant to its original shape — making it resistant to bending, tearing, or deformation during the struggle. The time-reset mechanic is what makes the pennant feel like a rigid-yet-living ribbon: it always wants to spring back to flat, but the Entwine signs keep driving it to wrap.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Repetition sigil | sigil / core — time substance | Keeps resetting the banner to its original form; without it the pennant would deform and tear under use |
| Sights Set ×N | sign — target (mental aim) | Lets the knight choose who/what to wrap without physical aim; without this the Entwine would be undirected |
| Entwine ×N | sign — form (coil/wrap) | The action that actually wraps the banner; without it the spell resets cloth but never restrains anyone |
| Strengthen ×N (on Entwine ends) | sign — power (durability) | Toughens the wrapped grip and the banner itself; without it the banner is more easily torn free |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Repetition sigil's substance is **time** — specifically, a continuous state-reset: it "reset[s] objects to the state they had when the spell first affected them" (sigils.md:99). Applied to cloth, this keeps the pennant in an elastic, undamaged state no matter what it endures. This is the same mechanic used in the **Serpent's Bed of Sand**, where a Repetition wrap prevents a soft sand cloud from being crushed, and the **Cookpot Lid**, where it keeps food fresh. The Capture Pennant adds an *action layer* on top: where those other spells use Repetition purely to preserve, this one also drives the pennant to *do something* (wrap around a target) before restoring it.

Sights Set is a directional-like targeting sign — probably the clearest example in the system of a sign that creates a **mental interface** rather than a physical direction, similar to Puppet (signs.md:299). Entwine's mechanic (signs.md:177) is documented: it makes the object on which it is drawn coil around other objects. Strengthen (signs.md:157) — officially the "Sign of Empowerment" (World Guide 2026) — makes the result more durable, which here translates to grip strength and tear resistance.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Repetition Seal** — uses the same Repetition sigil core for state-preservation, but without the action/targeting layer; simpler and less directed.
- **Serpent's Bed of Sand** — uses Repetition as a persistence wrap to hold a soft substrate intact, same mechanism as the pennant's damage resistance.
- **Flying Puppet of Diversion** — another object that a user controls with their mind (Puppet sign); Sights Set in the Capture Pennant is the targeting-focused analogue.

**Bottom line:** A well-designed two-ring contraption that cleanly separates the time/preservation layer (Repetition core) from the action/targeting layer (Sights Set + Entwine + Strengthen). The Repetition sigil is doing exactly what it always does — spring-reset the object — while the outer-ring signs drive the behavior. The recipe is medium-confidence because the exact sign counts and layout straddle the two rings in a way that was not shown in detail; but the sign identities are confirmed by the wiki description.

## How to draw it
1. **Inner ring:** place the **Repetition sigil** at center (the spiral-coil glyph, acting as the core substance).
2. **Straddling signs:** distribute **Sights Set**, **Entwine**, and **Strengthen** signs so they straddle the boundary between the inner and outer rings — the wiki says these run across both rings, not confined to one.
3. **Strengthen placement:** attach Strengthen signs to the ends of (some of) the Entwine signs rather than placing them independently.
4. Keep the ring layout radially symmetric so the pennant resets evenly; the Sights Set signs likely cluster on one axis to give a directional targeting arc.
5. Both rings should be closed and active simultaneously on the finished pennant.

## Usage ideas
- Standard capture and restraint tool of the Knights Moralis — wrap around a criminal or escaping person with a mental command, hold them in place with a grip that is hard to break.
- Rescue: wrap around an injured person to immobilize them for transport without their voluntary cooperation.
- Object containment: wrap around loose or dangerous cargo to lash it in place.
- The Repetition sigil alone would make the pennant useful as a self-repairing bandage or wrap — separate from the capture function.

## Similar spells
- **Repetition Seal** — same time substance/core, no action layer; purely preservative.
- **Serpent's Bed of Sand** — same Repetition preservation mechanic applied to a soft substrate.
- **Flying Puppet of Diversion** — another mind-controlled object-spell; Puppet plays the role of Sights Set there.

## Notes & limitations
- **Two-ring contraption:** this spell is a contraption; the wha-spell@1 format (single circle) cannot fully represent it. The JSON below encodes only the inner ring with the core sigil and representative signs.
- The exact number of Sights Set, Entwine, and Strengthen signs is not given in the wiki; counts and layout are inferred from the description.
- Although Repetition is a time sigil, the spell itself is not directly temporal on a *target* — it only keeps the pennant in its original form (acts on the pennant, not on time around the target).
- Not forbidden: no effect on the human body — the pennant wraps around the body externally, which is physical restraint, not magic applied to the body.

# Illusory Labyrinth

Illusory labyrinth is a forbidden spell that creates a labyrinthine area that loops infinitely, making it impossible to escape.

## Description
The spell contains the same central sigil as the scalewolf curse, with column and directional signs, the remaining signs and sigils all being unidentified. The spell creates a grand underground labyrinth within which even space is distorted, as if you walk to far you will show up right where you started.

## Usage
The spell is responsible for the formation of the underground labyrinth, which Iguin trapped all of Qifrey's Apprentices inside of. The glyph was guarded by a dragon, which the apprentices had to get past using the serpent's bed of sand spell, after which they destroyed the glyph and were extracted by Qifrey from the labyrinth just in time before it collapsed.

# Integration

Integration (unofficial name) is a earth-type spell that puts powder back together.

## Description
Integration contains a central earth sigil surrounded by several inverted (backwards) crushing keystones. It can take the particles (powdered or smashed) of an object and temporarily rearrange them to mimic the shape of the object the particles originally came from, allowing for easy identification of an unknown powder.

Because the crush keystones are inverted, the effect is the inverse of what it typically would be. The integration spell will take any solid particles (powdered or smashed) within it's range and temporarily rearrange them to mimic the shape of their original object.

## Usage
Integration was first used by Tartah to identify powdered medical herbs when Coco was sick. The herbs were in unlabeled vials, and as Tartah had silverwash (a form of complete color blindness), he was unable to identify them by traditional means. He and Coco then worked together to create the spell, which allowed Tartah to correctly identify the correct herb to treat Coco's fever.

# Beldaruit's Smoke Sculpture

> A complex smoke spell that conjures a large smoke dragon capable of flying through the air and roaring — used by Beldaruit as an atmospheric demonstration during his private conversation with Coco.

## Overview
Beldaruit's Smoke Sculpture is a spell of "unknown type" (per the wiki's classification) cast by Beldaruit in ch. 35 during his private conversation with Coco. He drew the glyph on a flat, circular, floating platform in the air. The spell created a dragon-shaped smoke sculpture capable of flight and vocalization (roaring). The wiki notes it is "very complex, utilizing several different signs and sigils" — meaning the exact composition is not documented in detail, but we can reason it from the known components listed in the Signs_Explained article.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Beldaruit draws a complex seal and a dragon made of smoke rises into the air, flies around, and roars. It is like the Smokesculpting Seal taken to its grand extreme: instead of a small steam figure above a teacup, it produces a full flying smoke dragon. The spell uses a Smoke sigil for the material, Levitation to make it fly, and Column signs to give the smoke form and projection. The dragon shape is provided by a decorative Dragon sigil.

## Validity
**Valid** for the identified components (Smoke core + Levitation + Column). The Smoke sigil provides a `create`-mode substance so no external source is needed. Levitation on smoke/gaseous matter is consistent with documented behavior (Levitation on fire/water/light lifts the effect; smoke as a gaseous substance should behave similarly — it levitates the magical output). The spell is described as "very complex" and likely contains additional unidentified signs beyond those listed in Signs_Explained.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):**
  - **Smoke** (`smoke`) — creates and generates smoke ([sigils.md:205](../sigils.md)). Capability **create/generate**; manipulation unconfirmed but the spell's shaping behavior suggests either additional signs or a shape-imposing sigil handle the sculpting.
  - **Dragon** (`dragon`) (probable secondary sigil) — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of a dragon" ([sigils.md:133](../sigils.md)). Decorative sigil imposing a dragon form on the smoke substance. The wiki says the spell forms "a fake dragon out of smoke" — the Dragon sigil is the most direct way to impose that shape.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Levitation ×4** (directional, motion) — levitates the smoke sculpture into the air and keeps it aloft. For fire/water/light, Levitation "causes the spell's effect, as well as objects held overtop the seal, to levitate in the air" ([signs.md:137](../signs.md)). Applied to smoke (a gaseous substance analogous to fire in behavior), Levitation lifts the smoke construct vertically.
  - **Column ×4** (directional, form) — "causes a spell to manifest in a column or beam above the seal" ([signs.md:119](../signs.md)). Gives the smoke construct vertical height and form. Listed in Signs_Explained under "Spells Using Column."
  - **Additional unknown signs** — "very complex, utilizing several different signs and sigils." Beyond Levitation and Column, the full sign set is undocumented. The roaring capability in particular may require additional signs not yet identified.
- **Ring:** closed (drawn on a floating circular platform).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
The Smoke sigil generates smoke; the Dragon sigil shapes the smoke into a dragon form; Column gives it vertical projection and body height; Levitation lifts the smoke dragon into the air where it flies and maneuvers. The roaring capability is an additional effect not explained by the identified signs — most likely an additional unknown sign or a Calling-like sound element.

The "flying" behavior: Levitation lifts the smoke construct; additional directional signs (possibly more Column or unknown signs) allow it to move laterally. Alternatively, the Dragon decorative sigil may imbue some autonomous flight behavior (like Bird_A, which "will fly around for a while" once created).

### Hypotheses for the roaring and flight
**Hypothesis 1 (most likely — roaring): A Calling or sound-related sign.**
The roar could come from a Calling sigil used as a secondary element encoding a recorded roar sound. Beldaruit may have pre-recorded the roar into the spell's composition. *What would confirm it:* the Calling sigil appearing as a secondary component, or any other sign with a documented sound effect.

**Hypothesis 2 (possible — roaring): Autonomous behavior from the Dragon sigil.**
The Dragon decorative sigil, like Bird_A which "fly around for a while," may intrinsically confer some behaviors associated with dragons (including vocalization). *What would confirm it:* other Dragon-sigil spells showing autonomous behaviors.

**Hypothesis 3 (most likely — flight): Bird_A's precedent applied to Dragon.**
Bird_A "creates a bird-like projection made from the seal's magic which will fly around for a while" without additional motion signs. The Dragon sigil may confer the same autonomous flying behavior. If so, Levitation provides the initial lift and the Dragon shape's autonomous behavior handles maneuvering. *What would confirm it:* the Dragon sigil used in other non-directed flying contexts.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The **Smoke sigil** creates gaseous smoke without external source ([sigils.md:205](../sigils.md)) — the spell is self-sufficient. Applied to a **Dragon decorative sigil**, the smoke takes dragon form. This parallels how the Water Horse spell (water + horse sigil = water horse) and Qifrey's Water Dragon (water + dragon sigil = water dragon) use a decorative sigil over an elemental one to impose a creature shape on the generated substance.

**Levitation** on smoke (gaseous state, similar to fire in behavior) lifts the effect aloft. The Signs_Explained listing of Levitation spells explicitly includes "Beldaruit's Smoke Sculpture," confirming Levitation is a component. Columns are similarly listed. Both are therefore **confirmed components**, not hypotheses.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Smokesculpting Seal** — also uses Smoke sigil to create shaped smoke; but that spell is a small decorative teacup sculpture whereas Beldaruit's is a large, flying, living-seeming construct.
- **Qifrey's Water Dragon** — the most similar in ambition: water + dragon sigil + multiple signs = a flying water dragon. The composition likely parallels this, with smoke replacing water.
- **Bird of Light Beacon** — light + Bird_A sigil = a flying light projection; the same decorative-sigil-as-autonomous-flyer mechanic probably applies to Dragon.

**Bottom line.** A high-mastery smoke-dragon spell: Smoke (substance) + Dragon (shape) + Levitation (flight) + Column (body projection) + unknown additional signs (roar, fine control). The identified components (Smoke, Levitation, Column) are canon-confirmed by Signs_Explained. The Dragon sigil is the most plausible shape-giver. The roaring capability and the full complexity are unexplained by these known parts — the spell has additional unidentified signs. Confidence in the core composition (Smoke + Levitation + Column + Dragon) is **medium**; the full recipe is **low confidence** due to the documented complexity and unknown signs.

## How to draw it
1. **Smoke sigil** at the center.
2. **Dragon decorative sigil** as a secondary element (wrapped around or beside the Smoke sigil, as with Qifrey's Water Dragon).
3. **Four Levitation signs** evenly spaced around the ring, arrows/inward-facing, to lift the smoke construct.
4. **Four Column signs** in the same or outer ring, facing inward, to project the smoke body vertically.
5. Additional signs (unknown) for the roaring behavior and fine flight control.
6. A large, finely-drawn seal is needed — this is documented as "very complex."

- **Artistic performance:** at festivals or ceremonies.

## Notes & limitations
- **Classified "Unknown type" by wiki** — the wiki does not assign it to a known spell category. Probably niche given its decorative/demonstration function.
- **"Very complex"** — additional signs beyond Levitation and Column are documented as present but unidentified. The composition here is a partial reconstruction.
- **Manipulation of smoke unconfirmed** ([sigils.md:205](../sigils.md)) — the Dragon sigil's shape-imposition may bypass the manipulation question (it imposes form on newly-created smoke, not manipulating existing smoke).
- Beldaruit drew this on a floating circular platform in midair — the physical substrate was itself magically supported.

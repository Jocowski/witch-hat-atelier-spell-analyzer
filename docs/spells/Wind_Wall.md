# Wind Wall

> Packs and shapes the surrounding air into a standing circular wall — a dense pressure barrier that deflects objects and contains people or animals.

## Overview
Wind Wall ("Wall of Wind") is a **wind-type spell invented by Alaira** that *"creates a circular wall of air, difficult for objects or people to penetrate, and can be used to contain people or animals."* Its canon debut is **Tetia** using it to briefly hold the transformed **Euini** in place. The seal takes the ambient air the Wind sigil can move, weaves it into a continuous ribbon, compresses it firm with Convergence, and erupts it outward (inverted Columns) into an enclosing curtain.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine). One closed composition: a **Wind** core + **13 signs** (1 Weave, 6 Convergence, 6 inverted Column), no unknown ids. **Bilaterally symmetric → stable.** Info issues only: "stable (bilateral)" and a spurious "beam skews down" (see the artifact note below). The ring being drawn open is just the app's activation visual and is not part of validity.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):** **Wind** (`wind`, element **air**) — moves/manipulates ambient air; it is the medium the whole spell shapes.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Weave ×1** (large, scale 2.2, wrapping the core; `transmute`) — shapes the moving air into one long, continuous ribbon: the wall's surface.
  - **Convergence ×6** (back/top half, upright; `power`) — concentrates and **firms** the air into a dense, pressurized sheet.
  - **Column ×6** (front/bottom half, **inverted**; `form`) — inverted, the air **erupts outward on all sides** instead of firing as a jet, spreading it into an enclosing ring.
- **Ring:** drawn open (prepared) in the source export; activation visual only.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink (no dyes).

## Deduced effect
**Engine scaffold (not authoritative):** "The air is woven into a long, flexible ribbon. The effect is focused down to a single point."

**Reading (mine):** A standing **circular wall of dense, fast-moving air** around the caster. The Wind core grabs ambient air; **Weave** shapes it into a continuous ribbon (the wall surface); **Convergence ×6** packs that air tight and firm so it resists being pushed through; and the **inverted Columns ×6** make the air erupt outward on all sides rather than as a directed jet, spreading it into an enclosing curtain. The result is a barrier that **blocks/deflects objects and contains people or animals** — matching Tetia holding Euini. It is a **pressure barrier, not a solid object**: excellent at deflecting and corralling, but a temporary hold, and dependent on there being air to grab.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Wind (core) | air substance (move, not create) | Without it there is no medium; the wall is *made of* ambient air. |
| Weave ×1 | transmute (surrounds core) | Gives the wall its **continuity** — air becomes one long ribbon/curtain rather than scattered gusts. |
| Convergence ×6 | power (firms loose matter) | Supplies **density/pressure** — turns a breeze into a barrier hard to push through. |
| Column ×6 (inverted) | form (erupt, not project) | Turns a directed gust-cannon into an **all-around enclosure**; without inversion you'd get a jet, not a wall. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The seal is a clean answer to the physical problem "make a wall out of air," and each sign solves one part of it:

- **Air can be moved but not created** — wind *"allows for the movement and manipulation, but not the creation, of air"* ([docs/sigils.md:65](../sigils.md#L65)). For a wall this is a non-issue (air is everywhere), but it means the wall is **made of moving-air pressure, not a solid sheet** — it *resists* and *contains* rather than being impassable. Canon agrees: *"difficult* to penetrate," used to *"briefly* hold" Euini.
- **Gaseous substance + Convergence = the firmness trick.** On loose earth/sand, Convergence *"can make loose particles pack tightly together and become somewhat rigid"* ([docs/signs.md:107](../signs.md#L107)); on a gas it does the analogous thing — compresses diffuse air into a dense, high-pressure sheet. Canon ties this sign **directly** to this spell: *"the effect of spells such as wall of wind … suggests it might make the magic manifest about or from a single point"* ([docs/signs.md:107](../signs.md#L107)). Without Convergence you'd have a draft, not a barrier.
- **Inverted Columns turn a weapon into an enclosure.** Upright Columns on air make a directed gust-cannon; **inverted**, the magic *"is driven inward and erupts rather than projecting out,"* emitting on all sides ≈ Dispersion ([docs/signs.md:34](../signs.md#L34)). That is exactly how a one-directional jet becomes an all-around curtain — the same inverted-Column "containment/eruption" mode that bursts crystal outward in Crystal Shard, here bursting air into a ring.
- **Weave gives the wall its continuity.** A ribbon is long, flexible, and continuous ([docs/signs.md](../signs.md), Weave); wrap a ribbon-of-air around the caster and you get a seamless wall rather than disconnected gusts. **Caveat:** the wiki describes this enclosing sign as *"a semicircular sign with small details on its edges"*; **Weave** is the best *functional* fit (it is the sign that "surrounds the central sigil"), but **Radial** — two nested arcs — is arguably the closer match to the literal word "semicircular." This is the one identification with real uncertainty.

**Closest canon comparisons.** Other Wind-core spells (Grasping Wind, Skysoaring, Flying Puppet of Diversion — the catalog's nearest at 0.45–0.55) share only the medium; they **propel** air. Wind Wall is the one that **densifies and encloses**. The instructive opposite is **Wall Breaker Seal** (earth + Crush): same word "wall," inverse verb — Wall Breaker uses non-inverted Crush to *destroy* a real wall into dust ([docs/lexicon/substances.md:143](../lexicon/substances.md#L143)); Wind Wall uses inverted Columns + Convergence to *build* a wall from air. Wall Breaker needs a real wall to feed it (manipulate-only earth); Wind Wall needs only the surrounding air.

**Bottom line.** A faithful, feasible "shape the air you already have" spell — Weave makes the air a continuous ribbon, Convergence compresses it into a firm dense sheet, and inverted Columns erupt it outward into an enclosing ring. It is a **pressure barrier, not a solid object**: great at deflecting and corralling, a temporary hold (canon's "briefly held Euini"), and useless where there's no air to grab. The only soft spot in the reconstruction is the identity of the enclosing sign (Weave vs. Radial).

## How to draw it
1. **Wind sigil at center.**
2. **One large Weave** wrapping the core (scale ~2.2) — the enclosing ribbon.
3. **Six Convergence** triangles across the **back** half (upright, points inward) — the densifiers.
4. **Six Columns** across the **front** half, **inverted** (driven inward → erupt) — the enclosure.
5. Keep **bilateral (left–right) symmetry**; the front/back split (Columns front, Convergence back) is deliberate, not an imbalance.

## Usage ideas
- **Defensive bubble** — deflect thrown objects, arrows, or loose debris in a circle around you.
- **Containment pen** — corral an animal or a transformed person (its canon debut use).
- **Windbreak / shelter** — hold off a storm, dust, or smoke from a small area.
- **Threshold control** — a doorway-width arc to stop something crossing, without a physical barricade.

## Similar spells
- **Grasping Wind / Skysoaring / Flying Puppet of Diversion** — share the Wind core but *propel* air rather than enclosing it.
- **Wall Breaker Seal** — the "wall" opposite: destroys a real wall (earth + Crush) where this builds one from air.
- **Vapor Bubble** — uses balanced Columns to *confine* airflow, a related "Columns shape air into a container" idea.

## Notes & limitations
- **Not forbidden.**
- **Recipe is a reconstruction**, not a decoded canon seal: the Wind core, inverted Columns (front), and Convergence (back) come straight from the wiki's construction description and the partial manga panel; the **counts (6 + 6)** are reasonable guesses ("a cluster" / "several"), and the **enclosing sign's identity** (Weave vs. Radial) is the main open question.
- **Engine "aim: down / beam skews down" is an artifact** — the front-loaded Columns are **inverted** (erupt/spread), so there is no real directed beam; override per the [Column Findings](../lexicon/signs-directional.md#L37).
- **Pressure barrier, not a solid wall** — it deflects and contains but is penetrable with force and temporary; it also needs ambient air (pair with an **Aeriforms** *sigil* if it ever had to work in sealed/thin air, since Wind cannot create its medium — [docs/lexicon/substances.md:164](../lexicon/substances.md#L164)).

# Petrification

Petrification is a forbidden spell that turns everything nearby, alive or inanimate, into a rock-like unknown material.

## Description
Petrification has an unknown glyph construction. It has the ability to turn everyone and everything nearby to stone.

## Usage
After discovering that magic is drawn rather than spoken, Coco copied several spells from the magic picture book sold to her by Iguin when she was a little girl. The last spell she drew was the petrification spell and, just as she finished drawing it, Qifrey snatched her up and whisked her out of her house, just before it and her mother were turned to stone.

# Water Pen

Water pen (Unofficial name) is a water-type spell that makes water a dagger shaped glob of water.

## Description
Water pen is a spell that consists of a central water sigil with an unknown triangle-like keystone below, unknown keystones shaped like parts of a circle's perimeter on each side, and a dispersion keystone with 2 column keystones protruding from the sides at the top of the glyph. It creates a dagger-shaped glob of water, which hovers below the glyph at an angle. conjuring ink can be poured into the dagger, which will saturate the water with ink. This allows the user to utilize the dagger to draw very large glyphs in practically any surface. It is best utilized by drawing the glyph in a palm quire and holding it within the hand.

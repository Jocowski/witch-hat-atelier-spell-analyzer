# Floating Expansion

Floating expansion is a niche spell that makes the object it's drawn on levitate and grow.

## Description
The spell contains a central window sigil, surrounded by enlarging signs. Float and column keystones are located in the gaps between the enlarging signs. The spell causes whatever it's drawn on to grow to many times it's original size, as well as causing said object to levitate. (It having the window rune in its center likely implies that rune's effect is focusing the spell on only what that rune has contact with, different from the diamond which involves all in its radius. (courtesy of Enderking, User) ).

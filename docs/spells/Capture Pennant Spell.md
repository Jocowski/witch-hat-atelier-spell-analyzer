# Capture Pennant Spell

The Capture Pennant Spell is a niche-spell used in the Silver-White Capture Pennant.

## Description
While the image quality for this spell is poor, it appears as if it has a central square shaped sigil with what may be a diamond inside, somewhat similar to a light spell, surrounded by signs similar in appearance to the footprint of a chicken (with a shorter back toe) pointing away from the center, and a sign similar to an arrow with a filled-in tip pointing towards the center. The spell is believed to allow the capture pennants of the Knights Moralis to be controlled by the person using the spell, allowing them to wrap the banner around people and objects. However, as a spell similar to a repetition seal was also seen on one of the banners earlier in the story,[1] it is unknown which spell actually lets the banner be controlled, or if it's both of them working in tandem.

## Usage
The spell, while only directly seen on the cover volume 9, is used each time the Knights Moralis use their capture pennants, the contraption this spell is part of. These pennants are used by the knights to hold people (typically criminals or injured persons) or objects in place by wrapping them securely in the fabric of the banner, and even aid in moving people or items from place to place.

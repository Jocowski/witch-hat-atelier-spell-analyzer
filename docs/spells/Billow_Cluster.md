# Billow Cluster

> A niche spell that collects any material directed into it and converts that material into a soft, fluffy cloud.

## Overview
The Billow Cluster (wiki name) is a niche-type spell created by Tetia. It uses a central Billowing sigil surrounded by Collection signs to pull in nearby material and transmute it into a cloud. Its only canon use is as a component of the larger **Serpent's Bed of Sand** contraption (ch. 7), where it received sand generated by the sand-grinding sub-seal and converted it into a soft cloud on which a pursuing dragon came to rest, buying the girls time to escape.

## In plain terms (sum up)
The Billow Cluster is a "material-to-cloud" converter. Place the seal anywhere near the material you want to turn into cloud — the Collection signs draw the material in, and the Billowing sigil at the center transforms whatever arrives into a fluffy cloud. It cannot create anything on its own; it is entirely dependent on a supply of material being fed to it. In the manga it was fed sand produced by an adjacent sub-seal, but in principle any material Collection can gather and Billow can transmute would work.

## Validity
**Valid.** The Billowing sigil occupies the center (the `canBeCenter` sign promoted to core), satisfying the core requirement. Collection signs surround it. The known grammar synergy `billowing-no-collection` (a warning that fires when Billow lacks Collection) is satisfied here — Collection is present. Engine: valid, active, stable (radial symmetry). No blocking issues.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):** **Billowing sigil** (`billowing_sigil`) — a sign-as-sigil occupying the center. The Billowing sign "allows a spell to produce a fluffy cloud" ([signs.md:198](../signs.md)). As a core it acts as a **transmuter**: it takes in supplied material and converts it into cloud. Capability is **transmute** (convert existing material), not create — it needs something to fluff.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Collection ×4** (directional/semi-directional, support) — "will collect material located above and around the seal, allowing it to be used by a spell. The open side is typically seen facing inwards" ([signs.md:189](../signs.md)). All four face inward, drawing material toward the Billowing center.
- **Ring:** closed.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
The Billow Cluster draws in material from around and above the seal (via Collection), then transmutes that collected material into a soft cloud (via the Billowing sigil at center). The result is a body of cloud at the spell's location whose size and density depend on how much material is supplied.

The spell has no self-supplied substance — the Collection signs are the intake pump and the Billowing sigil is the converter. Remove either and the spell does nothing useful (Billowing without Collection "has nothing to turn into cloud"; Collection alone is inert without a transmuter to pass material to).

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect on this spell |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Billowing sigil | core / transmute | Converts collected material into cloud; without it there is nothing to turn the material into |
| Collection ×4 | support / intake | Gathers surrounding material and feeds it to the Billowing center; without them, nothing is collected |
| Radial symmetry (4 Collection, evenly spaced) | geometry / stability | Balanced intake from all sides → no directional bias; stable spell |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Billowing sigil is the rare **sign-as-sigil**: it can occupy the center position (a `canBeCenter` sign, retconned like Repetition and the decorative sigils) and there it provides the spell's substance — specifically, a **transmutation** substance ("the affected matter is transmuted"). Its grammar says: convert material into cloud. State of output: **gaseous/fluffy** (soft enough that a dragon sleeps on it).

The critical precondition is documented in [signs.md:198](../signs.md): "Collection or supposedly is needed to collect and convert certain materials into clouds, but there seem to be some materials that cannot be converted." Two implications:
1. **Collection is mandatory** — without it the Billowing has no feedstock. The grammar interaction `billowing-no-collection` fires as a warning when this condition is unmet.
2. **Not all materials convert** — canon doesn't specify which materials resist Billowing; sand clearly works (Serpent's Bed), as presumably do most loose/granular and gaseous substances; rigid matter (solid rock) may resist.

The Collection signs face inward (open side toward the Billowing center), which is the standard orientation confirmed by [signs.md:189](../signs.md). Each Collection sign draws from its arc of the environment; with four evenly spaced the intake is omnidirectional.

**Closest canon analogue — Serpent's Bed of Sand's Billow sub-cluster.** The Billow Cluster is essentially extracted from the larger Serpent's Bed contraption — the wiki confirms it "served to convert the sand generated by the other parts of the spell into a cloud." The standalone Billow Cluster is the same mechanism used independently, without the sand-milling and Repetition outer ring.

**Bottom line.** A clean, self-consistent "transmute collected material to cloud" spell. Its grammar is simple — one transmuter core, one intake ring — and its only weakness is that it produces nothing without a material supply. As a sub-component it is unusually legible: the Collection signs are the teeth, the Billowing sigil is the stomach.

## How to draw it
1. **Billowing sigil** at the center (the clover/4-petal stroke glyph).
2. **Four Collection signs** evenly spaced around the ring (every 90°), **open side facing inward** toward the center.
3. Keep all four Collection signs the same size for radial symmetry (stability).
4. Bigger seal = stronger intake field and larger cloud output.

## Usage ideas
- **Sub-component in contraptions:** feed it material from an adjacent seal (sand, earth, water vapor, loose debris) to produce a soft cloud landing pad, smokescreen, or resting surface.
- **Soft barrier or cushion:** deploy near a fall point; loose material nearby becomes a cloud pad.
- **Smoke or gas absorption and reshaping:** if the material available is already gaseous (smoke, steam), may reshape it into a denser/softer cloud form.
- **Platform creation (with Repetition outer ring):** wrap the Billow Cluster in a Repetition ring (as in Serpent's Bed) to keep the cloud stable for extended use.

## Similar spells
- **Serpent's Bed of Sand** — the parent contraption; Billow Cluster is literally extracted from it.
- **Smoke Cloud** — another cloud/smoke creation spell, but using the Smoke sigil directly rather than transmuting collected material.
- **Billowing Collection** — the name used in the wiki navigation for the same concept; likely the same spell or a nearly identical variant.

## Notes & limitations
- **Material dependency:** no feedstock, no cloud — the spell does nothing in an empty environment. Always pair with a nearby material source or an adjacent sub-seal that generates material.
- **Not all materials convert** (per [signs.md:198](../signs.md)) — test material compatibility; rigid or crystalline matter may fail to convert.
- Creator: **Tetia** (ch. 7). The name "Billow Cluster" is the wiki's name; the wiki also lists a "Billowing Collection" in the niche spells navigation which may be an alternate name or a separate but structurally identical spell.
- The Billowing sigil acting as core is a confirmed `canBeCenter` case (grammar: `billowing_sigil`); the engine handles this correctly.

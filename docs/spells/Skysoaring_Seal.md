# Skysoaring Seal

> Produces directional thrust; drawn large on something like a sail, it lets the object fly.

## Overview
The Skysoaring Seal is a wind-type spell: a **Wind** sigil set low in the glyph with a single large **Levitation** sign spanning above it to the opposite side. It produces thrust in the direction the keystone points; drawn large enough on a big object (e.g. a sail), it can carry that object into flight. Coco used it during the Consent of the Crown test.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine-confirmed). Wind core + a directional Levitation keystone. Engine: aim "above the seal"; the single-keystone layout reads **asymmetric** by design (it's directional, not balanced).

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** Wind — moves air; here it provides lift/thrust.
- **Form (signs):** Levitation ×1, large, spanning from above the sigil toward the far side. Direction of the keystone = direction of thrust. Size of the keystone = amount of lift.
- **Ring:** closed (active); drawn **large**.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain.

## Deduced effect
Engine readout: *"The air stirs into moving wind, centered above the seal; it is lifted and made to levitate."* A one-directional push: the seal flings itself (and whatever it's drawn on) in the keystone's direction. Scaled up on a large surface, the thrust is enough for sustained flight.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Wind sigil (low) | substance | The air being driven for thrust. |
| Levitation ×1 (large) | form | Sets thrust **direction** (where it points) and **magnitude** (how big it's drawn). |
| Overall scale | power | Big enough → lifts large objects into flight (canon: a sail). |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Wind moves/manipulates air but does not create it ([docs/sigils.md](../sigils.md)); Levitation is the directional lift sign whose orientation sets the push direction. Skysoaring is the "thrust by a single dominant directional keystone" pattern — the same size-sets-power lesson seen in the Watershot → Skysoaring lineage (a deliberately unbalanced keystone makes a one-way push). The asymmetric layout is intentional: it's an engine, not a balanced emitter.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Wind sigil** low in the glyph.
2. Draw one large **Levitation** sign spanning from just above the sigil to the opposite side.
3. Point the Levitation in the **direction you want thrust**.
4. Draw the whole seal **large** (on a sail, board, etc.) for enough lift to fly.

## Usage ideas
- **Flight / gliding** — on a sail, board, or cloak to ride the thrust.
- **Launch / boost** — a one-shot directional shove for a vehicle or object.
- **Propulsion** — mount on a craft for steerable wind drive.

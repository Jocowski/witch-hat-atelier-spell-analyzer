## Deduced effect
The most likely function is a **preservation/sealing spell** for a cooking pot. The Repetition sigil locks the pot's contents in their current state (no cooling, no spoilage, no evaporation); Region directs this effect to the enclosed space (inside the lid, not outside); Convergence focuses or seals the effect at the lid-rim boundary.

Ranked hypotheses:

**Hypothesis 1 (most likely): Preservation of pot contents.**
The Cookpot Lid spell keeps food/liquid inside the pot in a preserved state — same temperature, same freshness, same quantity — for as long as the seal is active. This directly parallels the Repetition Seal's documented uses ("can be used to keep food from rotting," [signs.md:203](../signs.md)). Region scopes the effect inside; Convergence tightens the boundary. *What would confirm it:* a panel showing the spell maintaining food quality over time.

**Hypothesis 2 (possible): Airtight sealing.**
Convergence is documented to make "loose particles pack tightly together and become somewhat rigid" ([signs.md:183](../signs.md)). If Convergence acts on the pot's physical lid-rim (the gasket), it seals the lid airtight. Combined with Region (scope inside) and Repetition (maintain the sealed state), the spell creates a permanently airtight seal. *What would confirm it:* the spell being used to pressurize or vacuum-seal a container.

**Hypothesis 3 (possible): Maintenance of contents at a specific state (stop-watch variant).**
Like the Warmth-Retention Seal (which uses the Stop sigil rather than Repetition), the Cookpot Lid could lock the contents at a specific state. But Repetition (vs. Stop) implies it actively *resets* rather than *freezes* — a pot with Repetition would reset to the "just-cooked" state if stirred or cooled, like a spring returning to rest. *What would confirm it:* the pot contents returning to their initial state if disturbed.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Repetition** as a sigil is the "spring" substance: "restoring the object to its original state if somehow changed — can be used to make soft objects elastic or prevent things from rotting" ([sigils.md:99](../sigils.md)). Its state is **immaterial** (acts on time/history). For a cooking pot, "original state" = the contents at the moment the seal was activated.

**Region** as a directional sign ("all pointing inward → magic only manifests within the ring," [signs.md:173](../signs.md)) is the scope limiter. In the Cookpot Lid, the most natural reading is "inward" (effect confined to the interior of the lid). The Floating Drops spell shows Region pointed toward itself (magic manifests *on* the ring) — a variation that could also seal the rim.

**Convergence** functioning as a "seal" at the rim parallels its behavior in the Wind Wall spell ("manifest about or from a single point") and its sand-compaction role in Serpent's Bed ("makes loose particles pack tightly"). A Convergence sealing the rim would collapse any gap between lid and pot into a rigid seal.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Repetition Seal** — uses Repetition sigil + Convergence (listed among its signs) to reset objects continuously. Cookpot Lid may be a more specialized version scoped to a container's interior.
- **Warmth-Retention Seal** — another container-oriented preservation spell (Stop sigil + fire sigil to hold heat); the Cookpot Lid may be the time-reset counterpart (Repetition rather than Stop, and general rather than heat-specific).

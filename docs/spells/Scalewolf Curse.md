# Scalewolf Curse

Scalewolf Curse is a forbidden spell that permanently causes someone's body to be transformed. In the case of Euini, he was turned into a scaled wolf.

## Description
The spell contains a keystone identical to one in the illusory labyrinth spell, this keystone being made up of three dots arranged in a triangle shape, but other than that, all of its keystones are unknown. When it was used on Euini, the spell transformed him into a scalewolf. This happened as a result of the mirror cloak he was wearing fusing with him, as the cloak contained the fur and scales of the scalled wolf.

## Usage
The spell's first and only known use was by Sasaran, a Brimmed Hat Witch, during the events of the second pentagram test. Sasaran used the spell on Euini, forcibly fusing him with his cloak, resulting in his transforming into a scaled wolf.

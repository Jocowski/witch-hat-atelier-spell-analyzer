# Fish Guidance

> A niche guidance spell that makes magic fish (or other objects matching its parameters) float through the air and move toward the seal's location.

## Overview
Fish Guidance (unofficial name) is a niche-type spell used during the Golden Eve Procession (ch. 63) by Jujy to help Coco and Agott showcase Coco's Portable Waste Purification Pot. The wiki describes it as making "fish formed with magic using a decorative sigil move towards the seal through the air." This is a guidance-based spell: the Guidance sigil attracts matching objects (in this case, magically-formed fish), while Float paired with Crosshair provides the aerial levitation of those targets.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Fish Guidance is a "lure" spell. Draw it nearby, and any magic fish (or matching objects) in the vicinity will float through the air toward the seal. The Float sign normally only levitates the object the seal is drawn on, but when paired with a Crosshair sign it gains the ability to levitate its *targets* instead (per the documented canon exception — see Signs_Explained). The Guidance sigil then parameterizes which objects are attracted: those matching the decorative fish sigil. In the Golden Eve context, this herded decorative fish along a parade route.

## Validity
**Valid.** Guidance sigil at center provides the attraction substance. Float + Crosshair enables aerial movement of targets (the canon-documented exception to Float's normal self-only behavior). No blocking issues; radial symmetry is stable.

## Deduced effect
The Guidance sigil draws matching objects (decorative fish) toward the seal's location. The Float + Crosshair combination makes those attracted fish levitate through the air rather than crawl along the ground. Together, the spell herds floating magic fish through the air toward the seal — a procession lure or display spell.

The Guidance sigil's parameters are set by the companion sign/sigil. In the canon use, a decorative fish sigil is the most probable parameter-setter, either as a secondary sigil within the seal or as an external linked element; without it, the Guidance sigil would attract whatever broadly matches.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Guidance sigil is an **immaterial attraction force** that "needs matching objects present in the environment to pull" — a pump that needs a reservoir ([lexicon/substances.md](../lexicon/substances.md)). It is parameterized by its companion symbols: here, those symbols scope the match to decorative-fish-shaped magic objects.

The **Float + Crosshair** interaction is canon-documented in Signs_Explained: "the fish guidance spell, requiring float to be paired with crosshair in order to levitate the spell's target, suggest that float can't levitate things on which it isn't drawn unless given explicit instructions to do so." This is one of only three confirmed non-self-levitation cases for Float (the others being the Floatglow Lamp Seal and the Earth Lift Spell, both of which are anomalous and less cleanly documented).

Float is **non-directional** (radially symmetric, no front/back), so it provides omnidirectional gravity-cancellation for the targets. Multiple Float signs evenly spaced reinforce the levitation field.

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Billow Cluster / Purify** — other collection-type spells that attract material; but those transmute or clean it, whereas Fish Guidance only moves it.
- **Grasping Wind** — similarly "pulls things in" using Pull + a wind sigil, but that operates on wind-based matter. Fish Guidance operates on guidance attraction with float, a completely different mechanism.

**Bottom line.** A purpose-built procession/display spell: Guidance as the attractor, Float+Crosshair as the aerial-motion enabler. The Float+Crosshair pairing is canon-confirmed by Signs_Explained as the exact mechanism that distinguishes this spell's target-levitation from Float's normal self-only behavior. Confidence in the basic composition (Guidance + Float + Crosshair) is **medium** — grounded directly in Signs_Explained, but the exact counts and any additional signs are unconfirmed.

## How to draw it
1. **Guidance sigil** ("G") at the center.
2. **Four Float signs** evenly spaced around the ring (every 90°). Float is non-directional, so orientation does not matter, but even spacing ensures stable coverage.
3. **One Crosshair sign** at any position (also non-directional). Its presence alone triggers the Float-targets-externally behavior.
4. If a decorative fish sigil is needed to parameterize the guidance match, place it as a secondary core component at the center alongside Guidance.
5. Keep the ring radially balanced for stability.

## Usage ideas
- **Procession display:** herd decorative magic fish (or other animal-sigil-formed shapes) along a route toward the seal location for a visual spectacle.
- **Trap lure:** placed near an exit, draws magic constructs into a collection zone.
- **Parade coordination:** multiple Fish Guidance seals along a route could pass the fish from one to the next.
- **General levitating lure:** replace the fish parameter with any other decorative sigil to attract and float different magic constructs.

## Similar spells
- **Billow Cluster** — another attraction/collection spell, but converts material rather than herding it.
- **Purify** — uses Collection on water to gather and clean; a different domain of attract-and-process.
- **Grasping Wind** — a pull-based wind spell that draws matter toward the seal; uses Pull (direction) rather than Guidance (parameterized attraction).

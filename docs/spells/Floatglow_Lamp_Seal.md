# Floatglow Lamp Seal

> Creates a floating ball/beam of light held free of gravity — a flameless lamp.

## Overview
The Floatglow lamp spells are light-type spells that make floating balls of light. There are **two kinds**: a **free-floating** version and a **wall-anchored** version (used in the wall-anchored floatglow lamp). This entry models the **free-floating** version: a central **Light** sigil surrounded by **alternating Float and Column** signs, which should emit light (like Light Beam) while letting the source float free of gravity.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine-confirmed). Light core + alternating Float/Column ring. Engine: **stable, radial symmetry**; aim "above the seal."

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** Light.
- **Form (signs):** Float ×4 + Column ×4, alternating around the ring. Column projects the light; Float frees it from gravity so the glowing source hovers.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain.

## Deduced effect
Engine readout: *"The light is projected as a tight column or beam, above the seal; it floats free of gravity."* A hovering lamp: a ball/shaft of light suspended above (or free of) its surface — illumination you can set adrift.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Light sigil | substance | The light emitted. |
| Column ×4 | form | Projects/emits the light. |
| Float ×4 | form | Frees the light source from gravity so it hovers. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Float is the gravity-release sign; Column is the projection sign ([docs/signs.md](../signs.md)). Alternating them rings the light source with "emit + hover," yielding a free-floating lamp rather than a fixed beam (Light Beam) or an anchored light. The wiki notes the design "should produce a beam of light… while also allowing the object to float," matching the engine's combined readout.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Light sigil** at the center.
2. Ring it with **alternating Float and Column** signs (4 each), all inward.
3. Keep the ring even so the lamp hovers level and glows steadily.
4. (Wall-anchored variant differs — not modelled here.)

## Usage ideas
- **Portable lamp** — a flameless, floating light you can release into a room.
- **Marker / float-light** — set adrift to mark a spot or float over water.
- **Safe lighting** — light without an open flame.

## Similar spells
- **Light Beam** — same light + Column projection, *without* the Float (fixed beam).
- **Bird of Light Beacon** — light shaped into a moving form.
- **Pyreball Seal** — fire's "floating ball" analogue (levitation ring on fire).

## Notes & limitations
- **Two versions exist** (free-floating and wall-anchored, seal shown ch. 28); this entry models the free-floating one. confidence: medium.

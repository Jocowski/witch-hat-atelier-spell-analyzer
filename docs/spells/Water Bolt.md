# Water Bolt

Water bolt (unofficial name) is a water-type spell that creates water arrows.

## Description
Water bolt is a spell that consists of a water sigil near the bottom, with one horizontal row of bolt keystones going through the middle of the spell, perpendicular to the water sigil, and another and row of directional keystones running parallel to the row of bolt keystones. The spell shoots bolts of water straight ahead, and is more effective when drawn big, needing to be drawn on the ground in order for there to be enough room for it to be effective.

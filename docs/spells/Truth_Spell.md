## Deduced effect

Ranked hypotheses for the composition and mechanism:

1. **Most likely — vision-class sigil (vision_sigil or eye sign) + bind or envelop:** The vision family of signs in WHA relates to perception, concealment, and revealing. A spell intended to "reveal truth" could use a vision-class sigil as the substance (the "truth" is a perceptual substance — what is actually seen/known) with bind or envelop signs to wrap the target's speech, forcing what is "truly seen" to match what is spoken. This would be consistent with vision-class mechanics (Gathering Shadows, Makeover Mask use vision signs for concealment; inverting those mechanics could produce revelation rather than concealment).

2. **Possible — guidance sigil + compulsion mechanic:** The guidance sigil produces attraction force — it pulls things toward itself. Applied to a person (via being drawn on the body), it could create a "pull toward truth" — metaphorically, the spell draws out what the person actually knows rather than what they choose to say. Guidance is an immaterial substance; its attraction property could be applied to information/intention rather than physical objects.

3. **Possible — unknown pre-Pact mind-body sigil (similar to Memory Erasure's sigil):** Like the Memory Erasure, the Truth Spell operates on human consciousness — the mind, not just the body. It may use the same class of unknown niche sigil (the concentric-circles type or a cousin) that gives Memory Erasure access to mental content, here redirected from "erase" to "compel release."

This would be a "forced playback of internal knowledge" mechanic rather than a compulsion on the will.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The Truth Spell's classification as "drawn directly on the human body" distinguishes it from spells like the Scalewolf Curse and Petrification, which are drawn on a nearby surface and project their effect onto people. A body-drawn spell implies the inscription is the activation medium AND the target simultaneously — the caster draws it on the subject's skin, and it immediately affects the person whose skin carries it. This is the most direct form of body-affecting magic.

The most relevant comparable spell is the **Memory Erasure**, which is similarly used on the body (applied toward the face) and acts on human consciousness. That spell's concentric-circle sigil is the only known precedent for a mind-content-manipulation sigil in the documented system. The Truth Spell likely uses a related niche sigil, or possibly the same one with different signs that extract rather than erase.

The Counterclock Spell — listed alongside the Truth Spell as a body-drawn forbidden spell — is a documented time-manipulation spell (it manipulates time in a direction). The Truth Spell's placement alongside it suggests a similarly high-level effect but targeted at the mental/communicative domain rather than temporal.

**Bottom line:** The Truth Spell is a body-drawn forbidden spell that compels truthfulness. Beyond the name and classification, nothing is confirmed. It likely requires a mind-content or perception-class sigil not fully documented in the current system, paralleling the Memory Erasure's unidentified sigil. No reconstruction is possible.

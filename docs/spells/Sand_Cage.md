# Sand Cage

> Builds a rigid cage of sand around a target to trap it — the rigid counterpart of the Serpent's Bed of Sand.

## Overview
Sand Cage is a canon Earth spell that conjures a **rigid prison of sand** around its target. Tetia used it to
cage the transformed Scaled Wolf Euini so the rest of the atelier could attempt the inverted Scalewolf Curse on
him. Its guiding idea is the line *"if sand can be made soft as a bed, it can also be made rigid as a cage"* —
the same loose sand the Serpent's Bed of Sand softens, this spell instead **binds into a hard enclosure**.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** Earth — governs stone, sand, soil and wood; *manipulates but does not create* them
  ([sigils.md:53](../sigils.md#L53)). It needs a real source of sand/earth nearby.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Crush ×2, inverted** (north & south, inner) — semi-directional. Upright it pulverizes; **inverted it
    reintegrates loose grains into a cohesive solid, temporarily** ([signs.md:73](../signs.md#L73)). This is
    what makes the sand **rigid**.
  - **Column ×6 (normal) + ×2 (inverted)** — directional. The east/west pairs point at one another (one normal,
    one inverted); together with the intercardinal columns they raise and shape the **bars/walls** of the cage
    ([signs.md:34](../signs.md#L34)).
  - **Region (direction) ×8** — directional. Aim the structure and keep it contained around the target
    ([signs.md:96](../signs.md#L96)).
  - **Unknown Sign 5 ×2** — half-circle, at the N and S poles, convex side toward the core. **Function
    unconfirmed**; reads as the closing hoop/recurved lip that seals the cage's top and bottom (see below).
  - *Canon construction note:* the intercardinal marks are described in the wiki as a **fusion of one Column +
    two Region signs**; the app models them as the three component signs.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
> **Engine summary:** *"The earth, stone, and sand is reassembled from dust."* (with an *incomplete-effect*
> warning for the 2 unknown signs).

In plain terms: the spell gathers loose sand and **binds it into a rigid cage** around a target, holding it
trapped for as long as the spell runs. The inverted Crush supplies the cohesion (sand → solid), the Columns and
Region signs raise and aim the bars, and the half-circle signs appear to close the enclosure at the poles. It
is a containment tool, not a projectile — at full intensity (there is no power-tempering sign).

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Manipulate-not-create means the cage needs real sand.** Earth cannot conjure matter
([sigils.md:53](../sigils.md#L53)); it only rearranges what is already there. Sand Cage is therefore a
*sculptor of available sand*, not a conjurer — it works where there is loose earth to draw on (as in the scene
where Tetia cast it). Take the sand away and there is nothing to cage with.

**Rigidity comes from the inverted Crush, not from a dedicated "harden" sign.** Loose sand is granular and
flows; inverting Crush makes it **reintegrate into a cohesive, rigid body** — but only *temporarily*, the powder
slumping back once the spell lapses ([signs.md:73](../signs.md#L73)). That temporariness is exactly why the
cage is a *hold-it-for-now* measure ("this should hold him for now") rather than a permanent structure. The same
inverted-Crush reintegration drives the Integration spell; here it is put to a constructive, shaping end instead
of mere reassembly.

**The physical state fits the mechanic well.** Granular sand is the most fluid-like, controllable form of earth
([Earth_Orb.md](Earth_Orb.md)) — it flows into the cage's shape and then sets when the Crush binds it, the way
wet sand packs into a mold. A rigid block of stone could not be *formed* into bars this way without first being
ground down; sand is already in the right state to be re-bound into any shape.

**Closest canon analogue — the Serpent's Bed of Sand.** They are two faces of the same control over loose sand:
- **Same:** Earth core, loose-sand substance, the manipulate-only constraint, crush-based reworking of the
  grains, a structure built in place around/under a subject.
- **Differs:** the Bed makes the sand **soft** (a cushioning bed); the Cage makes it **rigid** (a hard prison).
  The spell's own line frames the pair exactly: soft as a bed ↔ rigid as a cage. Sand Cage adds the Column/Region
  "bars" and the half-circle poles to turn a shaped mass into an *enclosure*.

**Bottom line:** Sand Cage is the **rigid sibling of the Serpent's Bed of Sand** — it binds available loose sand
into a hard cage around a target, using inverted Crush for cohesion, Columns/Region for the bars, and the
half-circle signs (unconfirmed) to close the poles. It is feasible only where there is real sand to work, and
the prison is **temporary** by the nature of inverted Crush, making it a "hold them for now" trap rather than a
permanent cell.

## How to draw it
1. Place the **Earth** sigil at the center.
2. On the **east and west** points, draw a **pair of Columns** each, the two of a pair pointing at one another —
   one normal (inner) and one **inverted** (outer).
3. On the **north and south** axis, between the core and the poles, draw **inverted Crush** signs (these bind
   the sand rigid).
4. At each **intercardinal** point (NE/SE/SW/NW), draw the **Column + two Region** cluster that shapes and aims a
   corner of the cage.
5. At the **north and south poles**, draw the **half-circle (Unknown Sign 5)** with its convex side toward the
   core (closing the top and bottom).
6. Keep the layout symmetric for stability; a bigger, neater seal makes a stronger, longer-lasting cage. Cast it
   where there is **loose sand/earth** to draw on. Close the ring to activate.

## Usage ideas
- **Trap a creature or foe** (the canon use — it held the Scalewolf Euini while the others worked).
- **Temporarily contain something dangerous** while preparing a follow-up spell or escape.
- **Improvised rigid structure** from available sand — a barrier or pen, for as long as the spell holds.

## Similar spells
- **Serpent's Bed of Sand** (canon, earth) — the soft counterpart; same loose-sand control, opposite result.
- **Integration** (canon, earth) — same inverted-Crush reintegration, but only reassembles a form in place
  rather than building a cage.
- **Sand Bridge** (canon, earth) — another "shape loose sand into a structure" spell.
- **Wall Breaker Seal** (canon, earth) — the destructive face of Crush (upright → pulverize).

## Notes & limitations
- **The cage is temporary** ([signs.md:73](../signs.md#L73)) — inverted Crush holds the sand bound only while
  the spell runs; it is a "hold for now" trap, not a permanent cell.
- **Needs a real sand source** — Earth manipulates, does not create ([sigils.md:53](../sigils.md#L53)).
- **Two unidentified signs** — the half-circle Unknown Sign 5 (poles) is unconfirmed in function; the engine
  flags the deduced effect as incomplete. Best reading: a closing hoop / inward-recurving lip that seals the
  cage at top and bottom. Seen in only this spell.
- **Not forbidden** — manipulating inanimate sand is unrestricted (the *Scalewolf Curse* business around it is a
  separate, forbidden spell; the cage itself is not).
- **Engine ↔ catalog:** added to `data/spells.json` as `sand_cage` (origin canon, confidence medium); the
  analyzer self-matches this recipe at ~0.86.

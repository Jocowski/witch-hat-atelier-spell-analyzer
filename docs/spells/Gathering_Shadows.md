# Gathering Shadows

> Makes the object it's drawn on absorb all light, letting the user conceal themselves in shadow.

## Overview
Gathering Shadows is a vision-type spell: a central **Vision** sigil with **Eye** signs on 4 of its 8 points and **Bend** signs off the other 4. The object the seal is drawn on absorbs all light that strikes it, so by covering themselves with that object the user can hide within shadow. It appears as a building block of the **Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade** spell.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine-confirmed). Vision core + Eye ×4 + Bend ×4. Engine: **stable, radial symmetry**.

## Composition
- **Substance (sign-as-sigil):** Vision — the perception/light-handling core (occupies the center).
- **Form (signs):** Eye ×4 (on 4 of the 8 vision points) + Bend ×4 (off the other 4). Eye keys the spell to sight/light; Bend curves light and shadow around the object.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain.

## Deduced effect
Engine readout: *"The light shines outward; it absorbs light into shadow and bends light and shadow around its target."* The drawn object becomes a light sink — incoming light is absorbed and bent away, so the object (and whoever hides under it) reads as shadow. A concealment/stealth effect.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Vision sigil (core) | substance | Handles sight/light — the perception medium. |
| Eye ×4 | form | Ties the effect to light/sight (what is seen). |
| Bend ×4 | form | Curves light and shadow around the object → absorption/concealment. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Vision is a sign-as-sigil that can occupy the center ([docs/sigils.md](../sigils.md) / canBeCenter). Pairing **Eye** (sight) with **Bend** (curving) on the 8 vision points produces light-bending concealment — the same family of light-manipulation that the **Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade** builds on. Borrowshade is forbidden-adjacent (a concealment cloak); Gathering Shadows is the light-absorbing component, not itself flagged forbidden here, but see notes.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Vision sigil** at the center.
2. Place an **Eye** sign on 4 of its 8 points.
3. Place a **Bend** sign off the remaining 4 points.
4. Drawn on a cloth/object, then cover yourself with it to vanish into shadow.

## Usage ideas
- **Concealment / stealth** — hide in shadow under the treated object.
- **Glare control** — absorb harsh light on a surface.
- **Component** — as part of larger concealment magic (Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade).

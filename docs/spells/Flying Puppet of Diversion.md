# Flying Puppet of Diversion

Flying puppet of diversion is a wind-type spell that will cause nearby objects to dart through the sky.

## Description
Flying puppet of diversion contains a central wind sigil surrounded by the ring-like dancing puppet keystone. When the glyph is completed, it will cause any objects near it to dart into the sky and move around erratically, with enough strength to even carry some objects up into the sky with it.

# Crystal Shard

> Erupts several large, shard-like crystals suddenly out of the glyph.

## Overview
Crystal Shard is a crystal-type spell: a **Crystal** sigil ringed by **inverted Column** keystones. It causes several large crystalline shards to erupt abruptly from the seal. Richeh used it in a burst of emotion.

## Validity
**Valid** (engine-confirmed). Crystal core + inverted Column ring. Engine: **stable, radial symmetry**.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** Crystal — produces/manipulates crystal.
- **Form (signs):** Column ×4 (cardinals; the wiki shows four), **inverted**. Normal Column projects a beam *outward*; inverting it drives the force *inward*, so the substance erupts as shards instead of streaming out.
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain.

## Deduced effect
Engine readout: *"The crystal is driven inward and erupts rather than projecting out, above the seal."* A sudden bristle of large crystal spikes bursting from the glyph — a burst/area attack rather than a single directed shard.

### How each part shapes the spell
| Part | Role | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Crystal sigil | substance | The crystal material that forms the shards. |
| Column ×6 **inverted** | form | Inverts the beam → inward drive → eruption of shards. |
| Size (scale) | power | Larger columns → bigger, more powerful shards (power = sign size). |
| Count (4 vs more) | quantity | More columns → denser eruption ring + matcher count only; not the engine's power. |

## Element behavior & canon grounding
Column is invertible; its inverted variant is the documented "containment / eruption" mode ("Inverted variant seen in Wind Wall/Crystal Shard — column 'inward' / containment and eruption", [docs/signs.md](../signs.md)). Crystal Shard is the canonical crystal use of that inverted mode: rather than collimating a beam, the inverted columns make the conjured crystal explode outward as spikes.

## How to draw it
1. Draw the **Crystal sigil** at the center.
2. Ring it with **Column** signs and **invert** each one.
3. Keep the ring even for an all-around eruption.
4. Scale up for larger / more numerous shards.

## Usage ideas
- **Burst attack** — a sudden ring of crystal spikes around the caster.
- **Barrier / caltrops** — erupt shards to block a path or fortify a spot.
- **Anchor** — drive shards into a surface to pin or brace something.

## Similar spells
- **Crystal Ribbon** — the *shaping* crystal spell (a flexible ribbon); seal never shown in canon (see complex list).
- **Light Beam / Watershot** — the *non-inverted* Column-ring beam, the opposite of this eruption.
- **Wall of Wind** — also uses inverted columns (for an air wall), the same inward-drive principle.

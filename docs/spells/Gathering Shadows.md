# Gathering Shadows

Gathering Shadows is a niche spell that makes the object it is drawn on disappear into shadow.

## Description
The spell consists of a central vision sigil, with eye sigils located on 4 of it's eight points. Bend sigils are located just off of the 4 remaining points. The spell causes the object the spell is drawn on to absorb all light that hits it, allowing the user to conceal themselves within the shadows by covering themselves with the object.

## Usage
Gathering shadows was first seen as a component of the mirror cloak of borrowshade spell when Eunie was inspecting the inside of the cloak during The Sincerity of the Shield. The spell was then seen in it's entirety when Eunie was explaining the spell to Agott, Riche, and Alaira. Not long after, the spell was seen as a component of the cloak spell used in Sasaran's magic cloak, which he uses almost every time he appears.

Gathering shadows is included in the mirror cloak of borrowshade alongside mirror.
Gathering shadows is also included in the Cloak Spell.

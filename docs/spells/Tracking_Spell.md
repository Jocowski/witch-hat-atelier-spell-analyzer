# Tracking Spell

> A niche spell placed on magical items sold to outsiders so that the Knights Moralis can locate those items if they end up on the black market.

## Overview
The Tracking Spell is used by the Pointed Hat Witches to mark magical items sold or given to non-witches (outsiders). When such an item enters illicit trade, the Knights Moralis can use the spell to locate it. Luluci explains this in ch. 46 when she discovers Custas has left Kalhn Medical Facility. The spell appears to be a standard institutional practice — an anti-smuggling measure embedded in legitimate magical commerce.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Think of it as a magical barcode or GPS tag attached to items that leave witch hands. Every item sold to an outsider gets this spell stamped on it. If it shows up in the black market, a Knight Moralis can use the mark to find exactly where the item is. It's one of the ways the witch community polices the illegal spread of magic. The actual mechanics — what signs are in the seal — are unknown in the canon to date.

1. *Most likely — Guidance-based beacon (moderate confidence):* the `guidance` sigil creates an attraction pull toward a designated recipient (it's the substance of Pouches of Calling, which summon their paired counterpart).

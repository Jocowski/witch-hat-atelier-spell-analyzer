# Wall-Anchored Floatglow Lamp

> A wall-mounted magical lamp: a light sigil with levitation and convergence makes the lamp's
> conical top float and focus the generated light outward.

## Overview
The Wall-Anchored Floatglow Lamp is a light-type contraption seen throughout Qifrey's atelier
(ch. 2) — the magical wall sconces that light its hallways. It consists of a bottom dish that
holds an activated seal, with a conical top that floats above the dish when the seal is active.
The floating top acts as a reflector, directing the generated light outward into the room.
The sealed recipe uses the **light sigil + levitation + convergence + column** — confirmed by the
signs.md spell lists for both Levitation and Convergence. This distinguishes it from the
**free-floating Floatglow Lamp Seal** (which uses Float instead of Levitation and omits
Convergence).

## In plain terms (sum up)
This is a magical wall lamp. The light sigil generates light; column signs project it; levitation
signs lift the lamp's conical top (and the light source) free of gravity so it floats at the right
height; and convergence focuses the light into the cone so it shines outward rather than spilling
in all directions. The whole thing is wall-mounted in a dish fixture — place an activated seal in
the dish and the lamp lights itself and stays lit. It is the enchanted equivalent of a sconce or
wall lantern.

## Validity
**Valid.** Light sigil at center; column projects the light; levitation floats the conical top;
convergence focuses the output. All preconditions met: light is a create-mode substance
(self-sufficient), column has a substance to project, levitation has a target to lift, convergence
has a light output to focus. The radial symmetry of four levitation + four column signs with a
central convergence gives a stable, even lamp. No blocking or warning issues.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigil):** **Light** (`light`, fire family) — produces light. Capability **create**
  (a fire variant; self-sufficient, needs no external source). State **luminous**
  (sigils.md:29; substances.md, Light).
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Levitation ×4** (`levitation`, directional, motion) — lifts the lamp's conical top (and
    the generated light) free of gravity. On a **light** substance, levitation lifts **straight up**
    without steering (not directional — see CORE.md §4 and signs-directional.md, Levitation findings:
    "levitation on water/fire/light lifts vertically without steering"). The four levitation signs
    therefore provide uniform upward lift, holding the cone at a fixed height. Sign size sets the
    weight of the object levitated (signs.md:55).
  - **Column ×4** (`column`, directional, form) — projects the light outward as a beam/column.
    Balanced ring of four: light exits uniformly in the projected direction (above the seal /
    through the cone).
  - **Convergence ×1** (`convergence`, semi-directional, power) — focuses the light down to a
    point, concentrating it. On a luminous substance this functions as a **focusing / collimating**
    operator: rather than diffuse glow in all directions, the light is gathered and directed so the
    floating cone can reflect it efficiently outward into the room (signs.md:107; signs-semi-directional.md,
    Convergence: "focuses/concentrates the magic down to a single point").
- **Ring:** closed (active).
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink.

## Deduced effect
A self-sustaining, wall-mounted **floating lamp**. The light sigil creates light; Column projects
it upward through the floating cone; Levitation holds the cone aloft at the right height; and
Convergence focuses the emitted light so the cone's reflective surface can redirect it outward
into the space. The lamp glows steadily and requires no fuel — it runs in light's create mode,
which is self-sufficient. It stays lit as long as the seal is active.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
**Light is a fire-family create substance** — "functions to produce magic that manifests in the
form of light" (sigils.md:29). It never needs a source: the lamp works anywhere, without fuel, and
never burns out. This matches the lamps seen throughout Qifrey's atelier (ch. 2) — they are
long-duration, maintenance-free lights.

**Levitation on light:** per the canon rule, levitation on fire/light/water lifts **vertically
without steering** (signs.md:53–55; signs-directional.md findings). The four inward levitation
signs uniformly push the cone upward. Their size sets the maximum weight of the cone; a heavier
reflector needs larger levitation signs. The engine may report a spurious lateral aim or spin from
the levitation geometry — override this; the lift is purely vertical for a light substance.

**Convergence + Column + light:** this pairing distinguishes the wall-anchored version from the
free-floating Floatglow Lamp Seal (which uses Float instead of Levitation and omits Convergence).
- **Float** (free-floating version) frees any object from gravity and floats it passively. The
  free-floating lamp simply drifts wherever it is released — no focusing, no wall-mount.
- **Levitation + Convergence** (wall-anchored version): Levitation holds the reflector at a fixed
  height above the dish, and Convergence focuses the light so the reflected beam fills the room
  efficiently rather than scattering. This is the better design for a fixed wall fixture: brighter
  per unit of ink because the light is focused, not scattered.

**Closest canon analogue — Pyreball Seal:** the Pyreball (fire + levitation ring) is the fire-
substance counterpart to this lamp. Both use a levitation ring to hold a luminous/energetic
substance aloft above the glyph. The key difference: Pyreball has no convergence (the ball spreads
as fire naturally does), whereas the Floatglow Lamp adds convergence to focus the light output
for practical illumination.

**Bottom line:** A self-sufficient, wall-anchored magical lamp with clean mechanics. Light (create)
+ Column (project) + Levitation (hold the reflector up) + Convergence (focus the beam). The choice
of Levitation over Float is what anchors the lamp to the wall — the levitation signs hold the cone
at a specific height above the dish rather than letting it float freely away. And the choice of
Convergence over nothing is what makes the light directional and bright rather than diffuse.

## How to draw it
1. **Light sigil** at the center.
2. **Four Column signs** evenly spaced around the ring (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°), each pointing
   inward (longer line toward center). This projects the light upward through the cone.
3. **Four Levitation signs** alternating between the columns (45°, 135°, 225°, 315°), each with
   the arrowhead pointing inward (toward the core). Size the levitation signs to match the weight
   of the lamp's conical top.
4. **One Convergence** (downward triangle ▽) near the center, focusing the light output.
5. The ring should be radially symmetric; any imbalance would tilt the floating cone.
6. Draw on the seal-dish at the base of the lamp fixture. Close the ring to activate.

## Usage ideas
- **Room lighting** — the canonical use: line hallways and rooms with these for continuous, clean
  magical illumination.
- **Directional spotlight** — orient the conical top and the convergence to direct a focused beam
  at a specific area.
- **Permanent atelier installation** — placed once, requires no maintenance or fuel replacement.
- **Adaptation with color dyes** — adding a color-tinting dye to the ink would produce colored
  magical light without any recipe change.

## Similar spells
- **Floatglow Lamp Seal** — the free-floating variant: uses Float instead of Levitation, no
  Convergence; the lamp drifts freely rather than staying at a fixed height.
- **Light Beam** — light + Column only, producing a fixed upward beam without levitation or
  focusing.
- **Pyreball Seal** — fire + levitation ring; the fire analogue of a levitation-held energy source.

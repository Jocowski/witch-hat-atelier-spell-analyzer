# Rainflinger

Rainflinger is a mixed-type spell that repels water and/or dries objects in it's radius. It is unclear which spell is the current one, as one (or both) of the spell's variants were retconned.

## Old Versions
### Repelling
The old version of the repelling variant of the rainflinger spell contained a central air sigil variant, with modified fire signs on the sides of the sigil, and crosshair signs above and below the air sigil. This spell creates a water repellent area, creating bubble devoid of water within the spell's radius.
Dispel Water Old

### Drying
The drying variant of rainflinger contains a central air sigil variant, with modified fire sigils, almost identical to those on the retconned repelling variant, placed on the sides of the sigil. Lastly, crosshair signs above and below the air sigil. The construction of this variant of rainflinger is almost identical to that of the retconned version of the repelling variant, except it's fire sigil variants are inverted and the line that passes through the triangle of the sigil does not connect. This spell dries things off and warms them up.

## New Version
Introduced in Chapter 28, the new variant of rainflinger retconned either only one of the previous iterations of the spell or both of them. It is unknown which is the case.

### Rainflinger (new)
The current iteration of rainflinger contains a central water sigil, with a fire sigil located above and below it, a crosshair sign on each side. Like the old versions, this version of the spell creates a water repellent area, creating bubble devoid of water within the spell's radius, though it is not known if this is done by repelling or drying said water.

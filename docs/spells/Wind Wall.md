# Wind Wall

Wind wall is a wind-type spell invented by Alaira that creates a circular wall of air that is difficult to pass thorough.

## Description
Wind wall contains a central air sigil with a cluster of inverted column signs in the front of the glyph, and several convergence signs at the back, along with a semicircular sign with small details on it's edges located around the air sigil on the column half of the glyph. This spell creates a circular wall of air, which is difficult for objects or people to penetrate, and can be used to contain people or animals.

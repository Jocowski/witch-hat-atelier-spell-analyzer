# Smokesculpting Seal

> A niche spell drawn on a tea saucer that recreates the shape of nearby ingredients as a smoke or steam sculpture rising from the cup.

## Overview
The Smokesculpting Seal (official Japanese name: 煙結の陣, Kemurimusubi no jin — "smoke-binding formation") is a decorative niche spell used by Qifrey on tea saucers in the Kitchen spinoff (ch. 11). When a cup of tea is set on the saucer, the seal reads the shape of the ingredients added to the tea and forms a smoke or steam sculpture resembling those ingredients above the cup. Similar to Integration, it "reforms the original shape" — but rather than restoring the actual ingredients, it recreates the shape using smoke. Coco was mesmerized by it. Qifrey's bag for holding Marktea is embroidered with some of the seal's distinctive signs.

## In plain terms (sum up)
Place a cup of tea on the saucer. The seal "reads" what ingredients went into the tea and sculpts a miniature smoke or steam figure — an owlcat, a flower, whatever the ingredient implies — rising from the cup. It's entirely decorative: the smoke figure looks like the ingredient's shape but is made of smoke, not the ingredient itself. Like Integration, it can reassemble a shape from a scattered/dissolved state, but using smoke as the medium instead of the original material.

## Validity
**Valid** for the identified components. Two caveats:
1. The **center-area spiral+arrow sign is unidentified** — the wiki describes it as "an unknown sign or sigil that looks like a spiral with an arrow through it vertically in the center." This is not in the known sign list. The spell's core logic may depend on this unknown symbol, making the deduction partially incomplete.
2. The **outer asterisk-column hybrid signs** are also unidentified — the wiki describes "several signs which look like an asterisk mixed with a column sign" in the outer portions. These are also unknown.
3. The identified components (inverted Crush, Column, and water-droplet-like signs similar to the water sigil's rain marks) are coherent with the described effect.

## Composition
- **Substance (sigils):** **Smoke** (`smoke`) — "works to create and generate smoke" ([sigils.md:205](../sigils.md)). Capability **create/generate**; manipulation is unconfirmed. State **gaseous**. The smoke sigil provides the sculptable material.
- **Form (signs):**
  - **Crush ×4 (inverted)** (semi-directional, transmute) — inverted Crush "reforms" scattered/dissolved material into its original shape. The wiki explicitly states these are "inverted crush signs" — the same mechanism as Integration, which "inverts [crush signs] to reform" ([signs.md:155](../signs.md)). Here they reform the *shape* of the ingredients in smoke.
  - **Column ×4** (directional, form) — projects the smoke in a column upward from the saucer, channeling the sculpture vertically above the cup.
  - **Unknown spiral+arrow sign** (center/core area, possibly a sigil-like shape) — unidentified. Its position at the center suggests it may be the actual core or a shaping sign that determines how the smoke reads the ingredient shape. *Ranked hypotheses below.*
  - **Rain-like droplet signs** — similar to the water sigil's modifier marks; possibly collect moisture/steam from the tea to supplement the smoke medium.
  - **Outer asterisk+column hybrid signs** — unidentified outer ring signs. Possibly additional Column-like projectors or fine-tuning signs.
- **Ring:** closed.
- **Ink / dyes:** plain conjuring ink (drawn on ceramic saucer).

## Deduced effect
The seal creates the smoke substance, shapes it into the form of the nearby ingredients (via inverted Crush reading and reforming the ingredient shape in smoke), and projects it upward via Column. The result is a sculptural smoke figure above the tea cup that matches the shape of the tea's ingredients.

The mechanism parallels Integration (inverted Crush = reshape from dust/dissolved state into original shape) but applied to *smoke as a proxy medium* rather than the original ingredient particles. The smoke sigil provides an already-gaseous medium that inverted Crush can then shape into the desired form.

### Hypotheses for the unknown central sign
**Hypothesis 1 (most likely): A shape-reading / imprint sign.**
The spiral+arrow could be an unknown sign that "reads" the shape of nearby objects (or objects on the saucer) and encodes that shape as a parameter for the inverted Crush to recreate in smoke. This would be a target/scan function analogous to Crosshair's area-scoping, but operating on shape rather than area. *What would confirm it:* other spells using the same sign showing a "copy shape of nearby object" behavior.

**Hypothesis 2 (possible): A form/template sigil, not a sign.**
The spiral+arrow could be a sigil-like element (a decorative or object sigil depicting a generic "form") that provides the template substrate — the pattern the smoke is shaped against. If it's a sigil, it would serve as the core and the Smoke sigil would be a secondary element. *What would confirm it:* if the spiral+arrow appears in the center (core position) while Smoke appears elsewhere.

**Hypothesis 3 (long-shot): A variant Billow or weave-like transmutation sign.**
The vertical arrow through a spiral could be a directional variant of Billow (which also transmutes material into cloud/gas form) or a specialized weave-like sign that knits the smoke into a defined shape. *What would confirm it:* the sign appearing in other smoke-transmutation contexts.

All three hypotheses treat the unknown sign as the key to understanding how the spell "reads" the ingredient shape — without it, the deduction is incomplete.

## Element behavior & canon grounding
The **Smoke sigil** is a `create/generate` substance (sigils.md:205): it produces gaseous smoke without needing an external smoke source. This is important — unlike Integration (which manipulates the *actual* ingredient particles), the Smokesculpting Seal generates fresh smoke and then shapes it. The fact that it "creates and generates smoke" (but manipulation is unconfirmed) means the seal is self-sufficient for material.

**Inverted Crush** is the documented reform mechanism: "if the sign is inverted and the spell is used on a powdered substance, the powder will reform into its original shape" ([signs.md:155](../signs.md)). In Integration this restores real particles to their shape. Here, inverted Crush is applied to the *generated smoke* to force it into the shape of the ingredient — the smoke acts as a malleable "powder" that Crush reforms into a coherent 3D form.

The wiki notes the similarity to Integration explicitly: "Similar to integration, this spell will reform the original shape of the ingredients put into it. However, rather than doing this with the ingredients themselves, the smokesculpture recreates the shape of nearby ingredients using smoke instead." This confirms the shared inverted-Crush mechanism.

**Column** (directional, inward-facing typically) channels the shaped smoke upward from the saucer — consistent with the standard Column behavior of projecting a spell's effect "in a column or beam above the seal" ([signs.md:119](../signs.md)).

**Closest canon analogues:**
- **Integration** — uses inverted Crush to reform particles; the Smokesculpting Seal uses the same mechanism but with smoke instead of actual particles.
- **Serpent's Bed of Sand** — uses a Billow Cluster to convert material into cloud; Smokesculpting uses smoke directly rather than converting.

**Bottom line.** A mostly-legible smoke-sculpting spell: Smoke sigil generates the medium, inverted Crush shapes it to match the ingredient form, Column projects it upward. The unknown center sign is the missing piece that explains how the spell "reads" which shape to form — likely a shape-scanner or template operator. Confidence in the identified components (Smoke + inverted Crush + Column) is **medium-high** (wiki-described). Confidence in the full composition is **low** due to the unknown signs.

## How to draw it
1. **Smoke sigil** at or near the center.
2. **Inverted Crush signs** evenly around the inner ring (facing outward / inverted, as with Integration).
3. **Column signs** in the outer ring, facing inward, to project the smoke sculpture upward.
4. The **unknown spiral+arrow mark** at the center position (possibly the actual core; Smoke sigil may be secondary).
5. Keep the design on a suitable substrate (ceramic saucer or similar) that will accept the ink and sustain the spell.

## Usage ideas
- **Decorative tea service:** the canon use — display the ingredient's shape as a smoke sculpture above the cup.
- **Identification marker:** the shape of the sculpture reveals what is inside the cup/container.
- **Artistic display:** with a decorative sigil substituted as the "ingredient parameter," could sculpt any desired smoke figure.
- **Game prop:** Qifrey used it for a game of Hushtea — the sculpture reveals the ingredient to other players.

## Similar spells
- **Integration** — the closest kin; same inverted-Crush reshape mechanism, but on actual particles rather than smoke.
- **Smoke Cloud** — a simpler smoke-generation spell (Smoke sigil + other signs); Smokesculpting is a far more complex sculpting application.
- **Billow Cluster** — converts material to cloud (similar gaseous-output theme, different mechanism).

## Notes & limitations
- **Multiple unknown signs.** The spiral+arrow center sign and the outer asterisk+column hybrid signs are unidentified; the deduction is necessarily incomplete.
- **Kitchen spinoff.** From the Witch Hat Atelier Kitchen manga spinoff — some consider spinoff canon separate from the main series; flag if needed.
- **Manipulation vs. creation:** the Smoke sigil is confirmed to *create* smoke but *manipulation* of smoke is unconfirmed ([sigils.md:205](../sigils.md)). The shaping is achieved via inverted Crush (a transmutation operator), not direct manipulation — so the spell stays within the sigil's confirmed capabilities.
- The outer asterisk+column hybrid signs are distinctive enough that Qifrey's bag is embroidered with them — suggesting they are prominent design elements.

# Ring

The ring is a circle that encloses the sigil and signs of a spell. A spell will only activate if its ring is complete. This makes it possible to prepare spells ahead of time by leaving a small gap in the ring which can be filled to activate it on the fly. Additionally, It is possible to wrap a spell inside another ring and fill the gap between them with a second spell, allowing one to combine the effects of the two, even if the spells aren't drawn on the same object.

# Non-Directional Signs

> **Non-directional = radial symmetry, no front.** These signs read the same from every
> angle, so they have no business-end to aim and **cannot be inverted** (there is nothing to
> flip — see [../CORE.md](../CORE.md) §4). Rotation only spins them in place; it does not steer.
> Three of them — **Billow**, **Repetition**, **Vision** — are `canBeCenter` and can sit at the
> origin *as the substance*, standing in for a sigil. Several of the rest are canon-uncertain
> (Diamond/Window inferred by comparison; Crosshair/Bolt single-spell; Eye/Bend/Vision an
> inseparable trio); flagged below rather than guessed.

---

### Float  `float`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: motion

**Drawing.** Three parallel vertical S-curves (sigmoid squiggles) side by side — like rising
heat-shimmer or smoke. Radially/translationally repetitive; no point or arrow, so no front.

**Acts as.** A `motion` operator: *"floats free of gravity"* — pins the object it is drawn on
in midair, ignoring gravity. **Not invertible** (no front). Post-retcon it is the *weaker*
cousin of levitation: it floats the thing it is **drawn on** and, per canon, can only float a
*different* target if given explicit instruction (Float + Crosshair in the Fish Guiding Seal).

**Parameters.** None geometric to speak of — it neither aims nor scales a direction. Quantity
adds redundancy, not steering.

**Findings.**
- *Retcon:* in two early spells (Pyreball, an unnamed ch.3 spell) Float was later **redrawn as
  levitation** — so a float that seems to lift a remote object is probably a mis-ID'd
  levitation. Float lifts what it is *on*. (signs.md:83-85)
- The Floatglow Lamp (column + float) producing a floating *ball* of light rather than a beam is
  flagged in canon as a likely **flawed design**, not a rule. (signs.md:85)

**Appears in.** Floatglow Lamp Seal · Fish Guiding Seal · Floating Expansion (signs.md:90)

### Diamond  `diamond`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: target

**Drawing.** A single tall rhombus (two stacked triangles meeting at a waist) — a hollow
diamond outline. Bilaterally symmetric on both axes; no front. Visually the *negative space*
counterpart to Window's grid.

**Acts as.** A `target` operator: *"affects nearby objects rather than the object it is drawn
on"* — it scopes the spell **outward**, onto neighbours. **Not invertible.** It is the
counterpart that pairs with a center sign (typically Enlarge) to say *who* gets resized.

**Parameters.** None directional; it is a scope switch (`opposes: window`).

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (inferred):* the effect is **isolated by subtraction** — Spell of Reduction
  (Diamond + inverted Enlarge → shrinks *nearby* things) vs Floating Expansion (Window + Enlarge
  → grows *itself*). Same template, swapped scope sign ⇒ Diamond = "nearby". (signs.md:215)

**Appears in.** Spell of Reduction (signs.md:217)

### Window  `window`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: target

**Drawing.** A square divided into a 2×2 (tic-tac-toe-style) grid — a window with panes; a
plus inscribed in a box. Fully radial/four-fold symmetric; no front. `canBeCenter` (it sat at
the center of Floating Expansion).

**Acts as.** A `target` operator: *"affects only the object it is drawn on"* — scopes the spell
**inward**, onto the host object only. **Not invertible.** The complement of Diamond
(`opposes: diamond`); together they tell a center sign (Enlarge) whether to act on self vs.
neighbours.

**Parameters.** None directional; a scope switch. May occupy the center.

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (inferred):* effect isolated the same way as Diamond — Floating Expansion
  (Window + Enlarge → grows itself) vs Spell of Reduction (Diamond → nearby). (signs.md:223)

**Appears in.** Floating Expansion (signs.md:225)

---

### Crosshair  `crosshair`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: target

**Drawing.** A simple plus/cross of four short equal strokes radiating from a central gap (the
arms do not quite meet) — a gunsight reticle. Four-fold radial symmetry; no front.

**Acts as.** A `target` operator: *"is confined within an area of effect."* Best read as
scoping the spell to an **area or to things of a matching magic aspect** — confine the
manifestation, or single out same-aspect matter. **Not invertible.**

**Parameters.** None directional; it gates *what/where* is affected, not a heading.

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (single-spell):* only firmly seen in the Rainflinger glyph; the
  "same-aspect / area-of-effect" reading is the leading theory, not confirmed. (signs.md:239)
- In the Fish Guiding Seal it partners **Float** as the explicit instruction that lets Float
  lift a *remote* target rather than its own host. (signs.md:241; cf. Float findings)

**Appears in.** Rainflinger · Fish Guiding Seal (signs.md:241)

### Bolt  `bolt`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: form

**Drawing.** A vertical spear/needle: a long central line passing through a small elongated
diamond (rhombus) at its middle — an arrow-shaft with a faceted head. Bilaterally symmetric;
the diamond reads as a point but is **mirror-symmetric top↔bottom**, so it has no fixed front.

**Acts as.** A `form` operator: *"fragments into fast-flying bolts"* — reshapes the output
channel so the substance manifests as a volley of bolt-like projectiles instead of one stream.
**Not invertible.** Precondition/synergy: it **wants a `direction` sign** (Region) to aim the
volley; alone the bolts fly with no controlled aim. (`wants: ["direction"]`)

**Parameters.** None directional itself; aim is supplied by the partnered Region signs. Quantity
of bolts is a substance/intensity matter, not a geometry knob here.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01 (Water Bolt): Bolt + a row of **Region** = a fast horizontal volley. The engine's
  axis word (it reported `down`) is an **in-plane axis label, NOT gravity** — a seal drawn flat
  on the ground fires *horizontally*; do not narrate "down" as a vertical/downward shot.
- Water's create-mode makes Bolt self-sufficient ammo (no reservoir). Structural cousin of the
  Watershot jet (one stream) — Bolt is the same water, *fragmented* into arrows.

**Appears in.** Water Bolt (signs.md:257)

### Billow  `billowing`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: transmute

**Drawing.** A four-petal flower/clover: four bulbous lobes around a small central knot, each
lobe a swelling teardrop — reads as a puff of cloud. Four-fold radial symmetry; no front.
`canBeCenter`.

**Acts as.** A `transmute` operator: *"is converted into a soft, fluffy cloud"* (comfortable to
sit on). **Not invertible.** Precondition: it **needs a `support` sign** (Collection / Gather)
to feed it material — alone it *"has nothing to turn into cloud."* (`wants: ["collection"]`)
Some materials reportedly cannot be converted at all.

**Parameters.** None directional. As a center sign it becomes the substance the surrounding
collection feeds.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01 (Serpent's Bed of Sand): **Billow-as-center promotion** — a Billow *sign* at the
  origin is promoted to the circle's core/substance (the engine now does this in
  `compose.js`), so a Billow Cluster (Billow + 4 Collection) validates and deduces a cloud.
  Before the fix the engine read a bare Billow center as "no core / invalid" — a false-negative.
- It transmutes whatever it is fed: in the Serpent's Bed of Sand, rock is first **milled to sand**
  (non-inverted Crush) because *that* is the state Billow can fluff. Match the feedstock's state
  to what Billow can convert. (signs.md:123)

**Appears in.** Serpent's Bed of Sand · Billow Cluster (signs.md:125)

### Eye  `eye`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A lens/almond shape (two opposed arcs meeting at points left and right) with a
solid filled dot at its center — a stylized open eye with a pupil. Bilaterally symmetric; no
front. (Note: the lens is *horizontally* oriented, but the symbol acts radially.)

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: *"absorbs light into shadow."* It is part of the
**concealment trio** — with Vision and Bend it melds the host object (and what it covers) into
the shadows. **Not invertible.**

**Parameters.** None deducible; effect is qualitative, not geometric.

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (never seen in isolation):* Eye has only appeared **alongside Vision** (the
  Gathering Shadows variants and the Makeover Mask). Its solo effect is unknown, and canon
  suspects it **may actually be a sigil**, not a sign. (signs.md:263)
- Currently **indistinguishable from Bend** in effect — the engine separates them, but treat the
  vision+eye+bend bundle as one concealment behaviour until canon disambiguates. (signs.json)

**Appears in.** Gathering Shadows · Makeover Mask Spell · Cloak Spell (signs.md:265)

### Bend  `bend`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A single vertical line with a short hooked tick branching off each end in opposite
directions — a flattened "Z" / lightning kink, or a line bent back at both tips. Point-symmetric
about the center (180° rotation), so no fixed front.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: *"bends light and shadow around its target."* The third
member of the **concealment trio** (Vision + Eye + Bend → hide in shadow). **Not invertible.**

**Parameters.** None deducible; qualitative.

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (broad, fuzzy):* across Gathering Shadows, Wall Bend, and Petrification the
  unifying theme is *"bending or altering of reality"* — bending **vision** (concealment),
  **physical objects** (a possible Bend+Weave pairing in Wall Bend → pliable walls), or
  **reality** (Petrification). Exact function unconfirmed. (signs.md:279)
- Currently **indistinguishable from Eye** in the concealment context. (signs.json)

**Appears in.** Wall Bend · Gathering Shadows · Cloak Spell · Petrification (signs.md:281)

---

### Repetition  `repetition`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A double spiral / nested whorl — concentric curved arms coiling inward to a tiny
square knot at the center, like a stylized whirlpool. Roughly two-fold rotational symmetry; no
front. `canBeCenter`.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: *"reverts its target to an earlier, like-new state"* —
effectively rewinds time on an object (keeps food fresh, cleans clothes, repairs wear). Grammar
records an `invertedVerb` *"accelerates wear,"* but as a **non-directional** sign it has no
front to flip — treat the revert direction as fixed unless canon shows otherwise.

**Parameters.** None directional. May occupy the center as the substance.

**Findings.**
- *Canon retcon (sign → sigil):* as of the Volume 12 bonus, Repetition was **reclassified from a
  sign to a sigil** for some spells (e.g. it is the *sigil* of the Capture Pennant Spell). When a
  spell exports it as a sign and the engine flags "no core," this retcon is a likely cause —
  check before calling the spell invalid. (signs.md:131)
- Mechanically it supplies **persistence/preservation**: in the Serpent's Bed of Sand the outer
  Repetition wrap is what makes the sand structure *last*, distinct from Convergence (which makes
  it *firm*). (learnings, 2026-06-01)

**Appears in.** Serpent's Bed of Sand · Capture Pennant Spell (sigil) · Repetition Seal
(signs.md:133)

### Vision  `vision`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** An eight-pointed asterisk/star — eight straight rays radiating from a common
center at even 45° intervals (a "+" overlaid on an "×"). Eight-fold radial symmetry; the most
purely non-directional shape in the set. `canBeCenter`.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: *"manipulates the light around it."* With Eye and Bend it
forms the **concealment/illusion trio**; *separated* from Eye it instead aids **sight** (it is
the core of Qifrey's light-reducing glasses). **Not invertible.**

**Parameters.** None directional. Occupies the center with no sigil needed (signs.json effect:
*"Needs no sigil — it occupies the center"*).

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain:* its role **shifts by partner** — paired with Eye it makes *illusions /
  concealment*; alone it *aids vision* (Light-Reducing Spell). Canon suspects it **may actually
  be a sigil**. (signs.md:271)
- As a `canBeCenter` sign it can be the substance of its own seal (sight/light manipulation),
  same promotion mechanic as Billow/Repetition.

**Appears in.** Gathering Shadows · Makeover Mask Spell · Cloak Spell (signs.md:273)

### Cooling  `cool`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A short central vertical bar flanked by four small dots — two on each side,
top-left/bottom-left and top-right/bottom-right — like a thermometer bulb with frost specks, or
a minimal snowflake. Bilateral (and near four-fold) symmetry; no front.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: *"is chilled and cooled"* — lowers the temperature of the
substance/object. **Not invertible.**

**Parameters.** None directional; an intensity/quality modifier.

**Findings.**
- *Canon-uncertain (single-spell, vol.12 bonus):* introduced in the Vapor Bubble Spell. Its job
  there is best read as **condensing water out of the air** (chilling humid air past its dew
  point so the contraption can gather it), and/or simply making the drinking water cold.
  (signs.md:147)
- 2026-06-01 (Vapor Bubble): works hand-in-hand with **Gather/Collection** in water's cheap
  *collect* mode — chill → condense → draw the dew in. It is the "refrigeration" stage of an
  air-conditioning-plant style seal, not an output former.

**Appears in.** Vapor Bubble Spell (signs.md:149)

### Orb  `orb`  ·  non-directional  ·  operator: form

**Drawing.** A circle with a single vertical diameter line through it (a circle bisected
top-to-bottom), topped by a small ball/dot at the very top of the line — a sphere on a stand.
Bilaterally symmetric about the vertical; otherwise radial — no front.

**Acts as.** A `form` operator: *"gathers into a sphere held above the glyph"* — defines a
spherical containment space above the seal that the controlled material fills. **Not
invertible.** Note: it does **not** pull material in; material must be **added directly**.

**Parameters.** None directional (the sphere sits *above*, the out-of-plane default, not a
compass heading). Larger seal ⇒ larger sphere.

**Findings.**
- 2026-05-31: Orb **fills bottom-to-top under gravity**, like a bucket — so a **fluid** (Water
  Orb) pools cleanly and self-creates its own contents, while a **rigid** substance (rock, wood)
  would **stack and jam** and needs a compaction sign (e.g. Convergence) first. Earth Orb vs
  Water Orb is the canonical create-vs-manipulate contrast (earth needs a real source poured in;
  water makes its own).
- Canon Water Orb was filled by Qifrey **pouring water directly** into the sphere — Orb is a
  *container*, not an intake; pair it with Collection/Pull if you want it to fill itself.
  (signs.md:311)

**Appears in.** Water Orb (signs.md:313)

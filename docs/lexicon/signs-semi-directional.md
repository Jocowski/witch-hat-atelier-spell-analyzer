# Semi-Directional Signs

> **Semi-directional = invertible, but with no intrinsic direction of their own.** The sign has
> a "front," yet rotating it does *not* steer the spell; **size sets strength**, and **inverting
> it reverses the effect** (crush↔reassemble, enlarge↔shrink). Most are bilaterally symmetric
> like directional signs, but a few (rain, enlarge) are radially symmetric — those must be turned
> *inside-out* to invert rather than flipped end-for-end ([../signs.md](../signs.md):16). See
> [../CORE.md](../CORE.md) §3–4 for the operator/geometry model.

---

### Crush  `crush`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: transmute

**Drawing.** Two adjoining "∧∧" peaks — a low, jagged double-chevron zigzag drawn as one
continuous stroke (down-up-down-up). Bilateral about the vertical axis; no radial symmetry. The
peaks are the business-end: by default they point **outward** (away from the core) =
non-inverted = Wall Breaker.

**Acts as.** A `transmute` operator: pulverizes the substance into dust/sand on contact
("is pulverized into dust"). **Invertible** — inverted (peaks pointing **inward**) it instead
reassembles dust back into its original shape, *temporarily* (the Integration verb,
"is reassembled from dust"). Pairs best with **earth**, but the reassembly works on *any* powder,
not only stone/sand. Precondition for reach: little reach on its own — wants a **Column** to drive
it ([../signs.md](../signs.md):73).

**Parameters.** Size = how forcefully/how much it pulverizes. **Inversion** is the whole switch:
non-inverted = destroy, inverted = reintegrate. Rotation does not steer.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **Default facing = outward = Wall Breaker, NOT Integration.** Non-inverted Crush
  (peaks outward) destroys → dust; inverted Crush (peaks inward) = Integration, reassembles
  powder to its source shape *temporarily* (Tartah ID'd powdered herbs by original form). The
  grammar verbs are canon-correct — don't swap `verb`/`invertedVerb`. The historical bug was the
  app drawing Crush inward by default, so a fresh non-inverted Crush *looked* like Integration.
- 2026-06-01: **Integration works on ANY powder** (e.g. herbs), not just earth's stone/sand —
  "manipulate not create": it only rearranges existing particles, never conjures matter.
- 2026-06-01: In **Serpent's Bed of Sand** Crush is **non-inverted** (mill rock → sand, the state
  Billow can convert); in **Sand Cage** it is **inverted** (supply rigidity by reintegrating loose
  grains). Anime ep.7 notes Wall Breaker also "crushed water into mist" — behavior beyond earth is
  conjecture ([../signs.md](../signs.md):75).

**Appears in.** Wall Breaker Seal · Integration (inverted) · Sand Cage (inverted) · Serpent's Bed
of Sand · Smokesculpting Seal (inverted).

### Convergence  `convergence`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: power

**Drawing.** A single downward-pointing hollow triangle "▽" (three straight strokes). Bilateral
about the vertical axis. One point of the triangle is typically seen pointing **inward** toward
the core; it is drawn top-outward (the point/business-end at the **bottom** of the art).

**Acts as.** A `power` operator: focuses/concentrates the magic down to a single point
("is focused down to a single point"). On loose particles it also **compacts and hardens** them
into a rigid form (`hardensLoose`) — the rigidity trick on earth/sand. **Invertible** (no canon
inverted appearance documented).

**Parameters.** Size = how tightly it focuses / how much it compacts. Rotation does not steer
(the inward point is convention, not aim).

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-03: **Convergence does NOT make a spell spin.** Tilting Convergence vanes tangentially
  does not impart rotation — it only focuses/compacts to a point regardless of cant (Spell Checkers
  community, on a Rasen-Shuriken fan design). This *narrows* the general "tilt any sign → spin"
  rule ([../magic.md](../magic.md):51): that rule is about canting the **output/directional** signs;
  a `power` sign that converges to a point has no output channel to spin. For a **wind** spell that
  must spin, use the **Grasping Wind mechanism — angled/tangential `pull` (vortex/twist)** — or
  `sign_of_wind` (whirlwind keystone), not tilted Convergence. Put Convergence by the **core** to
  densify the substance instead. See [signs-directional.md](signs-directional.md) (Pull).
- 2026-06-02: **The "firmness trick" extends to gases, not just loose earth/sand.** In **Wind Wall**,
  Convergence ×6 (back half) compresses *moving air* into a dense, pressurized sheet — the reason the
  wind wall resists being pushed through. Canon ties the sign to this spell directly: its effect there
  "suggests it might make the magic manifest about or from a single point" ([../signs.md](../signs.md):107).
  So read Convergence-on-air = densify/pressurize (barrier), the gaseous analogue of pack-grains-rigid.
- 2026-06-02: **Likely the big downward triangle (▽) framing the Phantasmal Fireball.** Shape
  matches exactly, and the role fits: on the heatless-flame substance, Convergence *focuses +
  stiffens* the flame into a dense, persistent standing body so it holds as a contained orb
  rather than dissipating. (Community reconstruction — originally mis-catalogued as `unknown_06`,
  now reassigned here. Not canon-confirmed; awaiting the manga page.) See
  [signs-asymmetric.md](signs-asymmetric.md) ("unknown_06 retired").
- 2026-06-01: **Convergence can act as an intake/collection sign**, not only a focus/compactor.
  In **Qifrey's Water Dragon** it converges water held in nearby clouds to a point so the water
  sigils can draw it in. Don't assume Convergence-on-water = "compact into a dense body" (the
  Serpent's Bed of Sand rigidity reading) — check whether canon assigns it the gathering role.
- 2026-06-01: Convergence is drawn **top-outward** (point at the bottom of the art); an
  `inverted:true` export may just be the missing-`defaultFacing` artifact, not a deliberate
  inversion — check before reporting it.
- On earth/sand it provides the firmness (Serpent's Bed of Sand) and persistence-adjacent
  stiffness; in earth Orbs it is the compaction that stops rigid material jamming.

**Appears in.** Qifrey's Water Dragon · Serpent's Bed of Sand · Sylph Shoes Seal · Wind Wall ·
Wall-Anchored Floatglow Lamp · Repetition Seal · Light-Reducing Spell · Cookpot Lid.

### Radial  `radial`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: power

**Drawing.** A large arc/dome with a smaller concentric arc nested under it ("∩" over a little
"∩") — two nested upward-bowing curves, like a rainbow with a second band inside. Bilateral about
the vertical axis; reads as concentric.

**Acts as.** A `power` operator that **attenuates** — decreases the spell's intensity, converting
an intense form into a milder one ("is tempered to a gentler intensity"), e.g. fire → flameless
warmth. **Invertible** — the **Sand Bridge** seal uses it inverted (the only known inverted
Radial); the *effect* of that inversion is undocumented in canon — see the Findings theory below.

**Parameters.** Size sets the *amount* of tempering. With fire it sets the *kind* of output
(flameless warmth); the seal's overall size then sets the safe *amount*.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-03: **World Guide 2026 — community theory that "Radial may not be canon" (HOLD, not actioned).**
  When the official World Guide preview dropped (see [../world-guide-2026.md](../world-guide-2026.md)),
  the Spell Checkers ([[spell-checkers-community]]) argued that what we model as Radial — and especially
  *inverted Radial* — is actually the **Sign of Binding** (`bind`): upright Bind makes a substance
  **stick to itself** (so the Sand **Bridge** tiles cohere and don't fall apart), and **Snugstone**
  uses **inverted Bind** = "breaking the bind of fire," releasing it as **heat** (the flameless-warmth
  effect we currently credit to Radial). If true, Radial's two known uses collapse into Bind/inverted-Bind
  and Radial may not exist as its own sign. **Status: THEORY, deliberately not applied.** The preview
  page shown does *not* list "Radial," but absence ≠ proof; this is community analysis, not a guidebook
  statement. `radial` is left intact pending the full guide. (User decision, 2026-06-03: hold and record only.)
- 2026-06-02: **Inverted Radial — THEORY (not canon).** Sand Bridge is the only spell that uses
  Radial **inverted** (×2, east/west). Canon never documents an inverted Radial's effect
  ([..\signs.md](..\signs.md):247). *Deduction:* since inverting a semi-directional sign **reverses
  its effect** ([..\signs.md](..\signs.md):18) and normal Radial *attenuates* power, inverted Radial
  most likely **un-tempers / firms up** — it cancels the softening so the structure sets firm enough
  to bear weight (a power-*reducer* would be the wrong tool for a load-bearing bridge). This is a
  first-principles inference from the inversion rule + the bridge's need for rigidity, **not** a
  canon-stated effect — confidence low/theoretical. Engine blind spot: the engine is inversion-blind
  for Radial and still labels inverted Radial "weakened on purpose," so don't trust its power label here.
- 2026-06-01: **Snugstone** — Radial sets the *kind* of output (flameless warmth); seal *size* sets
  the safe *amount*. Safety is a calibration, not an inherent ceiling — amplification/power-dyes
  break it (Arcane Lens unbalanced it → melted the Ancients of Romonon).
- 2026-06-01: **Sand Cage caution** — a fresh app export once used `radial` at the N/S poles as a
  **placeholder** for a sign the app lacked; the canon "half circle signs" there are actually
  Unknown Sign 5, *not* Radial. Mis-reading injected a spurious "tempered/gentle" clause. Always
  check whether an odd Radial is standing in for a missing sign.

**Appears in.** Snugstone Spell · Sand Bridge (inverted, E/W). (Listed for Sand Cage only as a since-corrected placeholder.)

### Weave  `weave`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: transmute

**Drawing.** A near-complete circle (the "∪"/ribbon loop) whose two ends, instead of closing,
hook outward into short little tails at the bottom — like an O with a small open gap and two
flicked feet. Bilateral about the vertical axis. **One large sign that surrounds the central
sigil** (`surrounds`), not a ring of several.

**Acts as.** A `transmute` operator: turns solid objects into long, flexible ribbons on contact
("is woven into a long, flexible ribbon"). Invented by Richeh. **Invertible** (never seen inverted
in canon). Precondition: it must encircle the core; on a manipulate-substance (earth/crystal) it
needs a real solid present to transmute.

**Parameters.** Size = length/scale of the ribbon produced. It is a single wrapping glyph, so
"quantity" is not how it scales.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **Weave is a single large keystone wrapped around the core**, not a ring of many —
  "supposed to surround the central sigil" ([../signs.md](../signs.md):139). A single-sign
  "asymmetric/unstable" engine flag here is an artifact.
- 2026-06-01: **Boulder Stretch Rope** (earth) runs in earth's **manipulate** mode — needs a real
  boulder fed in ("a loom that needs raw stone"); the drama is overriding rock's rigidity. Same
  Weave sign as **Crystal Ribbon** (crystal) and **Light Tracer** (light, self-creating).
- 2026-06-02: **Crystal Ribbon** (archived) — crystal is **create+manipulate**, so unlike the earth
  Boulder Stretch Rope it's **self-supplying** (conjures its own crystal, then weaves it); the open
  question is only self-supply vs. reshaping an existing crystal (seal unshown). Confirms **size =
  ribbon length, not force** (override the engine's "power 2.35x"), and a lone Weave's
  "asymmetric/unstable" flag is an artifact.

**Appears in.** Boulder Stretch Rope · Crystal Ribbon · Light Tracer.

### Enlarge  `enlarge`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: transmute

**Drawing.** Four nested right-angle corner brackets at the four corners (each like an "⌐"/"L"
bracket), framing an empty square — a set of telescoping corner marks. **Radially symmetric**
(4-fold), so it inverts by being turned *inside-out*, not flipped end-for-end. The corners point
**outward** to grow; **inward** to shrink.

**Acts as.** A `transmute` operator that resizes the target ("grows far beyond its normal size").
**Invertible** — inverted it shrinks ("shrinks far below its normal size"). **Can sit at the
center** (`canBeCenter`) — it is the form itself, so it cannot be an empty enclosing circle. Needs
a **scope** partner: **Window** → it resizes the object the glyph is drawn on; **Diamond** → it
resizes *nearby* objects.

**Parameters.** Size = how much it grows/shrinks. **Corner direction = inversion** (out = grow,
in = shrink). The Window/Diamond partner sets *what* is affected, not Enlarge itself.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **Qifrey's Water Dragon** — Enlarge supplies the *scale* of the conjured creature;
  Convergence is intake, Columns are aim. Enlarge is the size knob, nothing more.
- A sign at center may or may not be needed for the standalone resize spell, but if used Enlarge
  must be placed in the center regardless ([../signs.md](../signs.md):231). It pairs by inversion:
  Floating Expansion (grow + Window) ↔ Spell of Reduction (shrink + Diamond).

**Appears in.** Floating Expansion · Spell of Reduction (inverted) · Qifrey's Water Dragon.

### Rain  `rain`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: form

**Drawing.** A dense radial sunburst — a central rounded square/cushion with many short spokes
("▦" core) radiating to a ring of little tick-marks all around (the falling drops). **Radially
symmetric**, so it inverts by turning *inside-out*. **One large sign that surrounds the core**
(`surrounds`); the central sigil sits in the empty middle.

**Acts as.** A `form` operator: the substance falls as rain across the surrounding area
("falls as rain across the surrounding area"). **Invertible** — turned inside-out it reverses
(reads as draw-in / drying rather than precipitation).

**Parameters.** Size = the radius/area covered by the rainfall. It is the whole form, so it scales
by size, not count.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **Rainbringer Seal** — one large Rain sign **IS** the entire form; don't ring
  multiples. Upright = rainfall; inverted ≈ draw-in/drying. The cleanest water sigil+sign pairing.
  Single-sign "asymmetric → unstable" is an engine artifact.
- May not be water-only: could plausibly produce firework-like falls with fire/light, or "lava
  rain" with fire+earth ([../signs.md](../signs.md):287) — untested conjecture.

**Appears in.** Rainbringer Seal.

### Puppet  `dancing_puppet`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: motion

**Drawing.** A radial "snowflake" of crossing strokes with small ring-eyelets along the spokes
("✺") — multiple arms radiating from center, each carrying little loops. **Radially symmetric**;
**surrounds** the core, which sits in the empty middle.

**Acts as.** A `motion` operator: makes the affected object dart and flit erratically through the
air ("darts and flits erratically"). Pairs best with **air/wind** (wind-puppet spells move
aerially). Notably **mind-directed** — like Sights Set, it lets a user steer the object with their
mind. **Invertible** is *uncertain* (signs.md hedges: "possibly… in which case it would be
semi-directional" — currently classed semi-directional, but inversion is unconfirmed).

**Parameters.** Size = strength/extent of the darting motion. The kind of movement depends on the
**sigil** (wind → aerial). Rotation does not steer (the user's mind does).

**Findings.**
- The motion's character is set by the substance: with a wind sigil it is purely aerial
  (Flying Puppet of Diversion, Cloak Spell). Sasaran flew his cloak with precision and no visible
  controls → strong evidence of mind-piloting; sealchair underside marks resemble Puppet variants
  ([../signs.md](../signs.md):295).
- **Uncertainty:** invertibility is unconfirmed in canon — flag the "semi-directional" label as
  provisional for this sign.

**Appears in.** Flying Puppet of Diversion · Cloak Spell.

### Strengthen  `strengthen`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

> **Official name (World Guide 2026): Sign of Empowerment** — JP "reinforcement vector" = strengthen/reinforce; note it may empower *effects*, not only physical durability ([../world-guide-2026.md](../world-guide-2026.md)).

**Drawing.** A small hollow triangle ("∧") sitting on a short horizontal baseline, with the
baseline extending into two little side-stubs ("⤧"-like) — a capped peak over a line. Bilateral
about the vertical axis.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: reinforces the target, making it tougher and more durable
("is reinforced, made tougher and more durable"). Marked **invertible** in data (semi-directional),
but **never seen inverted** in canon, so the reverse effect is unconfirmed.

**Parameters.** Size = how much durability it adds. Rotation does not steer.

**Findings.**
- Introduced in the volume 12 bonus; in the **Capture Pennant Spell** it toughens the banner of a
  Knight's capture pennant ([../signs.md](../signs.md):155). Function ("makes objects stronger")
  is presumed, not fully confirmed — flag as likely-but-unverified.

**Appears in.** Capture Pennant Spell.

### Entwine  `entwine`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

> **Official name (World Guide 2026): Sign of Entwining** — JP "coiling vector" = wrap/coil around something ([../world-guide-2026.md](../world-guide-2026.md)).

**Drawing.** A tall "I-beam" / dumbbell figure: a vertical central bar capped top and bottom by
short horizontal end-bars (serifs), like a girder ("⊟" stretched vertically). Bilateral about
both axes.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: makes the object it is drawn on (e.g. a ribbon) wrap or
**entwine** around nearby objects ("wraps and entwines around nearby objects"). Marked
**invertible** (semi-directional) but **never seen inverted**; mechanism unknown.

**Parameters.** Size = strength/extent of the wrapping. Rotation does not steer.

**Findings.**
- First seen in the **Capture Pennant Spell**, alongside Sights Set (aim) + Strengthen (durability)
  — together a Knight's pennant that targets, wraps, and holds. The exact mechanism is unknown
  ([../signs.md](../signs.md):171); flag as low-detail.

**Appears in.** Capture Pennant Spell.

### Aeriforms Defined  `aeriforms_defined`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A short vertical stroke flanked by two short diagonal strokes splaying down-and-out
from its top (a central bar with two angled "wings" — the same motif used as the **modifiers on
the wind sigil**). Bilateral about the vertical axis.

**Acts as.** A `special` air operator: shapes how the air takes form — "role not fully clear."
Confusingly it shares the *aeriforms* (air-**creating**) sigil's name but the *wind*-sigil
modifier's *design*, so its exact function is ambiguous. Known to serve as a **modifier to the
wind-underfoot sigil**. Marked **invertible** (semi-directional); never seen inverted.

**Parameters.** Presumably size = strength of the air-shaping effect. Function too unclear to map
its geometry knobs confidently.

**Findings.**
- Only confirmed use is as a modifier on the wind-underfoot sigil in the **Pegasus Carriage Spell**
  ([../signs.md](../signs.md):189). Name-vs-design conflict (aeriforms = create air; wind = move
  air) leaves its precise role **uncertain** — do not assert create-vs-move from this sign alone.

**Appears in.** Pegasus Carriage Spell.

### Glaives  `glaives`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** A trident/pitchfork: a vertical shaft rising to a small forked head (two short
claw-like prongs that curl back, with a tiny central tine) — claw-like protrusions atop a stem.
Bilateral about the vertical axis.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: determines **how deeply the magic embeds into an animal's
flesh** ("embeds the magic deep into the flesh of its target"). Marked **invertible**
(semi-directional); never seen inverted. May predate the Day of the Pact, and per Engendale's
comments may **not even be classified as a sign**.

**Parameters.** Size = embedding depth (how far into flesh the magic sinks). Rotation does not
steer.

**Findings.**
- **Body magic = forbidden territory.** Glaives act on living flesh; the only permitted body-magic
  is Memory Erasure ([../CORE.md](../CORE.md) §7). Seen only in Memory Erasure variants and
  Engendale's Slime Rendering Seal ([../signs.md](../signs.md):205) — flag any spell using it as
  forbidden-adjacent.
- Classification is itself uncertain (possibly not a true sign).

**Appears in.** Memory Erasure · Slime Rendering Seal.

### Bind  `bind`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

> **Official name (World Guide 2026): Sign of Binding** — JP "holding vector" = *fixes/anchors the
> effect in place* ([../world-guide-2026.md](../world-guide-2026.md)).

**Drawing.** A small hollow diamond/lozenge at top, sitting above a row of several short
down-hooks (a fringe of little prongs along a baseline) — a cap over a comb of teeth. Roughly
bilateral about the vertical axis.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: binds fast to the surface it touches ("binds fast to the
surface it touches"). Dominant theory = it adheres like **glue**; a lesser theory = it holds an
object together so it cannot break apart. The official JP reading ("holding vector — anchors the
effect in place") fits the "hold together / fix in place" reading. Marked **invertible** (semi-directional).

**Findings — World Guide 2026.**
- 2026-06-03: **Community theory: Bind may absorb Radial's role.** The Spell Checkers argue Bind
  (stick-to-itself) explains the Sand **Bridge** cohesion, and *inverted* Bind explains **Snugstone**
  (breaking fire's bind → heat) — i.e. what we model as Radial / inverted-Radial. **Held as theory, not
  applied** (user decision 2026-06-03); see the Radial entry's matching Finding and
  [../world-guide-2026.md](../world-guide-2026.md) Tier 3.

**Parameters.** Size = strength of adhesion/hold. Rotation does not steer.

**Findings.**
- Found in the **Wall Bend** spell; exact function unconfirmed — "glue to the touched surface" vs.
  "hold an object together" are both live theories ([../signs.md](../signs.md):303). Flag as
  theory-level.

**Appears in.** Wall Bend.

### Link  `link`  ·  semi-directional  ·  operator: special

**Drawing.** Two interlocking chain-links / a pair of overlapping angular loops, with short
diagonal flicks at the outer ends — reads as a chain motif ("⛓"). Bilateral about the vertical
axis.

**Acts as.** A `special` operator: **links its magic between the object it is drawn on and every
other object made from the same original object** ("links its magic to every object sharing the
same origin"). Marked **invertible** (semi-directional); never seen inverted.

**Parameters.** Size = strength/reach of the link. The *effect carried* across the link depends on
the partner form/transmute sign (e.g. with Weave → a ribbon stretched between the linked pieces).

**Findings.**
- Confirmed: links objects sharing a common origin — e.g. the **split-shard bangles** (rings carved
  from one crystal): Light sigil + Weave + Link → a ribbon of light spanning every ring made from
  that crystal ([../signs.md](../signs.md):327). Behavior with a partner sign **other than Weave**
  is unknown.
- Linked *identical* seals stack power generally ([../CORE.md](../CORE.md) §5) — but Link the *sign*
  specifically ties same-origin objects, distinct from the app's circle-to-circle `link` relation.

**Appears in.** Light Tracer (split-shard bangles).

# Substances — the Sigil Lexicon

> **What this is.** One entry per **sigil** — the *substance* a seal is made of (the operand its
> signs transform). Each entry says **how it is drawn**, then — most importantly — its **substance
> properties**: the **capability** (can it *create* its material, only *manipulate* existing
> material, or *collect* it from the environment?), its **physical state**, and what real-world
> **source** it needs if it cannot create. The capability question is the highest-leverage thing in
> the whole system: it decides whether a spell is self-sufficient or dead without feedstock — see
> [../CORE.md](../CORE.md) §2. Cite [../sigils.md](../sigils.md) by `file:line` for canon claims;
> flag uncertainty rather than inventing it.

## Fire

### Fire  `fire`  ·  fire

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: an upward triangle (flame) with a downward stem and two short
branch-ticks on the upper sides. Roughly radial/bilateral about the vertical.

**Substance.** Element **fire** — flame, heat, and light (grammar `fire`: substance "flame", raw
"burns, giving off heat and light"; qualities hot, luminous). Capability **create** *and*
manipulate: the base fire sigil works "to create and manipulate flames or heat depending on the
spell" (sigils.md:13). State **luminous/energetic**. No external source needed — self-sufficient.

**Behavior.** Manifests as flame/heat by default; the form sign decides the channel (column → a
lance of flame, orb → a floating ball, bolt → a volley). One of the four primary tetrad.

**Findings.** (newest first)
- 2026-06-01: **Radial on fire** tempers flame into gentle, flameless warmth — the *kind* of output;
  seal *size* sets the safe *amount* (Snugstone). Safety is a calibration, not an inherent ceiling.
- 2026-06-01: **Sign of Wind** on fire = a whirlwind keystone that twists the flame column into a
  spiral (Spiraling Flame); the engine can't measure the spin.

**Appears in.** Flame Shot Seal · Pyreball Seal · Snugstone Spell · Spiraling Flame · Rainflinger ·
Warmth-Retention Seal.

### Unburning Flames  `unburning_flame`  ·  fire

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: an open triangle outline with a short downward stem ending in a
small solid square, and three radiating spokes capped by small bars (a tri-armed motif). Three-sided.

**Substance.** Element **unburning_flame** — heatless flame (grammar: substance "heatless flame",
raw "burns as flame that gives off no heat"; quality luminous). Capability **uncertain**: it is
"somehow involved in the production of heatless flames as seen in the phantasmal fireball … It is
possible that this sigil alone is not enough to produce flames without heat and additional signs are
needed" (sigils.md:21). State **luminous/gaseous** (cold flame). Likely create-adjacent like fire,
but **flag uncertainty**.

**Behavior.** Produces flame that gives off light without heat. Mechanics not fully documented —
treat any deduction as tentative.

**Findings.**
- The fire-family `*_flame` variant: reason as fire minus heat. May require partner signs to fire at
  all (sigils.md:21).

**Appears in.** Phantasmal Fireball.

### Light  `light`  ·  fire

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a square with an inscribed diamond and a short cross-stub poking
past each edge (a windowed/cruciform square). Radial, four-fold.

**Substance.** Element **light** — a fire variant that "functions to produce magic that manifests in
the form of light" (sigils.md:29; grammar: substance "light", raw "shines outward", luminous).
Capability **create** (a fire variant; self-sufficient). State **luminous**.

**Behavior.** Same form signs as fire give light channels: column → a beam (Light Beam), bird → a
flying light projection (Bird of Light Beacon), weave → a light ribbon (Light Tracer).

**Findings.**
- **Levitation on light lifts vertically without steering** — only air/wind levitation aims;
  treat any lateral aim/spin the engine reports on light as an artifact.

**Appears in.** Bird of Light Beacon · Floatglow Lamp Seal · Light Beam · Light Tracer ·
Flowers Of Light · Beast Repellent.

## Water

### Water  `water`  ·  water

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a central hooked stroke flanked by a bottom-joined loop on the
left and a top-joined loop on the right (a droplet/wave motif). Roughly bilateral.

**Substance.** Element **water** (grammar: substance "water", raw "pours and flows outward",
quality fluid). Capability is the whole point: it works "to manipulate, collect, and create water"
— but **collecting is cheaper than creating** ("many water spells meant to be used over long periods
collect water rather than create it … creating water is more energetically costly than collecting
it", sigils.md:41). So water has **all three** modes; pick the cheapest the spell allows. State
**fluid**. One of the primary tetrad.

**Behavior.** Create-mode = self-sufficient ammo/jets (Watershot, Water Bolt). Collect-mode = harvest
ambient humidity/clouds for long-running or large spells (Vapor Bubble, Rainbringer, Qifrey's Water
Dragon). The form sign sets the channel; fluidity makes Orb pool cleanly.

**Findings.** (newest first)
- 2026-06-01: **Qifrey's Water Dragon** runs in **collect** mode — "uses water from nearby clouds
  (and likely bodies of water)" — and there **Convergence is the intake** (gathers cloud-water to a
  point), not the rigidity trick. A decorative-creature sigil over water = a controllable water
  creature; scale from Enlarge, aim from balanced Columns.
- 2026-06-01: **Vapor Bubble** is a dew-still: water core as a *collection basin*, air signs chill
  ambient humidity past dew point — the cheap collect mode (sigils.md:41).
- 2026-06-01: **Water Pen** uses **create** mode + fluidity: conjured in the palm with no reservoir,
  Dispersion + balanced Columns meter it into one coherent glob (a pen/brush, not a jet).
- 2026-06-01: **Watershot** — size sets aim: equal columns → straight-up jet; one oversized column
  leans the jet its way (the misfire that soaked Agott).
- 2026-05-31: **Inverting a water sigil has no known effect** — treat sigil-core inversion as
  cosmetic unless the element defines an `invertedVerb`. Water Orb makes its own water (vs Earth Orb).

**Appears in.** Watershot Seal · Water Bolt · Rainbringer · Rising Wave · Vapor Bubble · Water Horse ·
Qifrey's Water Dragon · Water Pen · Water Orb · Purify.

## Earth

### Earth  `earth`  ·  earth

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a horizontal bar with a stem over an inverted triangle, with a dot
to each side (·▽·). Bilateral about the vertical.

**Substance.** Element **earth** — wood, stone, sand, and soil (grammar: substance "earth, stone,
and sand", raw "rises up as rock and sand"; solid, heavy). Capability **manipulate only** — this is
the canonical anchor for the whole "needs a source" idea: it "allows for the manipulation of
numerous solid substances … However, it does not allow for the creation of these solid materials, as
seen … in the earth lift spell" (sigils.md:53). State spans **granular** (sand, loose soil) and
**rigid** (rock, wood). **Always state the real-world source** it must be fed; with none present it
does nothing. Also called the sigil of might; primary tetrad.

**Behavior.** A machine that needs feedstock. Convergence compacts loose grains into a rigid form
(the rigidity trick); inverted Crush reintegrates dust temporarily (a cage). Orb fills bottom-to-top
like a fluid, so sand pools but rigid rock jams without a compaction sign first.

**Findings.** (newest first)
- 2026-06-01: **Sand Cage** — inverted Crush supplies *temporary* rigidity (reintegrating loose
  grains); runs in manipulate mode — needs real sand present, and the cage is temporary.
- 2026-06-01: **Serpent's Bed of Sand** — Crush is *non-inverted* here (mill rock → sand, the state
  Billow can convert); needs real ground to mill.
- 2026-06-01: **Integration** = inverted Crush; works on *any* powder (Tartah's herbs), not just
  stone — "manipulate not create" means it only rearranges existing particles, never conjures matter.
  Default (non-inverted) Crush = Wall Breaker, peaks **outward**, destroys → dust.
- 2026-06-01: **Boulder Stretch Rope** — a single large Weave wrapped around the core transmutes a
  *real* rigid boulder into a flexible ribbon; cannot conjure stone ("a loom that needs raw stone").
- 2026-05-31: **Earth Orb vs Water Orb** is the model case for capability — earth needs a real
  source (a reservoir), water makes its own.

**Appears in.** Wall Breaker Seal · Integration · Boulder Stretch Rope · Sand Bridge · Sand Cage ·
Serpent's Bed of Sand · Wall Bend.

## Air

### Wind  `wind`  ·  air

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a central airflow spiral flanked by horizontal gust-dashes on each
side. Bilateral.

**Substance.** Element **air** (grammar: substance "air", raw "stirs into moving wind"; mobile,
invisible). Capability **manipulate / move, but NOT create**: it "allows for the movement and
manipulation, but not the creation, of air" (sigils.md:65). State **gaseous**. Needs **ambient air**
to move (almost always present, but it cannot conjure air in a vacuum — pair with Aeriforms if you
must create it). Also the sigil of levitation; primary tetrad.

**Behavior.** Moves existing air → wind, gusts, lift. **Wind/air levitation is the only directional
levitation** (it can steer); levitation on other elements lifts straight up.

**Findings.** (newest first)
- 2026-06-01: With Dancing Puppet, wind sends objects darting through open air (grammar synergy).
- **Levitation is directional only on air/wind** — override spurious lateral aim/spin the engine
  reports for water/fire/light lift.

**Appears in.** Skysoaring Seal · Wind Wall · Grasping Wind · Cloak Spell · Floating Drops ·
Flying Puppet of Diversion.

### Wind Underfoot  `wind_underfoot`  ·  air

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: an interlocking double spiral (an S-within-S / ∞ knot). Roughly
point-symmetric.

**Substance.** Element **air** (variant of wind). Capability/role **unclear**: "not yet clear, but
it is known that it helps to somehow support solid objects when they are suspended within air,
similar to an air platform" (sigils.md:79). State **gaseous** (an air platform). **Flag
uncertainty** — specifics unknown.

**Behavior.** Provides footing/support for objects held aloft (a standing air platform), rather than
moving air for propulsion. Mechanism undocumented.

**Findings.**
- Reason as the *support/platform* facet of air; pairs with other air sigils for lift + footing.

**Appears in.** Pegasus Carriage Spell · Sylph Shoes Seal.

### Aeriforms  `aeriforms`  ·  air

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: the central airflow spiral of Wind, but with gusts/rays radiating
on all sides (sustaining air) instead of two side-dashes. Radial.

**Substance.** Element **air** (variant). Capability is the **complement of Wind**: it "allows for
the creation and manipulation (but does not move) air" (sigils.md:73). So it **creates** air but
cannot move it — for both you need Wind too (CORE.md §2a). State **gaseous**.

**Behavior.** Generates/sustains air at the seal; combine with Wind to also propel it.

**Findings.**
- The canonical create-but-not-move counterpart to Wind's move-but-not-create — the textbook case
  for combining two sigils to cover both capabilities.

**Appears in.** (no catalogued canon spell yet — described as "Make air".)

### Whorling Winds  `whorling_wind`  ·  air

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a wind motif built around a central whorl, drawn distinctly
three-sided (unlike the other air sigils).

**Substance.** Element **air** (variant). Capability **manipulate via rotation**: "helps to
manipulate air through rotation" (sigils.md:87). State **gaseous**. **Flag uncertainty**: the
three-sided design "could indicate a connection to the fire sigil, meaning it might be heating the
air as we do with hot air balloons" (sigils.md:87) — possible heat coupling, unconfirmed.

**Behavior.** Spins/rotates air; speculated to warm it (lift via heated air).

**Findings.**
- Possible fire crossover (three-sided form) — treat the heating idea as a documented hypothesis, not
  fact.

**Appears in.** Pegasus Carriage Spell.

## Time

### Repetition  `repetition_sigil`  ·  time  (sign-as-sigil)

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: an outer ring enclosing a central spiral coil. Radial. (This is
the Repetition *sign* serving as a core; it has artwork.)

**Substance.** Element **time** (grammar: substance "the flow of time", raw "reverts what it touches
to an earlier state"). Capability acts on **time/state**, not matter — it manipulates the *history*
of whatever it touches. State **immaterial**. Needs a target object to lock (no source of matter
required). Retconned from sign to sigil (like the decorative sigils).

**Behavior.** "Continuously resetting time for the objects it affects to the state they had when the
spell first affected them … like a spring" — makes soft things elastic, prevents rot/deformation
(sigils.md:99). Inverted operator accelerates wear.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: In **Serpent's Bed of Sand**, the outer Repetition wrap is what supplies *persistence*
  (signs.md:131) — the soft-sand bed is held by continuous reset, not by the earth signs.
- A `canBeCenter` sign: a Repetition sign at the origin is promoted to the circle's core (substance).

**Appears in.** Repetition Seal · Serpent's Bed of Sand · Capture Pennant Spell · Cookpot Lid.

### Stop  `stop`  ·  time

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a large outer circle (clockface) with a small solid hub at center
and an inner ring of radial spoke/segment marks dividing the dial. Radial.

**Substance.** Element **time** (shares the time grammar; raw "reverts/halts"). Capability **halt**:
"outright halts time for the objects it affects" (sigils.md:107). State **immaterial**. Needs a
target; pairs with another sigil to halt **one aspect** of it.

**Behavior.** Freezes change. "By pairing it with other sigils … stop time for specific aspects of
an object, such as changes in heat if paired with a fire sigil" (sigils.md:107) — i.e. selectively
halt one property rather than the whole object.

**Findings.**
- Where Repetition *reverts* continuously, Stop *halts* — pairing with a second sigil scopes the halt
  to that sigil's aspect (fire → heat held, hence Warmth-Retention).
- 2026-06-02: **Warmth-Retention Seal** (archived) confirms the scoping rule and a structural point: a
  Stop+aspect-sigil state-lock is correctly **sign-free** — it suppresses a change rather than
  projecting/aiming a substance, so the engine's "no signs → raw discharge" warning is a false
  positive for this spell class. Catalog caveat: the matcher only records a Match when `signCount>0`
  (analyze.js:117), so sign-free spells never self-match (entry kept for provenance only). Opposite
  mechanism to Snugstone: Stop+Fire *retains* heat (a thermos); Snugstone *generates* it (a heater).

**Appears in.** Time Stop · Petrification · Warmth-Retention Seal.

## Misc

### Crystal  `crystal`  ·  misc

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a woven diamond lattice of crisscrossing diagonals (a faceted-gem
weave). Radial.

**Substance.** Element **crystal** (grammar: substance "crystal", raw "forms into hard crystal";
solid, rigid). Capability **create and manipulate**: "allows for the creation and manipulation of
crystalline objects" (sigils.md:197). State **rigid**. Self-sufficient (no source needed).

**Behavior.** Conjures/shapes hard crystal; weave makes a crystal ribbon, bolt/column make shards.
Rigid state means form signs that assume flow (Orb) want a compaction or shaping partner.

**Findings.**
- A rare sigil — canonically only Richeh uses it. Self-creating, so unlike earth it needs no source,
  but its rigidity behaves like rock for form-fit purposes.

**Appears in.** Crystal Shard · Crystal Ribbon.

### Smoke  `smoke`  ·  misc

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: two overlapping rounded cloud-puffs with a small inward spiral
curl at the lower left (a curling plume of smoke). Roughly bilateral, slightly asymmetric.

**Substance.** Element **smoke** (grammar: substance "smoke", raw "billows out as smoke"; airy).
Capability **create/generate**; **manipulation unconfirmed**: "works to create and generate smoke.
It has not been seen whether or not the sigil is also capable of manipulating smoke" (sigils.md:205).
State **gaseous**. Self-sufficient for generation; **flag** the open question on manipulation.

**Behavior.** Produces billowing smoke; pair with billowing/dispersion to spread it.

**Findings.**
- Generation is confirmed; reshaping/steering is not (sigils.md:205) — don't assume manipulate-mode.

**Appears in.** Smoke Cloud.

### Guidance  `guidance`  ·  misc

**Drawing.** **Text glyph "G"** — no reference artwork; rendered as the letter G in the app.

**Substance.** Element **guidance** (grammar: substance "a gathering pull", raw "draws matching
objects toward the glyph"). Capability **collect/attract**: "functions to attract objects to its
location that match parameters set by the other signs and sigils within a spell" (sigils.md:211).
State **immaterial** (an attraction force). Needs **matching objects present** in the environment to
pull — a pump that needs a reservoir.

**Behavior.** Draws in objects matching the spell's parameters (e.g. attracts all nearby Pouches of
Calling). The other signs/sigils set what counts as a match.

**Findings.**
- Pure attraction; useless with nothing matching nearby. Parameterized by its companion symbols.

**Appears in.** Pouch of Calling (paired with Calling).

### Calling  `calling`  ·  misc

**Drawing.** **Text glyph "C"** — no reference artwork; rendered as the letter C in the app.

**Substance.** Element **calling** (grammar: substance "a recorded sound", raw "echoes back a
recorded phrase"). Capability **collect-then-replay** (records, then echoes): "echoes back a recorded
phrase" (sigils.md:217). State **immaterial** (stored sound). Needs a phrase to have been recorded.

**Behavior.** Plays back a recorded phrase. Only seen in the Pouch of Calling.

**Findings.**
- Functions as a stored-sound token; pairs naturally with Guidance (which can attract Pouches of
  Calling to one another).

**Appears in.** Pouch of Calling.

## Decorative

> Decorative sigils depict living things. With them you can "either make a spell affect the living
> thing the sigil corresponds to, create magic in the shape of that living thing, or manifest magic
> inside things the same shape" (sigils.md:113). **They are mostly aesthetic** — "commonly said
> these sigils have no practical utility … now mostly used as decoration or by hobbyists"
> (sigils.md:113) — i.e. they shape *another* substance into a creature; they are **not a substance
> source themselves** (a creature sigil is not a fire/water source — CORE.md §7). **Horse is the
> notable exception with real utility.** Element for all is `decorative` (grammar: substance "the
> spell's magic", raw "takes the shape of the living thing it depicts"). State **immaterial** (a
> shape imposed on the paired element). Like Repetition, they were retconned from signs to sigils.

### Bird A  `bird_a`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a stylized bird with a peaked head/crest at top and two outspread
swept wings, fine feather-ticks along the wing edges. Bilateral. (Design changed in ch. 28; the front
portion may be a separate sigil — sigils.md:119.)

**Substance.** `decorative` — shapes the paired element into "a bird-like projection made from the
seal's magic which will fly around for a while" (sigils.md:119). Aesthetic; ideally the elemental
sigil sits in its center (sigils.md:119).

**Findings.** Seen only once (Bird of Light Beacon) — front section's status uncertain.

**Appears in.** Bird of Light Beacon.

### Bird B  `bird_b`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a bird with an upright body/neck and two swept wings, but a
rounder, more **duck-like** silhouette than Bird A. Bilateral.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of a bird, similar to bird A …
noticeably more duck-like" (sigils.md:127). Aesthetic shape-giver.

**Appears in.** (no catalogued canon spell.)

### Dragon  `dragon`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a long sinuous serpentine body drawn horizontally — coiled head at
left, spined back, sweeping tail/fins at right (a Dragonpass Isles Wyrm). Bilateral along its length,
no symmetry.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of a dragon" (sigils.md:133).
Aesthetic shape-giver; the depicted species is uncertain.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **The only canon spell using Dragon** is Qifrey's Water Dragon (sigils.md:135) — paired
  with Water sigils (the substance), Enlarge (scale), Convergence (cloud-water intake), Columns (aim).
  A decorative-creature sigil over water = a controllable water creature; the dragon settles into a
  water lily when spent (via the Water Flower sigil).

**Appears in.** Qifrey's Water Dragon.

### Flower  `flower`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a five-petalled bloom — a central pentagon with petal-arches and a
stamen/leaf flourish at each outer corner. Roughly five-fold radial.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of numerous flowers"
(sigils.md:141). Aesthetic shape-giver (e.g. blossoms of light).

**Appears in.** Flowers Of Light.

### Water Flower  `flower_water`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: the same pentagon-flower frame as Flower, but the petal-arches are
replaced by small water **droplets** hung around the rim. Five-fold.

**Substance.** `decorative` (color tinted to water). A modified Flower whose "petal-arches are
swapped for water droplets — shapes gathered water into a lily as it settles" (sigils.json). An
**end-state shaper**: it settles spent/gathered water into a water-lily form.

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: In **Qifrey's Water Dragon** it is the end-state shaper — when the dragon dissipates it
  settles the spent water into a water lily.

**Appears in.** Qifrey's Water Dragon.

### Horse  `horse`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a side-profile horse reduced to a few strokes — raised head/neck at
left, a level back line, blocky legs, hoof-ticks at the base. Bilateral along its length.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of a horse" (sigils.md:149).
**The utility exception:** with the Water Horse spell "it is possible to pull loads via the water
horse, suggesting that this decorative sigil (and perhaps others) has the potential for practical
usage" (sigils.md:149). So it is a shape-giver that also confers real work (traction).

**Findings.**
- 2026-06-01: **Water Horse** is a two-sigil spell — Water (the substance) + Horse (the shape-giver
  with utility). The matcher keys only on core + signs, so secondary sigils like Horse live in catalog
  `notes`. NOT `animal_signs` (a no-effect decorative *sign*).

**Appears in.** Water Horse.

### Owlcat  `owlcat`  ·  decorative

**Drawing.** Traced filled glyph: a winged feline-owl with a round head, two ear/horn tufts, and
spread wings/limbs — the **full** creature (head + body). Bilateral.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "likely for forming a full Owlcat … as opposed to just the head"
(sigils.md:157); **unconfirmed**, more info needed. Aesthetic shape-giver.

**Findings.** Whole-creature variant of Owlcat Head — confirms decorative sigils can be split into
body-part sub-sigils (sigils.md:163).

**Appears in.** (no catalogued canon spell.)

### Owlcat Head  `owlcat_head`  ·  decorative

top and short whisker/feather spokes flanking a central circle. Radial-ish.

**Substance.** `decorative` — "causes magic to manifest in the shape of the head of an Owlcat with
winter plumage, which is almost spherical" (sigils.md:163). Aesthetic. Identical to Owlcat minus the
body section — proof that **decorative sigils can be split into part-shapes** (sigils.md:163).

**Appears in.** (no catalogued canon spell.)

## Special (sign-as-sigil — hidden in palette)

> These are **signs serving as the core** (they need no separate elemental sigil). They live in
> `sigils.json` as `*_sigil`, family `special`, and are hidden from the palette. Their substance is
> defined by the sign itself.

### Vision (as sigil)  `vision_sigil`  ·  special

**Drawing.** An 8-pointed asterisk — four crossed strokes (vertical, horizontal, two diagonals)
through the origin. Radial. (Stroke glyph, not a traced fill.)

**Substance.** Element **light** — the Vision sign at center, which "manipulates the light around it"
(grammar `vision`). Capability **manipulate light** (an existing-light operator, not a creator of new
matter). State **luminous/immaterial**. Basis of concealment spells.

**Behavior.** On its own it only stirs light; with **Eye** (absorbs light into shadow) and **Bend**
(bends light/shadow around a target) it melds an object and what it covers into the shadows — true
concealment (grammar interaction `shadow-meld`).

**Findings.**
- A `canBeCenter` sign promoted to core; alone it is incomplete — pair with Eye + Bend for
  concealment (grammar note `vision-incomplete`).

**Appears in.** Gathering Shadows · Cloak Spell.

### Billowing (as sigil)  `billowing_sigil`  ·  special

**Drawing.** A 4-petal flower/clover — four lobed petals meeting at the origin. Radial. (Stroke
glyph.)

**Substance.** Element **transmutation** (grammar: substance "the affected matter", raw "is
transmuted"). The Billowing sign at center "converts material into a cloud" (Billowing Collection).
Capability **transmute** existing material into cloud — a **transmuter**, not a creator: it needs
material to fluff. State of output **gaseous/fluffy**.

**Behavior.** Turns supplied material into a soft cloud. **Precondition:** a `support` sign
(Collection / Gather) or an ambient supply — without it, "has nothing to turn into cloud" (grammar
`billowing-no-collection`).
